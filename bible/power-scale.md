# Power Scale

Canon HI3 power levels are inconsistent. These are this story's rulings.

## Combat Rating

Strength is measured against Valkyries, not with numbers. Every rank splits
into **Low**, **Mid** and **High**, and the gap inside one rank is real: a
High B beats a Low B almost every time.

The ladder, from bottom to top:

1. **Civilian:** an ordinary person.
2. **Peak Human:** Olympic athlete or special forces. The ceiling for a body
   Honkai energy can't touch.
3. **D** (Low, Mid, High)
4. **C** (Low, Mid, High)
5. **B** (Low, Mid, High)
6. **A** (Low, Mid, High)
7. **S** (Low, Mid, High)
8. **Herrscher:** a separate class, not a higher Valkyrie rank. Ranges from
   a newly awakened, incomplete Herrscher to a complete one.

A few canon figures (Kevin, Otto, Fu Hua) sit outside the ladder entirely.

### Canon S-ranks

Canon lists very few S-rank Valkyries: Cecilia Schariac (dead in the Second
Eruption, 2000), Reanna Brigantia (dead), and, later in the story, Bianka
"Durandal" Ataegina, Rita Rossweisse and Li Sushang. Durandal and Rita are
**not** S-rank in 2009; they're too young.

**Theresa Apocalypse is S-rank.** In 2009 she is the one active S-rank the
story needs to account for.

## Valkyrie ranks

Canon: Valkyries are women with high Honkai resistance and natural or
artificial stigmata. Only B-rank and above fight on the front lines. D-rank
Valkyries do support and logistics far from the real fighting. Valkyries
with poor Honkai adaptability die young, and any Valkyrie risks the
**Honkai cascade**, turning into a mindless zombie.

D and C ranks are logistics: medics, comms, drone operators, transport
pilots, battlesuit technicians. They are still trained Valkyries with
stigmata and can fight when they must.

| Rank | Role | Power | Environmental damage in a full fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Logistics, far from the fighting | Survives being hit by a car; can kill a lone Honkai zombie with a weapon | Breaks doors, cracks concrete |
| C | Logistics / combat support | Flips a car, fires heavy weapons by hand; dispatches Honkai zombies easily | Punches through brick walls, wrecks a room |
| B | Frontline | Fast enough to dodge small-arms fire; handles Archangel types and Chariots; fights Templars as a squad | Levels a house, craters a road |
| A | Elite, ace of a branch | Handles a Templar alone; a lineup of A-ranks takes on an Emperor-class | Destroys a building or a city block |
| S | Extremely rare: one or two at a time at most, some years none | Can handle an Emperor-class; Judgment-class needs a team with a few S-ranks and Divine Key-level weapons | Devastates a district; craters hundreds of meters wide |
| Herrscher | — | Wields an authority over reality | City-level and up; the strongest are continental or worse |

Eros starts as **Civilian**: a desk worker below Peak Human.

## Men, Knights and Paladins

- Honkai adaptability is uncommon in men, but in rare cases it happens.
  Those men become **Schicksal Knights** (Chevaliers), like Siegfried
  Kaslana. This is the canon path open to Eros once his Resistance rises.
- **Paladins** ("the Overseer's chosen") are the Overseer's personal guard.
  Cecilia was one. Theresa became one while still B-rank, the lowest-ranked
  Paladin ever.

## Honkai Resistance and Honkai Adaptability

Canon uses both words loosely (the HI3 wiki glossary calls Herrschers
"humans with high Honkai resistance" and Knights men with "any form of
Honkai adaptability"). This story splits them into two different things.

### Honkai Resistance

The body **withstands** Honkai energy. It's a wall, and it's what most
Valkyries have. Schicksal's cuff test measures it. Women have it far more
often than men; most men stay at F for life.

Resistance splits into **Low, Mid and High** like Combat Rating, and it is
his rank ceiling exactly: Mid C Resistance means a Mid C ceiling.

| Grade | What it allows |
|---|---|
| F | Normal human; ceiling Peak Human. Honkai energy only harms him. An artificial stigma transplant kills him. |
| E | Survives mild exposure. Survives an artificial stigma. |
| D | Hours in Eruption-level air. Basic battlesuits. |
| C | Standard battlesuits. |
| B | Frontline gear. |
| A | Elite gear. The top of what resistance can reach. |

- **Without a stigma:** resistance alone is survival and a ceiling, not
  power. Training and exposure can take him past Peak Human, up to Low D at
  most. Every hard push risks the energy pooling in him with nowhere to go,
  and that ends in a cascade (story ruling; not stated on the canon pages).
- **The cost of resistance:** the body is always fighting the energy. Under
  an artificial stigma and a battlesuit reactor, that fight is where the
  shortened life, the slow decay and the cascade risk come from.
- **Himeko is the model:** resistance, no adaptability, an artificial
  stigma, and she trained her way to the top.
- **Knights** (male Valkyries) are men with good resistance.

### Artificial stigmata and Resistance

Canon (HI3 wiki, Stigmata/Story): an artificial stigma "does allow for a
higher Honkai energy adaptability," but it shortens the candidate's life
significantly and the cascade risk rises over time. In this story's terms,
it raises Resistance, and the drawbacks are the price.

Each stigma has a grade: a **standard Schicksal stigma is C**, a
**top-quality one is B**.

| His Resistance | What the stigma does | Drawbacks |
|---|---|---|
| F | The transplant kills him. | |
| Below the stigma's grade | Lifts him to the Low of the stigma's grade | They show, and the bigger the gap, the worse. One grade: Himeko's shortened life and slow decay. Two grades: visible symptoms, faster decay, and regular maintenance from someone who knows Schicksal stigmata. |
| At or above the stigma's grade | Raises him to the High (peak) of his own grade | Mild and slow. His life is still shorter, as canon says it always is, but his body barely fights it. |

Examples for Eros: the Blue gift (Low D) plus a standard stigma lifts him
to Low C with Himeko-level drawbacks. A Purple gift (Low B) plus the same
stigma raises him to High B with mild ones.

### What each one gives

| | Resistance gift | Artificial stigma | Natural stigma |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resistance | Raises it cleanly | Raises it, at a cost (above) | Comes with Adaptability |
| Power | None. Survival and the ceiling only. | Raw strength, reflexes and healing, and the link to a battlesuit. The suit decides the abilities (Moegirl, 女武神装甲). | A unique ability or bloodline power that grows |
| Cost | None | Shorter life, decay, cascade risk | Draws the Honkai's attention |

The gift is the clean way up and the stigma is the dirty one that also
gives power. They stack: the higher his gift Resistance, the smaller the
stigma's drawbacks.

### Honkai Adaptability

The body **uses** Honkai energy instead of blocking it. It's the rarer,
upgraded state, found mostly in natural-stigma awakeners and in bloodlines
like the Schariacs and Kaslanas. It shows as "None" for almost everyone;
once it wakes it's graded C to S, and it raises the rank ceiling past
whatever his resistance allows.

**What it has over resistance:**

1. **It draws power from the Honkai.** In a high-HW zone a resistance
   Valkyrie weakens as her body works harder; an adaptable one gets
   stronger.
2. **It wakes a natural stigma,** which doesn't carry an artificial one's
   shortened life.
3. **It grows** with exposure and fighting. Resistance doesn't grow by
   itself.
4. **Lower cascade risk,** never zero, because the body isn't at war with
   itself.
5. **Faster healing** in tainted air.
6. **It's needed for S rank and for Divine Keys.**

**The danger:** extreme adaptability is what makes a Herrscher vessel. The
higher it climbs, the more the Honkai notices him.

**In men:** almost unheard of.

| Grade | Rank ceiling |
|---|---|
| None | Set by resistance |
| C | High C |
| B | High B |
| A | High A |
| S | High S; can touch a Divine Key without dying |
| Beyond S | The Herrscher threshold |

### What can change them

Only two things, never a settlement "raise":

- **Gifts.** A gift is a trait. Honkai Resistance is one (Blue Low D,
  Purple Low B, Gold Low A), and a Red gift can wake Honkai Adaptability
  (see [gifts-and-rewards.md](gifts-and-rewards.md)).
- **Body modifications** taken out of a run: an artificial stigma (see
  above), a World Serpent chimera procedure, a Previous Era MANTIS fusion,
  or a beast core fused into him (see
  [gifts-and-rewards.md](gifts-and-rewards.md), "Body modification").
  Besides the Red gift, a MANTIS fusion or a core is the only way a man
  ever gets Adaptability, and the most likely to kill him.

## How Eros gets stronger

There is no stat transfer from the runs. His real body has to earn its rank.

- **Resistance and Adaptability raise the ceiling.** Only gifts and body
  modifications change them.
- **Real training and real fights fill the room under the ceiling.** This
  is the grind, and it is slow.
- **Physical gifts** speed that growth or add abilities, but can't push him
  past his ceiling.
- **Experience packages** give him technique. His body has to catch up
  through training before he can use it fully.
- **Items and gear** (battlesuits, weapons) add power, within what his
  Resistance or Adaptability allows. A stigma is not gear (see below).

## Gear

- **Battlesuits:** combat suits that amplify the wearer. Each needs a
  minimum Resistance grade, and a stigma to link to (the suit feeds it
  through its reactor).
- **Stigmata are part of the body, not equipment.** Swapping stigmata in and
  out is a game mechanic, not lore. Canon has three types:
  - **True (Herrscher) stigmata:** tied to a Herrscher Core; every Previous
    Era Herrscher had one. Only these connect to a core of their type.
  - **Natural stigmata:** designed by Dr. MEI and Mobius in the Previous Era
    and written into human DNA. Everyone carries one dormant; it wakes only
    after enough Honkai exposure, so awakened ones are very rare. Bloodlines
    like the Kaslanas and Schariacs carry strong ones.
  - **Artificial stigmata:** Schicksal's copies, modeled on natural ones and
    the weakest type. Given by transplant surgery to girls whose
    compatibility is too low (Himeko, 2006). Side effects: a shorter
    lifespan, slow bodily decay, and the cascade risk stays.
  - **Battlesuits** feed an artificial stigma through the suit's reactor to
    boost the wearer's body (Moegirl, 女武神装甲). A better suit means more
    power; the stigma itself isn't upgraded. Canon never shows one being
    replaced, so in this story a Valkyrie keeps the stigma she got.
- **How Eros can get a stigma:**
  - **Artificial:** only as a body modification from a run where he had
    one. It arrives already in his body and follows the Resistance rules
    above (it kills him at F). It shows on any Schicksal scan as a
    Schicksal stigma in a man with no surgery record.
  - **Natural:** his own, dormant in his DNA like everyone's. It wakes only
    with Adaptability. It can't be taken out of a run, because a run body's
    stigma belongs to that body's DNA. Which stigma he carries is still open.
- **Divine Keys:** pre-era weapons, the strongest gear that exists. They
  kill anyone below S-grade Adaptability who tries to use one.
- **MANTIS** (Massively Augmented Neo-Tech Integrated Soldier): a Previous
  Era human whose genes were fused with a Honkai Beast's, taking its
  features and abilities (Kevin, Elysia). In the current era, World Serpent
  has a gentler chimera process with "much lower" side effects (the Gray
  Serpent, Rozaliya and Liliya). Source: HI3 wiki, MANTIS.

## Measuring the Honkai (HW)

Schicksal measures a Honkai by its **instantaneous power in HW**. The
threshold is canon series lore (Moegirl, 律者, citing the Honkai Gakuen 2
library): "每一次瞬时功率达到1000HW的崩坏就会诞生一名律者", meaning every Honkai
whose instantaneous power reaches 1000 HW gives birth to a Herrscher, and
that Honkai is then numbered. HI3's official definition (same page) agrees
on the structure without a number:

- **Small Honkai** happen everywhere, all the time. Limited energy, no
  Herrscher.
- **Large Honkai (Eruptions)** produce a Herrscher core, which forms in a
  suitable vessel (a human with extreme Honkai adaptability, often born
  with Honkai traits). They're numbered by the Herrscher they produce;
  Nagazora's will be the Third Eruption. The Eruption isn't caused by the
  Herrscher; a vessel being there is why it happens there. It's natural and
  can't be steered by humans.
- **Residual energy** after a Herrscher leaves can make a pseudo-Herrscher
  (拟似律者), not a true one.
- A Herrscher can trigger small Honkai and spawn beasts loyal to it, like a
  queen bee.

**HW = Honkai Welt** (HI3 wiki, "Honkai Welt (HW)").
