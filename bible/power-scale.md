# Power Scale

Canon HI3 power levels are inconsistent. These are this story's rulings.

## Combat Rating

Strength is measured against Valkyries, not with numbers. Every rank splits
into **Low**, **Mid** and **High**, and the gap inside one rank is real: a
High B beats a Low B almost every time.

The ladder, from bottom to top:

1. **Civilian:** an ordinary person.
2. **Peak Human:** Olympic athlete or special forces. The ceiling for a body
   Honkai energy can't touch.
3. **D** (Low, Mid, High)
4. **C** (Low, Mid, High)
5. **B** (Low, Mid, High)
6. **A** (Low, Mid, High)
7. **S** (Low, Mid, High)
8. **Herrscher:** a separate class, not a higher Valkyrie rank. Ranges from
   a newly awakened, incomplete Herrscher to a complete one.

A few canon figures (Kevin, Otto, Fu Hua) sit outside the ladder entirely.

### Canon S-ranks

Canon lists very few S-rank Valkyries: Cecilia Schariac (dead in the Second
Eruption, 2000), Reanna Brigantia (dead), and, later in the story, Bianka
"Durandal" Ataegina, Rita Rossweisse and Li Sushang. Durandal and Rita are
**not** S-rank in 2009; they're too young.

**Theresa Apocalypse is S-rank.** In 2009 she is the one active S-rank the
story needs to account for.

## Valkyrie ranks

Canon: Valkyries are women with high Honkai resistance and natural or
artificial stigmata. Only B-rank and above fight on the front lines. D-rank
Valkyries do support and logistics far from the real fighting. Valkyries
with poor Honkai adaptability die young, and any Valkyrie risks the
**Honkai cascade**, turning into a mindless zombie.

D and C ranks are logistics: medics, comms, drone operators, transport
pilots, battlesuit technicians. They are still trained Valkyries with
stigmata and can fight when they must.

| Rank | Role | Power | Environmental damage in a full fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Logistics, far from the fighting | Survives being hit by a car; can kill a lone Honkai zombie with a weapon | Breaks doors, cracks concrete |
| C | Logistics / combat support | Flips a car, fires heavy weapons by hand; dispatches Honkai zombies easily | Punches through brick walls, wrecks a room |
| B | Frontline | Fast enough to dodge small-arms fire; handles Archangel types and Chariots; fights Templars as a squad | Levels a house, craters a road |
| A | Elite, ace of a branch | Handles a Templar alone; a lineup of A-ranks takes on an Emperor-class | Destroys a building or a city block |
| S | Extremely rare: one or two at a time at most, some years none | Can handle an Emperor-class; Judgment-class needs a team with a few S-ranks and Divine Key-level weapons | Devastates a district; craters hundreds of meters wide |
| Herrscher | — | Wields an authority over reality | City-level and up; the strongest are continental or worse |

Eros starts as **Civilian**: a desk worker below Peak Human.

## Men, Knights and Paladins

- Honkai adaptability is uncommon in men, but in rare cases it happens.
  Those men become **Schicksal Knights** (Chevaliers), like Siegfried
  Kaslana. This is the canon path open to Eros once his Adaptability rises.
- **Paladins** ("the Overseer's chosen") are the Overseer's personal guard.
  Cecilia was one. Theresa became one while still B-rank, the lowest-ranked
  Paladin ever.

## Honkai Adaptability

How much Honkai energy a body can take. It sets a hard ceiling on his
Combat Rating. Women adapt far more easily than men; most men stay at F
for life.

| Grade | Rank ceiling | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| F | Peak Human | Normal human. Honkai energy only harms him. |
| E | Low D | Survives mild exposure. |
| D | High D | Basic battlesuits. Can survive an artificial stigma transplant. |
| C | High C | Standard battlesuits. |
| B | High B | Frontline gear. |
| A | High A | Elite gear. |
| S | High S | Top-tier gear; can touch a Divine Key without dying. |
| Beyond S | None | The Herrscher threshold. |

**Ways to raise it:** Purple-or-higher gifts that touch Honkai energy,
special heroine-run rewards, and rare items (Honkai crystals, beast core
fragments), which are risky to use.

## How Eros gets stronger

There is no stat transfer from the runs. His real body has to earn its rank.

- **Adaptability raises the ceiling.** Nothing else can.
- **Real training and real fights fill the room under the ceiling.** This
  is the grind, and it is slow.
- **Physical gifts** speed that growth or add abilities, but can't push him
  past his ceiling.
- **Experience packages** give him technique. His body has to catch up
  through training before he can use it fully.
- **Items and gear** (battlesuits, weapons) add power, within what his
  Adaptability allows. A stigma is not gear (see below). How Eros gets one
  is still open.

## Gear

- **Battlesuits:** combat suits that amplify the wearer. Each needs a
  minimum Adaptability grade.
- **Stigmata are part of the body, not equipment.** Swapping stigmata in and
  out is a game mechanic, not lore. Canon has three types:
  - **True (Herrscher) stigmata:** tied to a Herrscher Core; every Previous
    Era Herrscher had one. Only these connect to a core of their type.
  - **Natural stigmata:** designed by Dr. MEI and Mobius in the Previous Era
    and written into human DNA. Everyone carries one dormant; it wakes only
    after enough Honkai exposure, so awakened ones are very rare. Bloodlines
    like the Kaslanas and Schariacs carry strong ones.
  - **Artificial stigmata:** Schicksal's copies, modeled on natural ones and
    the weakest type. Given by transplant surgery to girls whose
    compatibility is too low (Himeko, 2006). Side effects: a shorter
    lifespan, slow bodily decay, and the cascade risk stays.
  - **Battlesuits** feed an artificial stigma through the suit's reactor to
    boost the wearer's body (Moegirl, 女武神装甲). A better suit means more
    power; the stigma itself isn't upgraded. Canon never shows one being
    replaced, so in this story a Valkyrie keeps the stigma she got.
- **Divine Keys:** pre-era weapons, the strongest gear that exists. They
  kill anyone below S-grade Adaptability who tries to use one.

## Measuring the Honkai (HW)

Schicksal measures a Honkai by its **instantaneous power in HW**. The
threshold is canon series lore (Moegirl, 律者, citing the Honkai Gakuen 2
library): "每一次瞬时功率达到1000HW的崩坏就会诞生一名律者" — every Honkai
whose instantaneous power reaches 1000 HW gives birth to a Herrscher, and
that Honkai is then numbered. HI3's official definition (same page) agrees
on the structure without a number:

- **Small Honkai** happen everywhere, all the time. Limited energy, no
  Herrscher.
- **Large Honkai (Eruptions)** produce a Herrscher core, which forms in a
  suitable vessel (a human with extreme Honkai adaptability, often born
  with Honkai traits). They're numbered by the Herrscher they produce;
  Nagazora's will be the Third Eruption. The Eruption isn't caused by the
  Herrscher; a vessel being there is why it happens there. It's natural and
  can't be steered by humans.
- **Residual energy** after a Herrscher leaves can make a pseudo-Herrscher
  (拟似律者), not a true one.
- A Herrscher can trigger small Honkai and spawn beasts loyal to it, like a
  queen bee.

**Story scale (our numbers, for consistency):**

| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0.01 to 0.05 HW | Background |
| 0.1 to 1 HW | A flicker; most are noise, some are a small event starting |
| 1 to 100 HW | Small Honkai: zombies, a few beasts. Pohang 2011 peaked at 41 HW |
| 100 to 999 HW | A bad Honkai: Emperor-class territory (the Ganesha, 2012) |
| 1000+ HW (instantaneous) | An Eruption; a Herrscher is born. Berlin 1953 (first), Siberia 2000 (second, well over 1000), Nagazora 2014 (third) |

**Nagazora's climb:** 0.3 HW (December 4, 2013), 9 HW (January 14, 2014,
the core surge), 12 (January 24), 15 (January 30), 28 HW (February 3, 1:11
a.m.), then over 1000 at the Third Eruption.
