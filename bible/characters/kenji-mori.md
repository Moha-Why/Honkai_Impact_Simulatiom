# Dr. Kenji Mori

**Role:** chief engineer of the Combat Simulation and Battlesuit Systems
team at the Far East Branch technical center. Eros's boss.

**Age:** 48 in 2009. Japanese.

**Appearance:** rumpled. Shirt untucked on one side, a lab coat with a
coffee stain he's stopped noticing, stubble most days. Reading glasses
pushed up into his hair and then lost there.

**Personality:** brilliant and buried. Sarcastic, blunt, and impatient with
anything that isn't the work. He delegates the second someone new walks in.
He works himself to the bone because when battlesuit firmware fails,
Valkyries die, and he has read those reports. He never talks about that.
In him, Eros sees exactly how he died the first time.

**How he talks:** fast, in half sentences. Swears mildly. Gives code modules
nicknames and assumes everyone knows them.

- "You found us with weather data. Fine. Here's three years of bugs."
- "Runs on Honkai energy. Don't lick it."
- "Does that. Welcome to Schicksal."

**Habits:** stacks empty coffee cans into a pyramid on his desk. Sleeps on a
cot in his office more nights than not. Three monitors, and the third one
is always showing a crash log.

**What he knows:** that Eros is a recruit genius who found Schicksal with
public data. He doesn't care how, only whether he can code.

**What he wants:** help with the backlog, and fewer dead Valkyries.

**Later:** Eros's mentor and his access to Valkyrie combat data and
battlesuit firmware.

**By 2014:** 53. Gave Eros the anomaly watch project (2010) and made him
senior engineer (2012). Still sleeps on the cot.

**In Chapter 1:** gives Eros the backlog; registers him on the computing
core (a shock, nothing else).

**In Chapter 2:** calls Eros down at 2 a.m. on January 14, 2014 for a
biometric hold during the core surge, when the System binds.
