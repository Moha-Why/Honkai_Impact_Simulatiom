# Kanda Yūko

**Role:** Eros's mother in Loop 8 of Mei's run only. Minor.

**Age:** thirty-three in 2004.

**Appearance:** small, quick, hair always coming out of its clip, a nurse's
watch pinned upside down on her cardigan.

**Background:** a night nurse in the children's ward of the ME Corp medical
institution in Nagazora. Single mother. Her son sleeps in the staff room or
on a spare bed in the ward on her night shifts, which is how he meets the
Raiden girl in 2004. Transferred to an ME Corp clinic in Osaka in 2007.

**Personality:** tired, kind, unsentimental. Lets the two children sit up
together at night because it's the only thing that makes the girl sleep.

**How she talks:** short, warm, practical. "Ten more minutes, then bed. Both
of you. I'm counting."

**Remarriage (Loop 8 childhood):** in 2008 Dr. Matsuda, a doctor at the
Osaka clinic, asks her twelve-year-old son if he may marry her. The boy says
no. She marries him anyway in spring 2009. He's kind and dull and calls the
boy "young man." She stays Kanda at work.
