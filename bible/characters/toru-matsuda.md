# Toru Matsuda

**Role:** Eros's eighteen-year-old classmate at Chiba Academy. Original
character introduced in Chapter 3. Holds the lunch-money wager and joins
the joke about Eros's daily rejections. His recording becomes part of the
school's treatment of Mei after the arrest.

**Personality:** sociable, inattentive to consequences, eager to be liked.
Can be embarrassed and pushed into helping without becoming a devoted ally.
He supplies a classmate's perspective, not knowledge of Honkai or the System.

**Speech:** quick, teasing, overexplains when caught. Calls Eros by name.

**Across loops:** no memories carry between versions. His choices respond
to what Eros actually does in that loop.
