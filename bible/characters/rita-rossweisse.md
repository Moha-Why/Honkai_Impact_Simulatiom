# Rita Rossweisse

**Role:** future heroine. In Chapter 2, Eros's liaison at HQ during the 2013
secondment. Canon character.

**Age (canon):** born March 1, 1995, which already matches the other
heroines. She turns 18 on March 1, 2013, early in the secondment. She is an
adult from then on; any spark between them is subtle and goes no further in
Chapter 2.

**Appearance (canon):** 168 cm, honey-blonde hair to the neck at the back
and slightly longer at the front, with the left eye covered by her fringe
(she sees fine). Reddish pink eyes. A smile that never leaves her face,
sweet to the point of being unsettling. A maid's uniform, monochrome with a
bow at the back. In battle, a scythe.

## Background (canon)

- Daughter of a well-known florist in Manchester, meant to take over the
  shop.
- In 2007, at 11, she was frozen with fear in a small Honkai incident in
  her hometown, and a Valkyrie dressed as a maid carried her to safety. It
  was Ragna. The next day Rita brought her sunflowers at the restaurant
  where Ragna worked undercover, and they talked all afternoon. Ragna told
  her that "maids are invincible".
- Joined Schicksal and trained as a Valkyrie, including an apprenticeship
  under Otto himself.
- In 2013 she's Otto's maid and one of HQ's most capable young Valkyries
  (story: A-rank; she reaches S-rank later in canon).
- Otto assigns her to Bianka, to help her mature and to make sure that
  when Bianka learns Otto isn't the man she thinks, she stays steady. In
  2013 that assignment is new.

## Personality (canon)

- Polite and elegant to a degree that makes people forget she's one of
  Schicksal's strongest Valkyries.
- Loves cleaning, cooking and being useful.
- Very well read (she relates to Catcher in the Rye), and speaks over a
  dozen languages, including Chinese and Japanese.
- Teases the people she likes, gently and relentlessly.
- Under the sweetness, she sees everything and reports what she must.

## How she talks

Soft, formal, unhurried, always smiling. Answers the question you should
have asked.

- "Master Eros. The Overseer hopes your stay will be comfortable."
- "That portrait is Kallen Kaslana. You've been looking at her for some
  time. Is something wrong with her?"
- "I'm only a maid."
- In German, once, to see what he does: "Your German is very poor for a
  man who works in Hamburg."

## With Eros (story, 2013)

- She's his liaison and guide, and plainly Otto's eyes. He knows it, she
  knows he knows, and both are perfectly polite about it.
- **The archive tour:** past the Kaslana portraits. Kallen hangs there and
  Siegfried's frame is empty. She notices him looking at Kallen too long.
- **Wit against wit:** she teases his cover story apart with one
  sentence in German.
- **What stays:** mutual wariness, some enjoyment of each other, and a seed
  of interest that stays only that.
