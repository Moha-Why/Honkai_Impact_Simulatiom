# Lukas Brenner

**Role:** Schicksal Far East Branch, internal security. He deals with leaks,
and recruits the people who find too much. Becomes Eros's handler.

**Age:** 44 in 2009. German.

**Appearance:** medium height, medium build, a face that's easy to forget,
which suits his job. Gray suit, rimless glasses, very clean shoes. Hair
going gray at the sides and cut short.

**Personality:** courteous, patient and calm. Never raises his voice and
never threatens anyone directly. He tells the truth when he can, partly
because it's simpler and partly because it scares people more. Dry sense of
humor that people often miss. He isn't cruel, and he isn't kind either; he
does the job in front of him.

**How he talks:** careful Mandarin with a slight accent, and he switches to
English when the details matter. Precise and polite. Leaves the threat
unsaid and lets the listener fill it in.

- "I'd rather not lie to you. It makes the paperwork longer."
- "There is another option. Most people don't need to hear it."
- "Is there anyone you'd like to call?"

**Habits:** reads a file at the start of every conversation, even when he
knows it by heart. Folds papers into exact halves. Sucks peppermints.

**What he knows:** Eros's whole file (no family, guardian died 2007) and the
anomaly program. He believes Eros knows nothing about Honkai beyond his own
data.

**What he wants:** the leak contained, and a useful mind working for
Schicksal instead of against it.

**Later:** his reports on Eros go up the chain. He's one of the first people
who might notice that Eros knows things he shouldn't.

**By 2014:** 49. Has coffee with Eros every March. Asked in 2010 why he
said yes so fast. In 2013: "You know, Eros, you're very boring." The
closest he has come to smiling.

**In Chapter 1:** waits in Eros's room, makes the offer, takes him to the
Far East Branch.

**In the Chapter 2 rewrite:** in March 2010 he also asks, "You asked about
the Kaslanas in orientation. Why?" Eros says he likes old families and old
stories; Brenner writes nothing down. The March 2013 coffee happens at HQ
during the secondment ("You're very boring"), and afterward he closes the
file. A collected drive from an outbreak zone (see ../future-arcs.md) could
open it again.
