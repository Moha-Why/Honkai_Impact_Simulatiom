# Bronya Zaychik

**Role:** future heroine. In Chapter 3, Cocolia's agent in Nagazora, seen
in Loop 3 and glimpsed in Loop 4. Canon character; story changes are marked.

**Age (story change):** born February 18, **1995** (canon August 18, 2000),
so 18 from February 18, 2013, before the adult Eros arrives in the Mei run
(April 13, 2013). She's 16 when she's brought to Cocolia's house in January
2012 (Loop 3's childhood log) and 18 at his door in January 2014.

**Appearance (canon):** short (147 cm) and slight, light gray hair in
drill-shaped twin tails, gray eyes, a blank face. Her legs are prosthetic
(canon: healthy when she arrives; Sin Mal breaks them at the orphanage in
November 2012, and X-10 in August 2013 paralyzes them for good. Story: she
is fitted with white prosthetic legs in September 2013). In the field, Project Bunny 19C, a
heavy mechanical symbiont she brought back from the Sea of Quanta, unfolds
around her.

**Background (canon):**

- Daughter of Alexei and Alexandra Zaychik. Orphaned, she ended up with a
  Russian gang and was made into a child sniper, "the Silver Wolf of the
  Urals".
- Sent to kill Cocolia in January 2012. The rest of the gang died; Cocolia
  spared her and took her into the orphanage, where Bronya called her
  "matushka".
- Best friend of Seele Vollerei, with romantic undertones; Seele cleaned her
  wounds when she first arrived, and they promised to go to the sea
  together. Sin Mal, jealous, broke her legs. Cocolia chose Bronya for the
  X-10 experiment (August 2013, the eighth attempt after seven failures).
  Seele, who has a stigma, asked to go in her place to protect her, and was
  lost in the Sea of Quanta. Bronya threatened to kill herself unless she
  was let in after Seele. Her long time in the trial let the Honkai into her
  legs and into the part of her brain that feels, and she came back without
  the use of her legs and without her emotions. Seele sent her back with
  Project Bunny 19C. Later, as the Herrscher of Reason, the Core of Reason
  rebuilds her legs and her feelings. (Sources: honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/X-10_Experiment,
  honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Bronya_Zaychik/Story.)
- **Before and after (story):** before X-10 she's guarded but feels things:
  she scowls, smirks, kicks under the table, says "you" and "I". After it
  her face is empty and she speaks in the third person. Only two people can
  still move something in her: Seele, and (in Loop 3) Eros, who sat with her
  before and after.
- A bio-chip in her brain lets Cocolia control her when she has to.

**Personality (canon):** deadpan voice, matching face. Very logical; bad
with intuition. Rarely shows empathy, but loves the HOMU franchise, video
games and Seele, and is competitive about games. Later sees Mei as an older
sister.

**How she talks (voice sheet):** always the third person, "Bronya," never
"I" (canon; 布洛妮娅). "Bronya thinks this strategy is flawed."

- **Before X-10 (story reading of canon):** still "Bronya," but with
  feeling in it: dry, scowling, teasing, protective. "Bronya ate your
  bread. It was yours." "He belongs to Bronya and Seele."
- **After X-10:** the same words gone flat, like a readout or a train
  announcement. Facts, probabilities, reports. She can't name a feeling, so
  she states things to check them ("Subject Eros will be here tomorrow").
- **Names.** People are "subject" ("Subject Eros," "Subject Captain"). Mei
  is "Sister Mei" (芽衣姐姐); Kiana is "idiot Kiana"; Cocolia is
  "matushka"; Seele is "Seele."
- **Report words** (evaluation, observation, warning) go inside the
  sentence, no colons: "Bronya's evaluation is that the plan is flawed."
- **What still gets through:** Seele, the people she's made her family,
  betrayal, games and HOMU.
- **The one break in Loop 3:** "Eros," his name without "subject," with
  everything in it.

**In Chapter 3:**

- **Loop 3 (the heart of the loop):** Seele and Eros are the two people
  she cares about most. Arrives at the house by Lake Baikal in January 2012
  with a hard face and healthy legs ("You're bad at this"); floors an older
  boy for him ("He's ours. Mine and Seele's"). Sin Mal breaks her legs in
  November 2012 while the boy says nothing; in hospital she won't let the
  doctors near until both of them are there. In 2013, at 18, she wears the
  chapel harness with a cast; watches the two of them like two doors; "I've
  got a different list now. Work it out." When Seele is gone she knows Eros
  knew, and holds his hand across the bunker anyway. In the Sea of Quanta
  Seele sends her back to him ("he promised me he would look after you. So
  go back and let him"). Afterward she follows him everywhere ("Subject Eros
  will be here tomorrow"), smiles once when he beats her score, and calls
  him every Sunday at nine from the house. In January 2014 Cocolia sends her
  to bring him home ("Matushka says come home"). He refuses, so she stays
  and follows him; she doesn't report the refusal. After Cocolia's people
  take him and lock him in the ME Corp tower, she stays with him and brings
  Seele's book. On the third night he goes over the terrace rail. She gets
  up on the white legs, too slow by three steps, and says "Eros," his name,
  the first thing with feeling in it since August.
- **Loop 8:** a small gray-haired figure on the roof across from the school
  gate on the morning of the Eruption. She doesn't reach the school roof
  before the Hyperion does.
