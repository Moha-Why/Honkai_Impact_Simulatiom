# Bronya Zaychik

**Role:** future heroine. In Chapter 3, Cocolia's agent in Nagazora, seen
in Loop 3 and glimpsed in Loop 4. Canon character; story changes are marked.

**Age (story change):** born August 18, **1995** (canon 2000), so 18 in the
run.

**Appearance (canon):** short (147 cm) and slight, light gray hair in
drill-shaped twin tails, gray eyes, a blank face. Her legs are prosthetic
(Sin Mal broke them at the orphanage). In the field, Project Bunny 19C, a
heavy mechanical symbiont she brought back from the Sea of Quanta, unfolds
around her.

**Background (canon):**

- Daughter of Alexei and Alexandra Zaychik. Orphaned, she ended up with a
  Russian gang and was made into a child sniper, "the Silver Wolf of the
  Urals".
- Sent to kill Cocolia in January 2012. The rest of the gang died; Cocolia
  spared her and took her into the orphanage, where Bronya called her
  "matushka".
- Best friend of Seele Vollerei, with romantic undertones; they promised to
  go to the sea together. Project X-10, the trial that was meant to make her
  immune to Honkai corruption, left her emotionally stunted. When Seele was
  lost in the Sea of Quanta, Bronya went in after her and came back with
  Project Bunny instead.
- A bio-chip in her brain lets Cocolia control her when she has to.

**Personality (canon):** deadpan voice, matching face. Very logical; bad
with intuition. Rarely shows empathy, but loves the HOMU franchise, video
games and Seele, and is competitive about games. Later sees Mei as an older
sister.

**How she talks:** flat, exact, third person. Calls almost everyone
"subject".

- "Bronya has located the subject."
- "Subject Eros is an obstacle. Bronya recommends he step aside."
- "Bronya does not understand the question."

**In Chapter 3:**

- **Loop 3:** already on the night train out of Nagazora when Eros takes Mei
  on it, with orders to bring Mei to Cocolia. Her move against Mei in the
  tunnel is what tips the awakening.
- **Loop 4:** a small gray-haired girl on the roof of the building across
  from Chiba Academy on the morning of the Eruption, watching. She's part of
  the outbreak's trigger. In canon she joins Mei and Kiana after the
  Eruption, so she survives the run.
