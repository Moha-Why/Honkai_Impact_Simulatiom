# The man in the gray car

**Role:** an Anti-Entropy operative working for Cocolia in Nagazora. Minor;
never named. Part of the operation she sets up around Mei after the arrest.

**Appearance:** forties, plain coat, plain face, a gray sedan. Forgettable
on purpose.

**Personality:** professional and unhurried. Doesn't explain himself.

**How he talks:** barely. "You've been asking about ME Corp."

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 1):** after Eros tries to warn Raiden Ryoma's office
and then a journalist, naming Cocolia, the man stops him on a quiet street
in December and shoots him. It's an ordinary death (a scattered detail
lost) and Eros's first lesson that fixed nodes push back.

**Later loops:** Eros sees the gray car once in Loop 4, parked across from
the school gate, and keeps walking.
