# The man in the gray car

**Role:** an unused possible Anti-Entropy operative working for Cocolia
in Nagazora. Minor; never named. No assigned scene in Until Tomorrow.

**Appearance:** forties, plain coat, plain face, a gray sedan.

**Personality:** professional and unhurried.

**Planning status:** retained as an unused concept. Chapter 3 does not use
the old street assassination. Cocolia's school operation can have ordinary
local staff without importing this abandoned encounter.
