# The man in the gray car

**Role:** an Anti-Entropy operative working for Cocolia in Nagazora. Minor;
never named. Part of the operation she sets up around Mei after the arrest.

**Appearance:** forties, plain coat, plain face, a gray sedan. Forgettable
on purpose.

**Personality:** professional and unhurried. Doesn't explain himself.

**How he talks:** barely. "You've been asking about ME Corp."

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 1):** after Eros digs into ME Corp with his father's
access, calls Ryoma's office with what he found, and hands a journalist a
folder, the man stops him on a quiet street in December and shoots him
(Eros is nobody to Cocolia in that file). "You've been asking about ME Corp." It's an ordinary death, a
scattered detail, and Eros had no Danger Sense to warn him.

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 3):** Eros's Thursday contact, level three of the
garage under the station. He would never harm one of Cocolia's children.
When Eros refuses to come home, he takes him, politely, with two men and
Bronya in the back seat: "Get in the car, please. She asked me to say
please." He takes his phone and console and locks him in the ME Corp
tower.
