# Chiba Academy: minor characters

## Ito Sayaka and Endo Natsumi

**Role:** Mei's "friends" before November 16. Rich, polished, popular.
After her father's arrest they drop her, and gossip about her.

- **Sayaka:** the leader. Pretty, sharp smile, always on her phone.
  "Honestly, I always thought there was something off about that family."
- **Natsumi:** follows Sayaka. Laughs a beat after everyone else.

**In Chapter 3:** in the final loop Eros records them gossiping about Mei
and lets her hear it.

## Mr. Fujita

**Role:** homeroom teacher of class 3-A. Tired, decent, close to
retirement. Introduces Eros as the scholarship transfer student and seats
him next to Mei because it's the only empty desk.

- "Please make our new classmate welcome. He's here on a scholarship, so
  he's very clever. Probably."
