# Chiba Academy: minor characters

Chiba Academy (千羽学园) is a private school in Nagazora for the children of
rich families. Most of the students arrive by car.

## Ito Sayaka and Endo Natsumi

**Role:** Mei's "friends" before November 16. Rich, polished, popular.
After her father's arrest they drop her and talk about her where she can
hear.

- **Sayaka:** the leader. Pretty, sharp smile, always on her phone. Runs the
  class group chat. "Honestly, I always thought there was something off
  about that family."
- **Natsumi:** follows Sayaka. Laughs a beat after everyone else. Is the one
  who moves her desk away from Mei's first.
- **Birthdays:** Sayaka May 20, 1995 (18 from May 2013; after November 16
  she turns up in a new white wool coat with gold buttons). The third girl
  in Mei's circle turns 18 in September 2013. Natsumi is born January 9,
  1996, and Hayashi Rin February 2, 1996 (both 17 all autumn).

**In Chapter 3:** Loop 7, Eros lets Sayaka see what she wants to see. Loop
8, he knows when they'll say it in the corridor and makes sure he's beside
Mei when she hears it, and does nothing to stop it.

**In Chapter 4 (Loop 4):** Mei asks Sayaka outright, in front of the class,
whether she's still her friend. Sayaka cries ("I'm sorry, I'm so sorry, my
mother said...") and sits with her at lunch for one day, chattering about a
TV drama (a clinic, a younger brother back from Osaka who, in Mei's words,
"has a face like a wet dog"), with the class chat turned face down: "You'll
have to watch it with me." Eros's police rumor, planted with Emi, sends her
back to the vending-machine table the next Tuesday. Natsumi sits behind Emi
and hears him plant it. In December she tells Mei at the shoe lockers, and
Mei sends her away ("Please don't come closer now to tell me who else I
should stay away from.").

- **Emi:** Sayaka's best friend. Sits behind Eros in maths and never liked
  Mei. The ear he puts the rumor in.

## Kuroda Shun

**Role:** the bully, and Eros's mirror. Minor but important.

**Age:** 18. Class 3-B.

**Appearance:** tall, handsome in a careful way, expensive watch, hair that
takes him twenty minutes. Captain of nothing, good at everything a little.

**Background:** son of a construction company's owner whose firm did
business with ME Corp. He confessed to Mei in the spring, in the courtyard,
in front of his friends, sure of a yes, and she turned him down kindly.
His friends haven't let him forget it.

**Personality:** charming to teachers, cruel in groups, never alone when
he's cruel. After November 16 his rejection turns into revenge. He's what a
rejected confessor looks like when he goes the other way.

**How he talks:** easy, smiling, loud enough for the room.

- "Hey, Queen. Who's paying for your lunch now?"
- "You still bothering her, scholarship? Read the news."
- "I'm not doing anything. I'm just talking."

**Bullying he leads or starts (Loop 8):** "criminal's daughter" written on
her desk, her indoor shoes in the trash, a bento knocked off her desk "by
accident", the joke that she'll be going to visit her father soon.

**In Chapter 3:** Loop 1, he's been Eros's pen pal since they were
thirteen (baseball, his father's company, a dog called Leo, "a girl in my
year everybody's afraid of"). He hugs Eros at the shoe lockers on his first
day and gives him a table in 3-B. After the arrest he writes on Mei's desk
and tells Eros at lunch, waiting for him to laugh. Eros sees the ink on his hand and doesn't. Before that he names Eros "the lightning rod" for confessing to Mei every day, runs a hundred-yen book on how long he'll last, and loses it. After the arrest: "You're still doing it? To her? Now?" Loop 2, at
fourteen he pays the chauffeur's nephew two thousand yen for advice on her
and is told she likes boys who confess in front of everyone, which is how
the spring courtyard confession happens (in that file). Loop 7, Eros feeds him gossip through a friend of a friend
and lets him do the damage. Loop 8, Eros lets the bullying run, then lets
Kuroda's group catch him behind the gym. They go further than he bet (a
pipe, a cracked rib). Mei breaks Kuroda's wrist with a bokken at the next
practice. When the outbreak hits, Kuroda is the first in the school to turn.

## Hayashi Rin

**Role:** kendo club vice-captain. Minor.

**Personality:** not cruel, just afraid of being next. After the arrest, the
club's parents complain, and Rin calls the vote that removes Mei as captain,
then can't look at her.

- "It's not personal, Raiden-san. The parents are asking questions."

**Loop 4:** apologizes by reflex, nearly cries when Eros takes the manager's
job, and tells him most things while he tapes her wrist. Keeps the club's
correspondence in the back of the record book and signs Mei's letter to the
board. Mei resigns before the vote and hands her the letter at her classroom
door: "I'd have voted for you." "I know you would have. So it's better that
you don't have to." Brings Mei's armor to 3-A in a bag.

- **Tomo:** a round-faced kendo first-year. Reties Eros's hachimaki ("You've
  tied that like a bandage") and warns him the captain once made a
  second-year cry.

## Mr. Fujita

**Role:** homeroom teacher of class 3-A. Tired, decent, close to
retirement. Introduces Eros as the scholarship transfer student and seats
him next to Mei because it's the only empty desk. After November 16 he does
nothing about the bullying and knows it.

- "Please make our new classmate welcome. He's here on a scholarship, so
  he's very clever. Probably."
- "Kuroda. Sit down. Please."

## School staff

- **The principal:** unnamed. Proud of the school's ME Corp patrons and
  reads the foundation scholarships aloud at the October assembly every
  year, through a microphone that whistles on every s. "Every year I have
  the honor of reading this list, and every year, I'm happy to say, it is
  much the same list." In Loop 4 he names Raiden Ryoma from the stage and
  makes Eros stand.
- **The caretaker:** does the evening round with his keys. "Raiden-san.
  I'm locking up."
- **The night secretary:** unnamed, in the school office. Takes Eros's
  anonymous call about Kiana on December 18 in Loop 5 ("I'll put it on the
  principal's desk tonight"). He dies on the hill the same night.
