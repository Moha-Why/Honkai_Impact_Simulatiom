# Anders Lindqvist

**Role:** Eros's father in Loop 1 of Mei's run only, an identity the System
built. Minor.

**Age:** early fifties.

**Appearance:** tall, thinning fair hair, rimless glasses, good suits. Always
looks as if he's just come off a plane, because he usually has.

**Background:** Swedish. ME Corp's overseas sales, based at the Nagazora
head office from the early 2000s, director from 2005. A widower. His son comes to live with him in
October 2013, in a company flat on the twenty-second floor of the ME Corp
staff tower.

**Personality:** polite, absent, generous with money and nothing else.
Incurious about his son as long as nothing reaches the office.

**How he talks:** short, in English to his son, in smooth business Japanese
to everyone else.

- "There's money on the card. Don't wait up."
- "Who asked you to look at that?"
- "Whatever you're doing, stop. Now. They're asking about you at work."

**In Chapter 3:** leaves his laptop and his access card lying around, and
Eros uses them to dig into the project that never existed. He notices only
after the arrest, and tells him to stop a week before the man in the gray
car does it for him.

**The childhood log (Loop 1):**

- **1998:** misses his flight because his three-year-old hid his passport in
  the bread bin. Four extra days of rain, a photograph on the pier at
  Ribersborg, and the only laugh his son ever hears from him. He asks for a
  copy. In 2013 it's the only photograph in the Nagazora flat, on the hall
  table behind the bowl of keys.
- **2003:** his son writes to the boss of the glass tower and Raiden Ryoma
  answers. Anders calls on a Wednesday to ask what he was thinking.
- **2005:** his ten-year-old tells him on the phone that Karin has weeks. He
  flies home, finds her marking at the kitchen table, and they have the
  worst fight of their marriage. After that he asks to speak to Karin first.
- **2006:** books a flight to Copenhagen four times and cancels it four
  times, each on the Friday. His webmail password is Ribersborg98. His son
  reads his mail every Sunday night from 2006 on.
- **2007:** takes the pier photograph and Mr. Raiden's letter off the kitchen
  wall and leaves the postcards.
- **December 2013:** "And from Malmö. Every Sunday night for seven years.
  From your grandmother's flat." / "Then you know about the flights."
