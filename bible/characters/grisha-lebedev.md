# Grigory "Grisha" Lebedev

**Role:** senior maintenance technician at Outpost 7, Siberia, in Run 1.
Eros's partner on the job and the first person he gets close to inside a
run. Dies in the Second Eruption.

**Age:** 46 in 1999. Russian.

**Appearance:** big and heavy, with a gray-shot beard that freezes white
outside. A hat with earflaps that he never ties. Missing the tip of his left
little finger (a fan belt, 1987).

**Personality:** loud, warm and stubborn. Treats Eros like a nephew who
needs feeding. Has worked at outposts for twenty years and believes nothing
the officers say. Brave without thinking about it: when something goes
wrong, he walks toward it.

**How he talks:** big voice, slow Russian, lots of teasing. Calls Eros
"boy" and later "professor". Answers serious questions with jokes.

- "You think the end of the world is coming, boy? Here it comes every
  winter."
- "Felt boots. Not those city things. You want to keep your toes?"
- "The officers say. The officers always say."

**Habits:** keeps tea and something stronger in the same thermos. Sends most
of his pay home to his wife and his daughter in Novosibirsk. Sings badly
while he works.

**What he knows:** generators, engines and outpost rumors. Nothing about
Honkai beyond "the special project" at the research city.

**In Chapter 1 (Run 1):** gives Eros felt boots on day one; laughs off his
warning to take leave at the end of 2000; goes back to the outpost radio
during the eruption instead of running.
