# Mr. Tanabe

**Role:** the Raiden family chauffeur. Minor. (Canon mentions Mei's
chauffeur but doesn't name him; the name is a story addition.)

**Age:** about 60.

**Appearance:** gray hair, white gloves, a black limousine he keeps
spotless.

**Personality:** kind, patient, discreet. Talks to Mei every day in the car
about school and her father's work.

**How he talks:** gentle, formal. "Young lady."

**Planned Chapter 3 branch (not drafted canon):** drives Mei to the ME
Corp records depot, notices that the supposed quarantine selectively
blocks visitors, and finds a usable service exit. He refuses an order to
abandon her without an independently verifiable plan. Positions the escape
vehicle, arranges the roadside room and takes part in planning the trip
to Yokohama. He is a chauffeur with judgment and useful local knowledge,
not a secret combatant.
