# Mr. Tanabe

**Role:** the Raiden family chauffeur. Minor. (Canon gives Mei a chauffeur
but doesn't name him; the name is a story addition.)

**Age:** about 60.

**Appearance:** gray hair, white gloves, a black limousine he keeps
spotless.

**Personality:** kind, patient, discreet. Talks to Mei every day in the car
about school and her father's work. Has driven her since she was small.

**How he talks:** gentle, formal. "Young lady."

**After November 16:** the assets are frozen and he's let go with the rest
of the staff. The limousine stops coming.

**In Chapter 3:**

- Before the arrest, he drives Mei to and from school every day.
- **Loop 2:** Eros is his nephew from birth, and Mei has known that boy
  since she was six. Waking at 18, Eros isn't him.
- **Loop 4:** let go at the end of November; by the next Tuesday ME Corp's car
  pool has taken him back, driving the new management. Eros puts that true
  fact beside another (somebody showed the police where to look) and tells
  Mei to go and look. She watches him open a car door for a new director and
  stops answering his calls.
- **Loop 6:** driving the car in 2004 when Mei is taken; the housekeeper's
  boy dies on the road beside it.
- **Loop 7:** let go at the end of November, he rings her every evening.
  Eros starts a rumor through Mochizuki's cousin that the driver showed the
  police where to look; Kuroda repeats it; Mei lets his calls ring out.
- **Loop 8:** on the first rainy afternoon after the arrest he comes to the
  gate in his own small old car to drive her home. Eros sees the car from the
  classroom window and walks her out the back gate. Tanabe waits in the rain, and she never knows he came.
