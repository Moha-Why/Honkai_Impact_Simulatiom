# Mr. Tanabe

**Role:** the Raiden family chauffeur. Minor. (Canon mentions Mei's
chauffeur but doesn't name him; the name is a story addition.)

**Age:** about 60.

**Appearance:** gray hair, white gloves, a black limousine he keeps
spotless.

**Personality:** kind, patient, discreet. Talks to Mei every day in the car
about school and her father's work.

**How he talks:** gentle, formal. "Young lady."

**In Chapter 3:** drives Mei to and from Chiba Academy; nods to Eros once he
starts walking her to the car.
