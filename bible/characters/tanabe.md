# Mr. Tanabe

**Role:** the Raiden family chauffeur. Minor. (Canon mentions Mei's
chauffeur but doesn't name him; the name is a story addition.)

**Age:** about 60.

**Appearance:** gray hair, white gloves, a black limousine he keeps
spotless.

**Personality:** kind, patient, discreet. Talks to Mei every day in the car
about school and her father's work.

**How he talks:** gentle, formal. "Young lady."

**Planned Chapter 3 (not drafted canon):** brings Mei to school before
the arrest and remains one of the few familiar adults who treats her
consistently afterward. Can notice the daily-confession routine without
approving Eros automatically. His availability and professional limits
change with the family's circumstances. He is not a secret fighter or
a source of unrestricted access to ME Corp.
