# Seele Vollerei

**Role:** Bronya's closest friend and the reason for Cocolia's whole
February. Minor in Chapter 3 (Loop 3's childhood only). Canon character,
heroine later.

**Age (story change):** born March 18, **1995** (canon birthday October 18;
her old canon profile gave her age as 14), moved with the other heroines so
that she's 18 before the adult Eros arrives in the Mei run (April 13, 2013).
She's 15 when she arrives at Cocolia's house in 2010 and 17 when she takes
Bronya's place in the experiment, in August 2012. She turns 18 on March 18, 2013, lost in
the Sea of Quanta.

**Age rule:** she only appears in the boy's childhood log (2010 to August
2012), always under 18, written as an ordinary teenage girl. The adult Eros
never meets her in Chapter 3.

**Appearance (canon):** short dark blue hair, blue eyes, small and slight.
Holds books against her chest.

**Background (canon):**

- An orphan at Cocolia's orphanage; Bronya's closest friend, with romantic
  undertones.
- Lost in the Sea of Quanta during one of Cocolia's experiments. Getting her
  back is what Cocolia wants most, and why she sets off the Third Eruption.
- Comes back later with a scythe and a darker second self.
  (https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Seele_Vollerei)

**Personality:** soft, shy, always reading, says yes to whatever matushka
asks. Brave only when someone else needs it.

**How she talks:** quietly, apologizing first. "Don't tell Bronya. Not
till it's done. And look after her." / "Keep my place. I'll want it back."

**She's a good kid.** She would never ask anyone to take a risk for her.
In canon she shows Cocolia her stigma and asks to go into X-10 in Bronya's
place.

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 3):** arrives in 2010 and follows Eros around for a
month; he helps her with mathematics. In spring 2012 she reads to Bronya on
the landing, and the two of them plan to see a warm sea. In August 2012
Cocolia chooses Bronya for the experiment; Seele shows Cocolia her stigma
and goes instead. She asks the boy to keep it from Bronya until it's done
and to look after her. Eros has him keep the secret, because her loss is
the engine of February. She gives him her book with the corner turned down.
He wakes at 18 with it on his shelf.
