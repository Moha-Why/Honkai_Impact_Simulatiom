# Seele Vollerei

**Role:** Bronya's closest friend and the reason for Cocolia's whole
February. Minor in Chapter 3 (Loop 3's childhood only). Canon character,
heroine later.

**Age (story change):** born March 18, **1995** (canon birthday October 18;
her old canon profile gave her age as 14), moved with the other heroines so
that she's 18 before the adult Eros arrives in the Mei run (April 13, 2013).
She's 15 when she arrives at Cocolia's house in 2010 and 17 when she takes
Bronya's place in the experiment, in August 2012. She turns 18 on March 18, 2013, lost in
the Sea of Quanta.

**Age rule:** in the childhood log (2010 to April 2013) she's under 18
until March 2013 and written as an ordinary teenage girl. In Loop 3's adult
half (April to August 2013) she's 18, in a faded blue dress and a
cardigan handed down from the older girls.

**Appearance (canon):** short dark blue hair, blue eyes, small and slight.
Holds books against her chest.

**Background (canon):**

- An orphan at Cocolia's orphanage; Bronya's closest friend, with romantic
  undertones.
- Lost in the Sea of Quanta during one of Cocolia's experiments. Getting her
  back is what Cocolia wants most, and why she sets off the Third Eruption.
- Comes back later with a scythe and a darker second self.
  (https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Seele_Vollerei)

**Personality:** soft, shy, always reading, says yes to whatever matushka
asks. Brave only when someone else needs it.

**How she talks (voice sheet):** soft, hesitant, apologizing first, with
pauses and trailing off. Like Bronya, she refers to herself in the third
person, "Seele" (希儿). When she forces herself to be brave her sentences
turn formal and complete.

- **Names.** Bronya is "Bronya-nee" (布洛妮娅姐姐, big sister Bronya);
  Cocolia is "matushka."
- **Tells.** Her hand goes to her chest, over the hidden stigma, when she's
  scared or lying. Fairy tales and picture books.
- **Her hidden spine:** timid, not a coward. She walks into terrible things
  for Bronya without hesitating.
- Story lines: "Don't tell Bronya-nee. Not till it's done... And look after
  her. Please." / "Keep Seele's place. Seele will want it back."

**Veliona (Dark Seele):** the stigma's other self. Says "I." Mocking,
confident, sharp; cruel to others and fiercely protective of Seele. Her eyes
are a luminous crimson; Seele's are blue. Seele doesn't remember what she
does.

**She's a good kid.** She would never ask anyone to take a risk for her.
In canon she shows Cocolia her stigma and asks to go into X-10 in Bronya's
place.

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 3):** arrives in 2010 and follows the boy around for
a month; he helps her with mathematics. In January 2012 she cleans Bronya's
split knuckles on her first night. In spring 2012 she reads to Bronya on
the landing, and they plan to see a warm sea ("You can come if you carry
the bags"). In December 2012, after Sin Mal breaks Bronya's legs, her dark
side pushes Sin Mal down the same stairs (eyes the wrong color; she doesn't
remember it). In August 2013 Cocolia chooses Bronya for X-10. Seele shows
Cocolia the mark on her chest and takes her place, then comes to the adult
Eros at one in the morning: "Don't tell her. Not till it's done... And
look after her. Please." He keeps the secret, because her loss is the
engine of February. "Keep Seele's place. Seele will want it back." He watches her come
apart in the machine in the bunker under the house.
