# Seele Vollerei

**Role:** Bronya's closest friend and the reason for Cocolia's whole
February. Minor in Chapter 3 (Loop 3's childhood only). Canon character,
heroine later.

**Age (story change):** born October 18, **1995** (canon birthday October
18; her old canon profile gave her age as 14), moved with the other
heroines so she is 18 by the Third Eruption. So she's 14 when she arrives
at Cocolia's house in 2010, 16 when she goes into the Sea of Quanta in
autumn 2012, and turns 18 there, lost, on October 18, 2013.

**Age rule:** under 18 in every Chapter 3 scene. She dresses and is
written as an ordinary teenage girl. The world's adult fashion never
touches her before October 2013.

**Appearance (canon):** short dark blue hair, blue eyes, small and slight.
Holds books against her chest.

**Background (canon):**

- An orphan at Cocolia's orphanage; Bronya's closest friend, with romantic
  undertones.
- Lost in the Sea of Quanta during one of Cocolia's experiments. Getting her
  back is what Cocolia wants most, and why she sets off the Third Eruption.
- Comes back later with a scythe and a darker second self.
  (https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Seele_Vollerei)

**Personality:** soft, shy, always reading, says yes to whatever matushka
asks. Brave only when someone else needs it.

**How she talks:** quietly, apologizing first. "Would you go instead?
You're braver than me." / "Keep my place."

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 3):** arrives in 2010 and follows Eros around for a
month; he helps her with mathematics. In August 2012 she asks him to go
into the experiment instead of her. Eros has him say no, because her loss
is the engine of February. She gives him her book with the corner turned
down. He wakes at 18 with it on his shelf.
