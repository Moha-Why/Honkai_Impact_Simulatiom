# Naoko Senda

Status: retired Collateral-outline concept. Not introduced in drafted
prose and not part of the current Until Tomorrow plan.

**Role:** ME Corp records clerk retained to finish the depot handover.
Witness and unwilling escape partner.

**Age:** 29. Japanese.

**Appearance:** neat lipstick, a cardigan over office clothes, a lanyard with
too many keys. Keeps a brightly colored tab on the otherwise dull staff
ledger so nobody can say they did not see it.

**Personality:** talkative, proud of knowing where things actually are.
Complains, negotiates and wants to be paid. Neither a silent mentor nor a
martyr. Anger at being treated as disposable helps her act despite fear.

**What she wants:** unpaid wages, a safe way out and the ledger proving
which orders she followed. She will not surrender the one document that
prevents her being blamed for the entire transfer.

**What she knows:** copied a local approval before it vanished; can identify
alterations to the staff records and the off-site office holding the
sponsor's authorization. She cannot supply that missing authorization
herself or clear Ryoma of everything.

**How she talks:** specific complaints, practical bargaining, fast when
nervous. Will correct a detail in the middle of a frightening discussion.

- "If they're innocent boxes, why does one of them need a doctor?"
- "The ledger comes. You can complain when we're in the car."

**Retired Collateral outline:** draws attention to the medical collection; is detained;
leaves with Mei and the ledger; prepares the manifest that makes the
escape vehicle look routine; identifies the appointment in Yokohama.
Retains her own aims after rescue.
