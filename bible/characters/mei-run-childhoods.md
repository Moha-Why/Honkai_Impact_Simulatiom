# Mei run: the childhood casts

Minor characters who exist only in the childhood logs of Mei's heroine run
(Chapter 3). Each belongs to one life.

## Loop 1, Malmö

Loop 1 is the test save, so its childhood has the most forks of any loop
(eleven, plus the birth pick). Eros uses them to learn the engine.

- **Karin Lindqvist:** his mother. Teaches Swedish at a lower school, warm,
  tired, pins her husband's postcards on the kitchen wall in rows. Ill from
  2004, diagnosed in Lund in February 2005, dies March 2007. Furious with
  Anders after the 2005 false alarm and never quite forgives him for
  believing it.
- **His grandmother ("Mormor"):** Karin's mother, three streets away,
  cardamom buns on Saturdays. Keeps a blue Venetian glass bird with a gold
  beak on her windowsill (her mother's, 1931). Seventy-eight in 2007. Gets
  Honkai sickness in September 2008 walking to the harbor to look for him;
  mostly recovers. Starts forgetting in 2011 (the doctors say the harbor
  might cause it later, or might not). Falls in a kitchen fire in December
  2012 and breaks her hip; into a home in May 2013.
  - "You broke the bird. Sigge never went in this room. I knew the whole
    time, Eros. I wanted to see if you'd tell me."
- **Sigge:** her fat gray cat. Blamed for the bird in 2000 and given to the
  Bergs downstairs. Still in their window, fatter, in 2008.
- **The Bergs:** the family downstairs from the grandmother. Take Sigge.
- **The school psychologist:** tests him in 2001 (score 104). "A perfectly
  ordinary boy who has done a great deal of counting."
- **Kuroda Shun (canon to this story):** his pen pal from January 2009 to
  March 2012. See chiba-classmates.md.
- **Raiden Ryoma:** answers the boy's 2003 letter addressed to "the boss of
  the glass tower." See raiden-ryoma.md.
- **Anders Lindqvist:** see anders-lindqvist.md.

**Things the boy keeps:** the Book of Bad Days (a scrapbook of Honkai news
cuttings from February 2000, each with its distance from Malmö measured in
string on the atlas; page nine is Cocolia at the gate of her orphanage, from
Sydsvenskan, 2001; the back pages hold his list of password guesses). The
photograph from the pier at Ribersborg, August 1998 (Anders takes it in
2007). Mr. Raiden's letter (Anders takes it too). Four years of Kuroda's
letters and one photograph of Kuroda with his dog, Leo.

## Loop 2, the garage

- **Tanabe Michiko:** Mr. Tanabe's sister, twenty years younger, works at a
  dry cleaner's. Killed with her husband on the coast road, January 2001.
- **His father:** drove the number 14 bus and sang in the bath. His cap
  stays on the shelf over the garage for twelve years.

## Loop 3, Siberia

- **His parents:** a power-station turbine engineer and the village's only
  teacher, ninety kilometers south of the research city. Both die in the
  Second Eruption, February 16, 2000.
- **The girl with the gloves:** three, in the back of the truck; he gives
  her Cocolia's gloves. One of the house's children afterward.
- **Seele Vollerei (canon):** soft, shy, always reading; arrives in 2010,
  follows him for a month, lost on one of Cocolia's experiments in autumn
  2012.

## Loops 4, 5 and 7, the port children's home

- **Mrs. Hōjō:** director of the Sakura-no-Ie home by the old port. Gave him
  the name from his hospital band. Writes "a responsible boy" in his file;
  cries when he passes the scholarship.
- **Daichi:** a bigger boy who took the little ones' dinners; moved to Kobe
  after Eros told.
- **The noodle stall man:** by the station; gives him the end of the pot on
  Saturdays.
