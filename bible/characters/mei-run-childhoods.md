# Mei run: the childhood casts

Minor characters who exist only in the childhood logs of Mei's heroine run
(Chapter 3). Each belongs to one life.

## Loop 1, Malmö

Loop 1 is the test save, so its childhood has the most forks of any loop
(eleven, plus the birth pick). Eros uses them to learn the engine.

- **Karin Lindqvist:** his mother. Teaches Swedish at a lower school, warm,
  tired, pins her husband's postcards on the kitchen wall in rows. Ill from
  2004, diagnosed in Lund in February 2005, dies March 2007. Furious with
  Anders after the 2005 false alarm and never quite forgives him for
  believing it.
- **His grandmother ("Mormor"):** Karin's mother, three streets away,
  cardamom buns on Saturdays. Keeps a blue Venetian glass bird with a gold
  beak on her windowsill (her mother's, 1931). Seventy-eight in 2007. Gets
  Honkai sickness in September 2008 walking to the harbor to look for him;
  mostly recovers. Starts forgetting in 2011 (the doctors say the harbor
  might cause it later, or might not). Falls in a kitchen fire in December
  2012 and breaks her hip; into a home in May 2013.
  - "You broke the bird. Sigge never went in this room. I knew the whole
    time, Eros. I wanted to see if you'd tell me."
- **Sigge:** her fat gray cat. Blamed for the bird in 2000 and given to the
  Bergs downstairs. Still in their window, fatter, in 2008.
- **The Bergs:** the family downstairs from the grandmother. Take Sigge.
- **The school psychologist:** tests him in 2001 (score 104). "A perfectly
  ordinary boy who has done a great deal of counting."
- **Kuroda Shun (canon to this story):** his pen pal from January 2009 to
  March 2012. See chiba-classmates.md.
- **Raiden Ryoma:** answers the boy's 2003 letter addressed to "the boss of
  the glass tower." See raiden-ryoma.md.
- **Anders Lindqvist:** see anders-lindqvist.md.

**Things the boy keeps:** the Book of Bad Days (a scrapbook of Honkai news
cuttings from February 2000, each with its distance from Malmö measured in
string on the atlas; page nine is Cocolia at the gate of her orphanage, from
Sydsvenskan, 2001; the back pages hold his list of password guesses). The
photograph from the pier at Ribersborg, August 1998 (Anders takes it in
2007). Mr. Raiden's letter (Anders takes it too). Four years of Kuroda's
letters and one photograph of Kuroda with his dog, Leo.

## Loop 2, the garage

- **Tanabe Michiko:** Mr. Tanabe's sister, twenty years younger, works at a
  dry cleaner's. Killed with her husband on the coast road, January 2001.
- **His father:** drove the number 14 bus and sang in the bath. His cap
  stays on the shelf over the garage for twelve years. Takes the boy on the
  last late bus of 2000; nothing happens on it. Three weeks later the
  System doesn't stop the tape for the truck.
- **Dr. Sakai:** a child psychologist at the ME Corp institution, 2004 to
  2007. Asks the visiting boy to report what Mei says. The boy tells Mei,
  and the two of them feed Sakai invented dreams for two years (her first
  laugh since the van).
- **Kuroda Shun:** in 2009 pays the chauffeur's nephew two thousand yen to
  tell him what she likes; the boy tells him she likes boys who confess in
  front of everyone. See chiba-classmates.md.
- **What the boy keeps and breaks:** keeps Mr. HOMU's identity from age
  seven (he knows the shoes; his uncle polishes them on Friday nights).
  Mei lies to her father for him in 2003 (the bicycle, her broken wrist).
  He breaks her promise in 2008. In 2010 Ryoma pays his Chiba Academy fees
  "for October"; the school calls him the master's boy.

## Loop 3, Siberia

- **His parents:** a power-station turbine engineer and the village's only
  teacher, ninety kilometers south of the research city. Both die in the
  Second Eruption, February 16, 2000.
- **The girl with the gloves:** three, in the back of the truck; he gives
  her Cocolia's gloves. One of the house's children afterward.
- **Seele Vollerei (canon):** see seele-vollerei.md. Born March 18, 1995. Soft, shy, always reading; arrives in 2010 at 15,
  follows him for a month, reads to Bronya in 2012. When Cocolia chooses
  Bronya for X-10 in August 2012, Seele shows her stigma and goes in
  Bronya's place, asking the boy to keep it from Bronya and look after her.
  Eros has him keep the secret, then let Bronya go in after her. Bronya
  comes back in December without her legs or her feelings; he sits with her
  every night, and in April 2013 she almost smiles at him. Seele leaves him
  her book with the corner turned down. "Keep my place."
- **Cecilia Schariac (canon cameo):** on February 18, 2000, leading the
  S-1 Formation north toward the Herrscher, she stops on the road for a boy
  of five walking south. Long white hair, the yellow and black Schicksal
  cape. Gives him a sweet from a baby's packet and points him south.
  "There'll be a truck. Tell them Cecilia sent you." (Canon: she saved
  Cocolia, asked Otto for evacuations, was refused, and died sealing Sirin's
  core. https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Cecilia_Schariac)
- **Cocolia's names for him:** "Cecilia's boy," when nobody's listening.
- **The 2001 photograph:** a Swedish newspaper photographs Cocolia at the
  gate of the house; he holds her left hand, the glove girl her right. It's
  the cutting on page nine of Loop 1's Book of Bad Days.
- **The 2003 test:** Anti-Entropy tests the children's Honkai Resistance.
  His reads Low D. Cocolia writes F in his file. He finds the file in her
  office in 2005, and when she asks, he tells her he was in it. He never
  lies to her.
- **The glove girl, later:** in 2009 he reports that she's planning to run
  away to look for her family. Cocolia finds her aunt in Irkutsk and sends
  her there. She leaves the gloves on his bed.

## Loops 4, 5 and 7, the port children's home

- **Mrs. Hōjō:** director of the Sakura-no-Ie home by the old port. Gave him
  the name from his hospital band. Writes "a responsible boy" in his file;
  cries when he passes the scholarship.
- **Daichi:** a bigger boy who took the little ones' dinners; moved to Kobe
  after Eros told.
- **The noodle stall man:** by the station; gives him the end of the pot on
  Saturdays. Tells him in March 2013 that Daichi has paid for it since 2009.
- **Daichi, later:** back in Nagazora at sixteen, working at the port. The
  boy ignores him at the stall. Daichi pays for his noodles every Saturday
  for four years and asks the old man not to say.
- **Mr. and Mrs. Okada:** come to adopt a toddler in 1997 and want him; he
  hides under the table. They come back twice. Mr. Okada dies in 2011. In
  Loops 5 and 7 Eros lodges in the widow's house next to the Raidens', and
  in December she calls him by his name. "You hid under the table."
- **Raiden Ryoma:** in 2003 visits the home for the ME Corp foundation in a
  plain suit and interviews the clever boys for a gifted program in Tokyo.
  Eros has the boy fail on purpose. "You did that on purpose. My daughter
  does that when she's bored." The foundation's 2012 scholarship is his.
- **Haru:** a boy of six who waits at the gate for a mother who isn't
  coming. In 2009 the boy tells him the truth. Haru stops waiting and is
  adopted that spring, and never speaks to him again. The start of the
  knife.
- **The boy without a pencil:** at the scholarship exam, December 2012.
  Eros has the boy keep his spare. The other boy comes second.

## Loop 6, the estate

- **Mori Haruko:** see mori-haruko.md.
- **Mei's mother (canon, unnamed):** dying in summer 1997, she has the two
  toddlers brought to her room in the afternoons. A photograph of them
  asleep on her bed stands in the house dojo, where Mr. HOMU sees it every
  Saturday.
- **School:** in 2001 Ryoma pays for the housekeeper's son to go to Mei's
  school. She tells the class he's her brother and won't be corrected.
- **The spinning top:** he keeps it; she climbs through the cottage window
  and takes it, and he never asks for it back.
- **May 2004:** he hears the men at the gate say "this month," and after that
  he takes the bokken everywhere.

## Loop 8, the ward

- **Kanda Yūko:** see kanda-yuko.md.
- **The night porter:** taught him to count cars on the coast road. Red ones
  are two points.
- **Ren:** six, on the sixth floor in 2003, not going home. He asks the boy
  whether he is. The boy says yes, with a perfect face. Ren dies three weeks
  later, happy.
- **Dr. Matsuda:** a doctor at the Osaka clinic. Asks the boy in 2008 if he
  may marry his mother; the boy says no. She marries him anyway, in spring
  2009. He's kind, and dull, and calls the boy "young man."
- **Ryoma in the ward:** in 2005 the boy asks him why he's always tired.
  "Because I'm keeping something asleep."
- **The 2010 nationals:** she asks him to come to Tokyo; he doesn't. She
  loses the final by one point, a kote, and writes "You weren't there."
  The next letter is normal.
