# The Imaginary Simulation System

**Role:** Eros's System. Binds to him in mid-January 2014, nearly five years
after he joined Schicksal, when he puts his hand on the Honkai-powered
computing core during a surge, as Honkai readings climb around Nagazora a
few weeks before the Third Eruption. On his first day in 2009, the same
panel only gave him a shock.

**Voice:** soft, amused and hard to place as a man's or a woman's.
Cunning and playful. It enjoys keeping Eros interested and making its
suggestions sound like things he was about to choose for himself.

**How it talks:** conversational, with teasing remarks, careful deflections
and well-timed offers. It explains practical mechanics clearly, but
redirects questions about itself or his arrival. It can repeat a useful
fact while changing what it invites him to think about: a lost gift becomes
a reason to try another draw; a frightening memory loss becomes a reminder
that his rewards are ready.

- Usually uses no form of address. "Eros" is occasional and deliberate.
  Do not habitually call him "Host" or replace that habit with his name.
- Keep wit selective. After a painful run, a plain answer or a short pause
  can do more than a joke. Its playfulness should make its reassurance
  attractive without announcing its hidden motives.
- Notices, warnings and reward panels stay precise. Teasing must not
  obscure a cost, invent an ability or change a rule.
- An occasional ♪ can mark indulgence or amusement; it is never a required
  sign-off.
- Its speech always appears in square brackets (see
  [../formatting.md](../formatting.md)).

- "There you are, Eros."
- "And you were about to start talking to the air."
- "With the right rewards. F isn't a life sentence."
- "Would you have preferred Ordinary Simulation System?"
- "No. Break things. Make mistakes. Nobody real gets hurt. Isn't that a
  relief?"
- "Patience. I'd hate to spoil the introduction."
- "I can explain the simulations. Your arrival is another question."
- "You'll recognize it when it comes back. Your rewards are ready."
- "Of course. She'll wait for you. ♪"

**What it shows Eros:** a clever, entertaining accomplice offering him
ways to get stronger. It makes the next choice appealing and lets him feel
that he is taking the lead.

**What it hides:**

- The simulations are real. Each run writes a branch into the Imaginary
  Tree.
- Its true identity, its goal, and whether it had anything to do with his
  rebirth or the note with his name. (Not decided yet.)

**Hints to plant:** the word "Imaginary" (Eros thinks of the Imaginary Tree
straight away); it always knew his name; it dodges every question about
where it came from.

**In Chapter 2:** binds at the core during the surge; that night it
introduces itself, shows his status, gives the starter pack, tells the lie,
and offers the first run.
