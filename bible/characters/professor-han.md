# Professor Han Wei

**Role:** computer science professor at the university in Hangzhou. Eros's
advisor from 2006.

**Age:** 54 in 2009.

**Appearance:** heavy-set, thinning hair, a sweater vest in every season.
Glasses he pushes up with one knuckle.

**Personality:** kind, careful and a little cowardly, and he knows it. Lived
through hard years and learned which questions not to ask. Proud of Eros and
slightly afraid of him.

**How he talks:** gentle and hedging. Starts sentences with "Well" and
"Perhaps". Taps his pen on the table when he's nervous.

- "Well. Perhaps this is something to leave alone."
- "Some data is missing because someone wanted it missing. Those people
  have phone numbers."

**What he knows:** that Eros's anomaly map is real, and that it points at
something governments are hiding. He guesses military, not Honkai.

**What he wants:** for Eros to have a long, quiet career, and to stay out of
trouble himself.

**In Chapter 1:** takes Eros on at fifteen; looks at the anomaly map and
tells him to drop it.

**In the Chapter 2 rewrite (April 2010):** lunch in Hangzhou during Eros's
first Qingming leave. Listens to the Germany story, sees through it, and
doesn't push. "Are you happy?" "I think so." "Then I won't ask about the
phone numbers."
