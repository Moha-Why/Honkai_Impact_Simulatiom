# Otto Apocalypse

**Role:** the Overseer of Schicksal, Theresa's "grandfather", and the most
dangerous person alive for Eros. In Chapter 2, a portrait in a corridor and
one glimpse at HQ in 2013. Canon character.

**Age:** born in 1452 or 1477 (sources disagree). Over 500 years old by
2009. He survives by moving his consciousness into new bodies.

**Appearance (canon):** looks like a man in his thirties. Long pale blond
hair, red eyes, a gentle, handsome face. White and gold robes like a
bishop's. A smile that stays on his mouth and never reaches his eyes.

**Background (canon, the parts Eros knows):**

- Took over Schicksal after Kallen Kaslana's death in the 15th century, and
  has ruled it ever since.
- Everything he has done for five centuries serves one goal: bringing
  Kallen back. He has studied the Imaginary Tree and the Sea of Quanta for a
  world where she lives.
- Ran the A-3 experiments (Kallen's cells with the Emperor-class Vishnu's
  genes) in 1972.
- Theresa is his creation and calls him grandfather. They're at odds in
  2009, and he gave her the island anyway.
- Made K-423, the clone the world will know as Kiana.
- Doctor to the amnesiac Bianka (Durandal) since she was small, and truly
  gentle with her, in his way.

**Personality (canon):** charming, theatrical, endlessly patient,
manipulative to the bone. Uses everyone, his own family included, and
believes he's entitled to. Capable of real tenderness, mostly toward the
dead. Brilliant.

**How he talks:** soft, warm and amused, with classical references and
gentle questions that are really orders.

- To Bianka: "You trained all night again. You'll make me worry."
- To anyone else: "How interesting."

**Danger to Eros (story):**

- Fate Deviation doesn't draw his attention by itself. But a man who
  predicts eruptions, and later carries a System with "Imaginary" in its
  name, is exactly what he has spent 500 years looking for. Eros must never
  give him a reason to look twice.
- If a run ever touches Otto's past, Otto will remember Eros (see
  [../simulation-system.md](../simulation-system.md)).

**In Chapter 2:**

- **2009:** the life-size portrait in the corridor by the orientation room.
- **2013, HQ:** visits Bianka in the simulation room, gentle with her and
  cold-smiling with everyone else. His eyes pass over Eros for one second.
  Nothing happens. Eros doesn't sleep that night.
