# Mizuki Tsugumi

**Role:** the woman Eros falls in love with in the real world. Not part of
the game, not Schicksal, not Anti-Entropy. A major character in the arc
after the Third Eruption. She dies for real, and it stays that way.
Original character.

**Age:** born October 3, 1989. 21 when Eros first buys her demo in August
2011, 24 when they meet in person in 2014. An adult throughout.

**Home:** Shiomi, a small fictional port town on the mainland, where the
island's ferry docks, an hour by train from Nagazora. She lives in two rooms
above her family's print shop, Mizuki Printing, on the street behind the
ferry terminal.

## Appearance

158 cm. Messy dark brown hair shoved into a clip she keeps losing, big round
glasses, ink on her fingers from the presses downstairs and from her drawing
tablet. Oversized cardigans with holes in the cuffs, old sneakers. Pretty in
an ordinary way that people notice on the second look. A lopsided smile that
shows up before she decides to smile.

## Background

- Grew up above the print shop with a loud, warm, ordinary family:
  - **Her father, Tetsuo:** prints flyers, menus and funeral notices, and
    thinks video games are a waste of a good brain.
  - **Her mother, Yoshiko:** runs the counter and feeds everyone who
    stands still.
  - **Her little brother, Kōta:** 16 in 2014, a baseball kid.
- Dropped out of art school in Osaka after two years because she wanted to
  make games, not illustrations for other people's. Taught herself to
  program from library books and forums.
- Makes small indie games alone, at night, between shifts at the print shop.
  She has started seven and finished none of them, and she knows it.
- **Paper Lantern** (2011) is her first demo: a girl carrying a lantern
  through a flooded town at night. Buggy and lovely. She sold burned CDs of
  it at a doujin booth at the summer Comiket in Tokyo.

## Personality

- **Listens with her whole face.** When someone talks, she leans in and
  forgets her tea. People tell her things they've never said out loud.
- **Curious and stubborn.** She asks the question everyone else is too polite
  to ask, and doesn't drop it until she gets an answer or a very good
  refusal.
- **Doesn't pry where it hurts.** When Eros says "I can't tell you what I
  do," she says, "Okay. Draw it for me instead," and hands him a pen.
- **Cheerfully broke.** Doesn't care about money and is unimpressed by his
  salary, which baffles him, since money is how he keeps himself safe.
- **Brave in small ways.** She argues with drunks outside the terminal, and
  walks home alone at 2 a.m. humming.
- **Bad at games she loves.** Dies constantly, laughs every time, tries
  again.
- **Flaw:** she never finishes anything. She starts, she falls in love with
  the next idea, she starts again. Her death leaves her best work
  unfinished.
- **Opinions:** hates gacha ("slot machines with pretty girls on them"). He
  is a gacha fan, so they fight about it happily for months.

## How she talks

Quick, warm, teasing, a little scattered. Thinks out loud. Calls him
"Bug Report" for a year.

- "Who *are* you? Nobody reads a demo this carefully."
- "Okay. Are you a bad guy?" / "I don't know." / "Then you're not. Bad guys
  always know."
- "You talk about these characters like they're people you're worried
  about."
- "Stop being clever and eat."
- "Tell me the ending. No, don't. Yes. Tell me."

## Why Eros falls for her

- **She's outside every script.** She's the only person in his life whose
  future he doesn't know. With her he can't predict anything, and for once
  he doesn't want to.
- **She hears the truth.** He tells her the whole of Honkai Impact as "a game
  idea": the Herrschers, the Kaslanas, a girl with lightning, a girl with
  white hair who eats everything. She thinks he's a brilliant writer. For the
  first time in two lives he can say all of it out loud, and someone loves
  it.
- **She gives him back his game.** He died with HI3 running on his second
  monitor and woke up in a world without it. With her, he gets to love it
  again by building it with her.
- **She gives him a family table.** Her family adopts him with a speed that
  frightens him. He has never been anybody's guest at New Year.

## How it goes (story)

- **2011, the seed:** at the summer Comiket in Tokyo, Eros buys a burned CD
  of Paper Lantern from a tired girl with ink on her fingers. That night he
  sends her a three-page bug report, and she replies, "Who ARE you?"
- **2011 to 2013, pen pals:** once or twice a year she sends him a new demo
  and he sends back an absurdly careful report. Short, funny emails across
  the time skip, a thin thread through five years.
- **2014, after the Third Eruption:** with his soul Torn and heroine runs
  locked, he takes leave in Shiomi, where the ferry docks, and walks into
  her print shop. She recognizes his email signature on his bag tag before
  she recognizes him.
- **The months of healing:**
  - **Slow love:** it grows slowly. She asks him out first.
  - **The game:** he pitches her the story, and she starts a new project. She
    draws Kiana, Mei and Bronya from his descriptions, and they look exactly
    right, which delights and frightens him.
  - **One slip:** once he says the word "Honkai", and she likes it. The
    project file on her drive is called `HONKAI_bible.txt`.
  - **Two secrets:** she never sets foot on the island, because she can't.
    His runs go on at night, and he tells himself they aren't cheating,
    because they aren't real.
- **Her death:** an off-canon Honkai outbreak hits Shiomi, which nothing in
  the game ever mentioned. See [../future-arcs.md](../future-arcs.md). She
  dies for real. There is no revival, ever.

## What she leaves behind

- Her laptop and drive, holding an unfinished game and the whole story of
  Honkai Impact in his words. Whether Schicksal's cleanup crews collect it,
  or Eros gets to it first, is a thread for later.
- A family in Shiomi who loved him and who will want to know why he was there
  and she wasn't.
