# Raiden Mei

**Role:** heroine. The first heroine run (Chapter 3). In the real world, the
girl at the center of the Third Eruption. Canon character; story changes
are marked.

**Age (story change):** born April 13, **1995** (canon 1997), so 18 from
April 2013. Third year at Chiba Academy in the 2013 to 2014 school year.

**Appearance (canon):** very long black hair with a blue-violet sheen, to
her thighs, with long strands framing her face and straight bangs (a hime
cut). Half of it tied up in a ponytail with pins. Tall, taller than most
girls and some boys, with a swordswoman's posture. Chiba Academy uniform.

**Background (canon):**
- Born in Nagazora. Only child of Raiden Ryoma, CEO of ME Corp (secretly
  an Anti-Entropy subsidiary). Her mother died of cancer in 1999.
- Strict upbringing, never had a pet. Close to her father, who taught her
  the Raiden clan's Hokushin Itto-ryu swordsmanship. A prodigy.
- Abducted by terrorists in 2004; three years of trauma treatment at an ME
  Corp institution.
- At Chiba Academy, a school famous for rich students, she struggled to
  make real friends; people wanted the Raiden name. One of the few people
  she was close to was her chauffeur, who drove her in a black limousine
  and talked to her every day.
- **November 16, 2013:** her father is arrested for massive fraud (framed by
  Cocolia of Anti-Entropy). Her few friends drop her; others try to use the
  situation. She decides nobody cares about her, only the name, and cuts
  herself off completely until the Third Eruption.

**Personality (canon):** compassionate and gentle, fiercely protective of
people she cares about. Diligent, helps classmates. Intelligent and good at
piecing things together. Her cooking is famously good. Underneath, lonely
and tired of being valued for her name. When hurt, she goes quiet and
polite and shuts the door. Her cold side shows when someone she loves is
threatened.

**How she talks:** soft, polite, precise. Says less than she means. Uses
people's names carefully. When cold, very short.

- "No, Eros. I understood you the first time."
- "Not in front of everyone."
- "Why didn't you give me her message?"
- "Did you forget something?"
- "Ask me after class."

**Habits:** makes her own bento every morning, always slightly too much.
Straightens things on desks. Practices sword forms alone at dawn in the
family dojo. Touches her hair pins when she's nervous.

**The Herrscher inside:** see `herrscher-of-thunder.md`.

**School reputation (deliberate adaptation):** the Lightning Queen of Chiba
Academy, admired and hard to approach, known for rejecting confessions.
The detailed nickname backstory is especially visible in Houkai Gakuen 2
material. This novel adopts it without presenting every version's events
as Honkai Impact 3rd canon.

**Planned Chapter 3, Until Tomorrow (not drafted canon):** Eros enters as
her transfer-student classmate on October 1, 2013. His daily confessions
are rejected before and after her father's arrest. His continued presence
becomes meaningful alongside how he treats her changed circumstances.

Across four loops she encounters his fan fantasy, manufactured dependence,
attempted scripting and eventual cooperation. These are separate branches;
she remembers only her current one. In the final loop she forms different
relationships with Eros and Kiana and retains the right to reject either.
Kiana creates the physical opening during the eruption. After Eros takes
a fatal heart wound for Kiana, Mei participates in a brief kiss and regains
control. The persona yields rather than being erased.

After completion the real Mei can receive echoes of the final loop,
including the loss of someone her real school records do not contain.
