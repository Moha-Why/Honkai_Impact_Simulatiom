# Raiden Mei

**Role:** heroine. The first heroine run (Chapter 3). In the real world, the
girl at the center of the Third Eruption. Canon character; story changes
are marked.

**Age (story change):** born April 13, **1995** (canon 1997), so 18 on
April 13, 2013, the day the adult Eros takes over in every loop. She's the
youngest of the run's girls; the others are born in 1994. Third year, class 3-A, at Chiba Academy in Nagazora in the 2013
to 2014 school year.

**Appearance (canon):** tall (172 cm), taller than most girls and some boys,
with a swordswoman's straight back. Very long dark purple hair with a
blue-violet sheen, to her thighs, straight bangs and long side strands, half
of it tied up with pins. Indigo eyes. Chiba Academy uniform worn exactly
right. When the Herrscher wakes, see
[herrscher-of-thunder.md](herrscher-of-thunder.md).

## Background (canon)

- Only child of Raiden Ryoma, head of ME Corp (secretly tied to
  Anti-Entropy). Her mother died of cancer when Mei was two.
- Her stigmata woke when she was small, and her father sealed it as well as
  he could. Kidnapped by a terrorist group in 2004 (story: age 9), then
  three years of therapy at an ME Corp institution. She doesn't talk about
  it.
- A cushioned life after that. Servants made her lunches, a chauffeur
  (Mr. Tanabe) drove her in a black limousine, and everyone at school
  fussed over her. Her father was often away, but he kept her busy with
  kendo lessons from "Mr. HOMU", a masked teacher in a ridiculous costume.
  She never knew it was him. He taught her that a strong heart is what gets
  you through a fight.
- A prodigy with a sword. Kendo club captain, first place in the regional
  girls' individual in spring 2013.
- **November 16, 2013:** her father is arrested for economic fraud, charged
  with illegal financing under a project that never existed. Cocolia framed
  him to take ME Corp. The assets are frozen, the servants and the car are
  gone, and the house goes quiet.
- **After the arrest:**
  - Nobody will sit with "the criminal's daughter". She works out that the
    friendliness was always for the name.
  - She cooks for herself for the first time, and it's awful.
  - She finds the Mr. HOMU costume in the training room and understands
    that her father was teaching her all along.
- **Then Kiana:** an honest, loud underclassman (story: same year, a
  transfer) who gets past her guard, stands between her and the bullies,
  and loves her terrible lunches with total sincerity. That's how Mei's love
  of cooking starts.

## The Lightning Queen (story choice)

Before the arrest, the school calls her the Lightning Queen (雷电女王, a real
canon alias; tying it to school gossip is from fan sources). She's beautiful,
rich, top of the year and unbeatable with a sword, and she's cool and
untouchable in a polite way. Boys confess to her in a steady stream, by
letter, in the courtyard, behind the gym. She turns every one down, kindly
and finally, and never gossips about who asked. After the arrest the
confessions stop overnight, and the nickname turns into a sneer.

## Personality (canon)

- Gentle and compassionate. Diligent, quick, good at piecing things
  together. She helps classmates without being asked.
- Fiercely protective of the people she loves. Her cold side shows when
  someone she loves is threatened.
- A long habit of neglecting her own feelings. She was lonely at home long
  before the arrest and accepted it silently.
- After the arrest she goes jaded. Still soft-hearted, but the guard goes up
  with everyone, Kiana included at first.
- When hurt, she goes quiet and polite and shuts the door. She doesn't cry
  in front of people.

### Her three faces (story, built on canon and on how Chinese fanfic
writes her)

- **The Lightning Queen (before November 16):** proud and confident, the
  untouchable heiress of the school. Not cruel, but her grades, manners and
  status make other students feel small. Polite and razor-sharp; a
  rejection is a door closed without hurry and without a crack left open.
  The school legend is that on Valentine's Day in her first year she turned
  down twelve boys before lunch (a fan detail, not confirmed in canon; the
  canon alias "Queen of Thunder" comes from her standing at school).
- **The fallen heiress (after November 16):** withdrawn, afraid of contact,
  sure anyone who comes near her wants to mock her or use her. Brief,
  formal, distance-making speech. Inside: "Don't get close to me," "I'm a
  criminal's daughter." She can feel something wrong building in her body
  (a story choice; fanfic writes it this way) and starts to believe she's
  dangerous to anyone near her. Canon: after the Eruption she asks Himeko
  to kill her because the power inside her is too dangerous.
- **The Herrscher (the Eruption):** see
  [herrscher-of-thunder.md](herrscher-of-thunder.md).

## How she talks

Soft, polite, precise. Says less than she means. Uses people's names
carefully. When cold, very short. She counts things when she's flustered.

- "I'm sorry. No." (Her standard rejection: courteous, final.)
- "Thank you. I'm not looking for anything of that kind. Please don't
  spend any more of your time on it." (The long version, before the
  arrest.)
- "Please don't sit here. It's better for you if you don't." (After.)
- "That's forty-one, Eros. You can stop counting. I already am."
- "How do you know that name?"
- "Don't. You'll say you're sorry, and you'll mean it for a week."
- "Eros. Look at me when you lie. At least do that."
- "It's bad. I know it's bad. You don't have to eat it."

## Habits

- Straightens things on desks, including other people's.
- Practices sword forms alone at dawn in the family dojo.
- Touches her hair pins when she's nervous. (One of them is the item Eros
  can carry out of the run.)
- Before the arrest, a servant-made bento in a lacquered box. After, her own
  bento in a plain box, always slightly too much, first burnt, later very
  good.

## The Herrscher inside

See [herrscher-of-thunder.md](herrscher-of-thunder.md). It feels her pain
and her joy. Corruption feeds it.

## Meters in her run

- **Affection** moves on what he actually does: showing up, staying, eating
  what she cooks. Clever lines and game knowledge don't move it. That's why
  manipulation by omission works on her in Loop 4. What he does is real;
  what he lets happen around her is the lie.
- **Trust** drops hard on anything that feels like being studied or used
  (game knowledge, money, being handled).
- **Corruption** rises when she's isolated, used or made dependent. High
  Corruption speeds up the awakening and makes the Herrscher crueler.
  Built from pain he caused, it turns the persona against him. Without
  Trust under it, it turns into possession.

## Hidden objectives (her heroine run)

The System never lists these. At settlement it shows which ones he hit. S
needs at least five of eight, plus In Love and Fallen.

**Love's side**

1. **The last one standing:** after November 16 he's the only one still
   confessing every morning, and she starts waiting for it.
2. **Her father's sword:** she teaches him the Raiden family art.
3. **Her own words:** she tells him she loves him first, while he's alive.
4. **Wake beside him:** both of them alive when the Hyperion arrives.

**Corruption's side**

5. **The Queen's cruelty:** she hurts someone for him and isn't sorry.
6. **Cooking for one:** she cooks only for him; nobody else gets her food,
   Kiana included.
7. **Not Kiana's hand:** on the roof she refuses Kiana's hand and takes his.
8. **Thunder for him:** she uses the Herrscher's power for him.

## In Chapter 3 (Mei run: eight loops)

- **Loop 1:** he mentions her kendo final, her mother and Mr. HOMU. "How do
  you know that name?" Trust Wary.
- **Loop 2:** he's Tanabe's nephew, her friend from six. He tells her father
  about the burst bulb at thirteen. "You promised." Five years of silence.
  In October 2013 he tells her who Mr. HOMU is. "You kept his. I lied to
  him for you with my arm in a cast. You couldn't keep mine for a night."
- **Loop 3:** he's Cocolia's operative, sent to isolate her.
- **Loop 4:** he breaks her down himself. The persona kills him.
- **Loop 5:** barely appears.
- **Loop 6:** lived. The housekeeper's son, Mr. HOMU's other student,
  killed at nine in the car when she's taken. In that branch she grows up
  having lost him.
- **Loop 7:** the dawn dojo. Kiana sent north, Fallen too early, the persona
  kills him to keep him.
- **Loop 8:** lived. The nurse's son at the ME Corp institution, 2004 to
  2007. He teaches her to count until she sleeps (the origin of her habit).
  At twelve she gives him one of her two pins, "So you'll come back," and
  wears one from then on. In September 2013 he comes back. The rest of the
  autumn as before. On the roof the persona puts its hand through his chest;
  he holds up the pin; she comes back through him, pulls the lightning out,
  and breaks down with him in her lap. She won't let go of him to take
  Kiana's hand. She tells him she loves him while he's alive, and kisses
  him. He dies as the Hyperion arrives.
- **Hidden objectives in Loop 8:** 2, 3, 5 and 7 hit; 1, 4, 6 and 8 missed.
- **After the run (real world):** from February 5, 2014 she wears **one
  pin**. She can't remember losing the other. Strong echoes surface at once,
  vivid as memory and known to be false: a children's ward at night, a boy
  teaching her to count, "So you'll come back." Her habit of touching her
  pins becomes touching the gap. On the real roof she hesitates one second
  before taking the real Kiana's hand.
