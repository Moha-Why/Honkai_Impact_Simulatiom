# Raiden Mei

**Role:** heroine. The first heroine run (Chapter 3). In the real world, the
girl at the center of the Third Eruption. Canon character; story changes
are marked.

**Age (story change):** born April 13, **1995** (canon 1997), so 18 from
April 2013. Third year, class 3-A, at Chiba Academy in Nagazora in the 2013
to 2014 school year.

**Appearance (canon):** tall (172 cm), taller than most girls and some boys,
with a swordswoman's straight back. Very long dark purple hair with a
blue-violet sheen, to her thighs, straight bangs and long side strands, half
of it tied up with pins. Indigo eyes. Chiba Academy uniform worn exactly
right. When the Herrscher wakes, see
[herrscher-of-thunder.md](herrscher-of-thunder.md).

## Background (canon)

- Only child of Raiden Ryoma, head of ME Corp (secretly tied to
  Anti-Entropy). Her mother died of cancer when Mei was two.
- Her stigmata woke when she was small, and her father sealed it as well as
  he could. Kidnapped by a terrorist group in 2004 (story: age 9), then
  three years of therapy at an ME Corp institution. She doesn't talk about
  it.
- A cushioned life after that. Servants made her lunches, a chauffeur
  (Mr. Tanabe) drove her in a black limousine, and everyone at school
  fussed over her. Her father was often away, but he kept her busy with
  kendo lessons from "Mr. HOMU", a masked teacher in a ridiculous costume.
  She never knew it was him. He taught her that a strong heart is what gets
  you through a fight.
- A prodigy with a sword. Kendo club captain, first place in the regional
  girls' individual in spring 2013.
- **November 16, 2013:** her father is arrested for economic fraud, charged
  with illegal financing under a project that never existed. Cocolia framed
  him to take ME Corp. The assets are frozen, the servants and the car are
  gone, and the house goes quiet.
- **After the arrest:**
  - Nobody will sit with "the criminal's daughter". She works out that the
    friendliness was always for the name.
  - She cooks for herself for the first time, and it's awful.
  - She finds the Mr. HOMU costume in the training room and understands
    that her father was teaching her all along.
- **Then Kiana:** an honest, loud underclassman (story: same year, a
  transfer) who gets past her guard, stands between her and the bullies,
  and loves her terrible lunches with total sincerity. That's how Mei's love
  of cooking starts.

## The Lightning Queen (story choice)

Before the arrest, the school calls her the Lightning Queen (雷电女王, a real
canon alias; tying it to school gossip is from fan sources). She's beautiful,
rich, top of the year and unbeatable with a sword, and she's cool and
untouchable in a polite way. Boys confess to her in a steady stream, by
letter, in the courtyard, behind the gym. She turns every one down, kindly
and finally, and never gossips about who asked. After the arrest the
confessions stop overnight, and the nickname turns into a sneer.

## Personality (canon)

- Gentle and compassionate. Diligent, quick, good at piecing things
  together. She helps classmates without being asked.
- Fiercely protective of the people she loves. Her cold side shows when
  someone she loves is threatened.
- A long habit of neglecting her own feelings. She was lonely at home long
  before the arrest and accepted it silently.
- After the arrest she goes jaded. Still soft-hearted, but the guard goes up
  with everyone, Kiana included at first.
- When hurt, she goes quiet and polite and shuts the door. She doesn't cry
  in front of people.

## How she talks

Soft, polite, precise. Says less than she means. Uses people's names
carefully. When cold, very short. She counts things when she's flustered.

- "I'm sorry. No." (Her standard rejection, said kindly.)
- "That's forty-one, Eros. You can stop counting. I already am."
- "How do you know that name?"
- "Don't. You'll say you're sorry, and you'll mean it for a week."
- "Eros. Look at me when you lie. At least do that."
- "It's bad. I know it's bad. You don't have to eat it."

## Habits

- Straightens things on desks, including other people's.
- Practices sword forms alone at dawn in the family dojo.
- Touches her hair pins when she's nervous. (One of them is the item Eros
  can carry out of the run.)
- Before the arrest, a servant-made bento in a lacquered box. After, her own
  bento in a plain box, always slightly too much, first burnt, later very
  good.

## The Herrscher inside

See [herrscher-of-thunder.md](herrscher-of-thunder.md). It feels her pain
and her joy. Corruption feeds it.

## Meters in her run

- **Affection** moves on sincerity, not technique. Clever lines and game
  knowledge don't move it; meaning it does. (Eros learns this in Loop 4.)
- **Trust** drops hard on anything that feels like being studied or used
  (game knowledge, money, being handled).
- **Corruption** rises when she's isolated, used or made dependent. High
  Corruption speeds up the awakening and makes the Herrscher crueler.

## In Chapter 3 (Mei run, four loops)

- **Loop 1:** rejects the fanboy daily; unsettled when he knows about her
  mother and Mr. HOMU (Trust: Wary, Affection: Noticed).
- **Loop 2:** isolated and made dependent by Eros. Kisses him in January; the
  kiss fails the objective (Corruption: Compromised). Wakes early as the
  Herrscher and the persona kills him.
- **Loop 3:** gets on the train with him the night before the Eruption. The
  awakening follows her.
- **Loop 4:** rejected confessions turn into the one kind thing anyone says
  to her after November 16. Her first awful bento, shared with Eros and
  Kiana. The Mr. HOMU story, told by her this time. Turns on the roof;
  surfaces in flashes; comes back when he kisses her as he dies, and holds
  him on the roof's edge.
- **After the run:** living-heroine echoes surface in the real Mei at once
  (dreams, déjà vu, a boy who confessed every morning).
