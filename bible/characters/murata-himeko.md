# Murata Himeko

**Role:** St. Freya teacher, A-rank Valkyrie. In the real world, Theresa has
sent her to Nagazora quietly in late January 2014 (Chapter 2). In Chapter 3,
a cameo in the Mei run. Canon character.

**Age:** born June 11, 1987, so 26 in early 2014.

**Appearance (canon):** tall (167 cm), long red hair, orange eyes. In the
field, a crimson coat over black, and a greatsword. Off duty, a flask she
doesn't hide well.

**Background (canon):**

- Daughter of the scientist Murata Ryusuke. Finished high school at 15 and
  studied aerospace at Caltech under Einstein and Welt Yang.
- In 2005 an attack meant for her father and Welt nearly killed her. Her
  father died and left her a farewell letter; Welt vanished. She finished
  her PhD and joined Schicksal to find out why.
- Low Honkai Adaptability and no special talent. She trained her way into
  the Valkyrie Assault Squad at 19, with an artificial stigma that shortens
  her life.
- In 2010 the Assault Squad was destroyed and she was the only survivor
  (Eros read the report in Chapter 2). She became a teacher at St. Freya.
- In canon she responds to the Third Eruption with the Hyperion, fights the
  possessed Mei, and takes Kiana, Mei and Bronya aboard.

**Personality (canon):** a strong, reliable leader who is also a serious
drinker, even while teaching. Brave to the point of reckless when lives are
at stake. Fond of her students.

**How she talks:** blunt, warm, teasing, a teacher's voice when she wants
it.

- "Kid. Whatever you're about to do, don't."
- "Somebody tell me why the readings in this city look like that."

**In Chapter 3:**

- **Loop 4, January:** a red-haired woman asking questions at the station
  and near the school. Eros recognizes her and doesn't speak to her.
- **Loop 4, the end:** the Hyperion's lights come over the roof as Eros
  dies. She arrives too late to see his face clearly.
- She isn't close to him in the run, so she gets no echoes afterward.
