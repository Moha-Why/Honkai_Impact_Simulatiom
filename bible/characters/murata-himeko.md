# Murata Himeko

**Role:** A-rank Valkyrie and St. Freya teacher. The real-world investigator
Theresa quietly sent to Nagazora in Chapter 2. Canon character.

**Personality:** practical, forceful and protective, without treating a
frightened girl as a weapon to dispose of. Can be blunt and impatient.

**In Chapter 3:** her real-world field query reaches the anomaly watch
room after the heroine simulation settles. She has no knowledge of the
System and need not have met Eros to ask whether the branch employs a
person with that name. The new information comes from Mei's unexpected
question about him.
