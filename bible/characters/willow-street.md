# Willow Street Children's Home: minor characters (after 2007)

See also [director-zhou.md](director-zhou.md).

## Auntie Qian (Qian Meifang), the new director

**Role:** took over Willow Street after Director Zhou died in 2007.

**Age:** about 45 in 2010.

**Appearance:** round, quick, reading glasses on a beaded chain, hands
always busy.

**Personality:** practical and harried, warmer than Zhou and less sharp. She
was one of the aunties when Eros was a boy, and still calls him "the little
accountant".

**How she talks:** fast Hangzhou dialect, scolding as affection.

- "A donor sends money every month and writes 'for the bowls.' Can you
  imagine? We bought a stove. We bought radiators."
- "You're too thin. Germany doesn't feed you?"

**What she doesn't know:** that the donor is him.

**In Chapter 2:** Qingming visits in 2010 and 2012.

## Lin Xiaoyu

**Role:** a boy at Willow Street. Minor.

**Age:** 9 in 2012.

**Appearance:** small, cowlick, too-big school uniform.

**Personality:** quiet, does sums in his head, gets bored in class. Eros
sees himself at that age.

**In Chapter 2 (2012):** Eros gives him a laptop and his email address, and
quietly pays for a tutor. After that, a short email from Xiaoyu once a
month, usually a math problem.
