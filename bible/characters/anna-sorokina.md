# Major Anna Sorokina

**Role:** commander of Outpost 7, Siberia, in Run 1. Minor.

**Age:** 51 in 1999. Russian.

**Appearance:** tall, thin, gray hair pinned flat. Always in a full
uniform, buttoned to the throat, even at three in the morning.

**Personality:** strict, tired and correct. Follows orders exactly because
the alternative is worse. Not unkind, just finished with arguing.

**How she talks:** clipped, formal, no wasted words.

- "Request denied. Next."
- "Your contract is two years, Technician. You will serve two years."

**In Chapter 1 (Run 1):** denies Eros's transfer request. Appears mostly in
log lines.
