# The Herrscher of Thunder (the 3rd Herrscher)

**Role:** the Herrscher core (the Gem of Conquest) inside Mei, and the
persona that wakes with it. Appears in Chapter 3 (Mei run) in Loops 2, 3
and 4.

## Canon

- Mei's stigmata was the Gem of Conquest, sealed by her father. In February
  2014, Cocolia and Bronya set off an artificial outbreak at Chiba Academy
  to wake it. The burst of Honkai energy turns almost everyone in the
  school and city into zombies.
- A second personality takes over Mei: one that hates humanity and is far
  more violent than she is. Kiana, immune, fights it on the roof and beats
  it back. When Mei wakes and sees what she did, she tries to jump from the
  roof. Kiana holds out her hand and won't stop holding it out.
- The persona feels Mei's pain **and her joy**, including what she feels for
  Kiana. It's touched when Kiana catches Mei at the roof's edge, then
  frightened of hurting her, and it hides inside Mei to watch Kiana in
  secret.
- In the manga's escape, Kiana takes a fatal blow for Mei and the persona
  takes over. It won't let Kiana die there because it wants to kill her
  itself. It keeps up a mask of aggression and never admits it cares.

## Appearance when it takes over

Mei's body. Eyes with hot pink pupils, two dark red horns, hair grown much
longer and lifting in the static like a curtain. A single half-wing of
lightning at her back. Lightning crawls over her skin and the roof. The air
smells of ozone and burnt metal.

## Personality (story version, built on canon)

- **Regal and contemptuous.** People are insects, scum, beneath notice.
- **Possessive of Mei.** It sees itself as the part of her that doesn't need
  anyone. "She's mine. She was always mine. You only rented her."
- **Fascinated by Kiana.** Kiana is the only thing on the roof worth
  breaking, so it wants to break her itself, slowly, and won't let anything
  else have her.
- **It knows everything Mei felt,** and uses it. It knows about every
  confession, and despises Eros for how much Mei came to wait for them.
- **Its weakness is that it feels her joy.** In canon, a moment of real
  happiness in Mei breaks its hold. When her joy is Eros (Loop 4), it feeds
  the persona instead.
- **Corruption shapes it.** Built from pain he caused, it hates him (Loop 2).
  Devotion without trust makes it possessive, and it keeps things by
  breaking them (Loop 3). Devotion with trust makes it agree with Mei for
  the first time (Loop 4).

## How it talks

Mei's voice, lower and slower, every word deliberate. Never shouts. Uses
names like insults.

- "You're still standing. How interesting. Insects usually kneel."
- "Surrender to me." (臣服于我, the line Chinese fanfic loves her for.)
- "You think thunder bows to something like you? Know your place."
- "She waited for you every morning. It was disgusting."
- "She doesn't need you. She has me."
- "Come closer, Kaslana. I want to see your face when it breaks."
- To Eros in Loop 2: "You. You're the one who made her small."
- Loop 3, to Eros: "Everything dies tonight. I'm not letting it have you."
- Its last line in Loop 4, at dawn: "She'll find you."

## How fanfic writes her

Chinese fanfic (trxs.cc and similar) plays her as arrogant royalty who
enjoys crushing what's beneath her, and who still, through Mei's link,
caves instantly to protect Kiana while insisting she's only protecting her
vessel. In Loop 4 that protectiveness points at Eros.

## Power

Herrscher-level: lightning, electromagnetic blasts, the half-wing, speed
beyond anything human. Its lightning is a **soul attack**, so a death to it
costs Eros a large chunk of soul and drops him a whole stage
(see [../soul-and-fate.md](../soul-and-fate.md)).

## In Chapter 3

- **Loop 4:** wakes a week early, fed by Corruption built from pain. Goes
  through Kiana's guard to kill Eros. A soul attack; he drops to Worn.
- **Loop 7:** wakes three weeks early with no Kiana in the city and takes Mei
  completely. Kills Eros itself so the end of the world can't have him. He
  drops to Torn.
- **Loop 8:** wakes on the fixed date, possessive of Mei and hating what she
  needs ("She's mine. You only rented her."). Turns from Kiana and puts its
  hand through the right side of his chest. When he holds up the pin, Mei
  surfaces under it and pulls the lightning out of him, so the wound stays a
  wound and not a soul attack.
