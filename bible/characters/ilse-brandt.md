# Sergeant Ilse Brandt

**Role:** Schicksal infantry instructor at a training camp in Germany,
1996, in Run 4. Minor.

**Age:** 39 in 1996. German.

**Appearance:** tall, cropped blond hair, a scar across the bridge of her
nose, a whistle on a cord she never uses because her voice is louder.

**Personality:** harsh, fair, and honest about what kills people. Not a
Valkyrie; a career soldier who has buried a lot of recruits.

**How she talks:** loud, short, never repeats herself.

- "Good hands. Slow feet. That combination dies young."
- "Your technique writes cheques your legs can't cash, recruit."

**In Chapter 2 (Run 4):** trains Eros; warns him that his skill is ahead of
his body.
