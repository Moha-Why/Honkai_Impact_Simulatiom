# Halina Nowak

**Role:** night-shift nurse at a hospital in Krakow, 1987, in Run 2. The
only person Eros gets to know in that run.

**Age:** 47 in 1987. Polish.

**Appearance:** stocky, gray-blond hair under a nurse's cap, reading glasses
on a chain, a smoker's voice. Comfortable shoes.

**Personality:** tough, practical, gallows humor. Twenty years of night
shifts have made her hard to frighten, and when she is frightened she gets
angry instead.

**How she talks:** blunt, quick, swears in Polish under her breath.

- "If the dead start walking, orderly, you push the trolley faster."
- "Hold the door. Hold it! I don't care what's on the other side."

**In Chapter 2 (Run 2):** works the night shift with Eros; barricades the
ward with him during the outbreak.
