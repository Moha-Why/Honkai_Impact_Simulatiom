# Cocolia

**Role:** off-stage operator behind Ryoma's framing and the artificial
Third Eruption. Active antagonist in the period of the planned Mei run,
not her later detained self.

**Source basis:** Anti-Entropy executor, rival of Ryoma, eventual controller
of ME Corp and caretaker of the orphanage associated with Bronya and
Seele. See [../research/ch03-mei-chiba.md](../research/ch03-mei-chiba.md)
for sources and continuity distinctions.

**In the period covered:** removes Ryoma through fabricated economic
charges and uses the resulting access and vulnerability. Her artificial
Honkai event is intended to awaken Mei's power associated with the Gem of
Conquest. Mei's distress is relevant but not a substitute for this
deliberate external operation.

**What is fixed for this novel:** the Gem is already within Mei during
the school period. The chapter does not invent a verified implantation
date from incompatible older summaries.

**How she acts in the plan:** through local staff, work orders, equipment
and recovery instructions. She need not personally watch Eros confess
or command every school bully. She can detect interference with an
operation and change its execution without knowing about time loops.

**Loop 3 adaptation:** the changed delivery and earlier backup release
are original AU logistics, seeded before they matter. They are not a
claim that canon supplies those exact devices or dates.

**Limits:** not omniscient, not able to read the System, and not the sole
voice of all Anti-Entropy. Bronya's recovery assignment supplies later
pressure; it does not need to interrupt the author's final rooftop scene.
