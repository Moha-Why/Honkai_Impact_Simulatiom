# Cocolia

**Role:** the hidden enemy of the Mei run. Mostly offscreen in Chapter 3:
seen on television, felt through her people. Canon character.

**Age:** forties.

**Appearance (canon):** tall (168 cm), wavy blonde hair, purple eyes, black
eyeliner and pale pink lipstick, egg-shaped earrings that match her eyes. A
green dress uniform with red trim and gold buttons, a black belt, long brown
heeled boots, a white cape lined in red. Looks like a general even in a
boardroom.

**Background (canon):**

- A Russian army officer (a captain in 2000). At a Siberian supply depot she
  caught a colonel, Alexei Zaychik, selling army weapons, refused to keep
  quiet, and was jailed on his word.
- The Second Eruption wrecked the jail. Alexei died begging her to save his
  wife. She took his car, found the wife, held a gun on her to keep her
  quiet, and drove until the beasts caught them. Cecilia Schariac saved
  them both. That night tied Cocolia's life to the Honkai.
- Left the army and, with Anti-Entropy's help, opened an orphanage for
  children the Honkai left behind. She doesn't kill children.
- In January 2012 a child sniper sent to assassinate her, Bronya, failed.
  Cocolia took her home instead. Seele Vollerei was lost in the Sea of
  Quanta during one of her experiments, and getting Seele back is the thing
  she wants most.
- Leader of Anti-Entropy's radical wing.

**Against the Raidens (canon):**

- Framed Raiden Ryoma for economic fraud (illegal financing under a project
  that never existed), and he was arrested on November 16, 2013. She then
  took control of ME Corp herself and set up an operation in Nagazora aimed
  at Mei.
- In February 2014, to get Seele back, she and Bronya set off an artificial
  Honkai outbreak at Chiba Academy to wake the Gem of Conquest inside Mei:
  the Third Eruption.
- Older Guns GirlZ lore adds "Project Mei", in which she led Ryoma into
  putting the Gem in his daughter. Eros half-remembers this; treat it as a
  rumor, not confirmed.

**Personality (canon):** iron discipline, political skill, a soldier's
directness. Truly loves her orphans in her own way and will still use them.
Believes the ends are the point.

**How she talks:** level and commanding, warm only with her daughters.

- To the cameras: "ME Corp will honor every contract. Nothing about our
  mission has changed."
- To Bronya: "Bring her to me, Bronya. Gently, if you can."

**In Chapter 3:**

- Loop 1: Eros names her to a journalist and dies for it.
- Every loop: in December she appears on television as the new head of ME
  Corp, and Eros recognizes her instantly.
- Loop 3: her orders put Bronya on the train.
- She never meets Eros in person.
