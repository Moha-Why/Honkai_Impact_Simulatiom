# Asami Kudo

**Role:** D-rank Valkyrie, logistics. Medical and transport. Escort and
intake for new recruits. Not a heroine.

**Age:** 25 in 2009. Japanese.

**Appearance:** short black bob, tired eyes, a small scar through one
eyebrow. Wears a hoodie over her uniform whenever she can get away with it.
Always carries a long hard case (two pistols and a med kit).

**Personality:** chatty, irreverent and bored by escort duty. Treats new
recruits like luggage. Has seen enough of the front line from the back to
have no illusions about it, and jokes about it instead.

**How she talks:** casual, teasing, short. Calls Eros "genius" and doesn't
mean it as a compliment.

- "Lunch." (about the case)
- "Normal for a guy. Don't take it personally."
- "Most people ask questions first, genius."

**Habits:** chews gum constantly. Falls asleep anywhere, instantly, on any
vehicle.

**What she knows:** almost nothing about Eros beyond "new tech recruit".

**What she wants:** her shift to end.

**Later:** a friendly face at the branch, and his window into ordinary
Valkyrie life: the D and C ranks nobody writes songs about.

**By 2014:** 30. Eros's friend; drags him to the harbor bar once a month.
Still D-rank; turned down C twice because C means combat support. "I've
seen what comes back. I fly them back. I'm fine where I am."

**In Chapter 2:** at the harbor bar on January 16 tells him he looks like
the girls she flies back from deployments ("Sleep. Eat something hot. Don't
be alone in the dark too much."); catches him with a knife on the seawall,
doesn't believe "YouTube", and fixes his stance.

**In Chapter 1:** escort during the draft; tells him what D-rank logistics
means on the plane; runs his Honkai Adaptability test.

**In the Chapter 2 rewrite:** gives him the island tour on his second day
(2009, "never order the squid on Mondays"); crashes his Spring Festival
dumplings with two logistics girls (2010); finds his "birthday" in his file
and throws him a party at the harbor bar (2012); chooses his furniture when
he moves into the sea-view flat (2012); notices he's been "somewhere fancy"
after HQ (2013).
