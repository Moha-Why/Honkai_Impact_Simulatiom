# Director Zhou Lan

**Role:** director of the Willow Street Children's Home in Hangzhou. The
closest thing Eros has to a parent in this life. Dies in 2007.

**Age:** 60 in 1991, 76 when she dies.

**Appearance:** small and straight-backed. Gray hair cut short so she never
has to think about it. Reading glasses on a cord around her neck. Cardigans
with the elbows patched by hand.

**Background:** a middle school math teacher for twenty years before she
took over the home. Runs it on almost no money, with thirty-odd children and
one stove.

**Personality:** strict, practical and dry. Doesn't praise anyone directly
and doesn't treat Eros as special, even after she knows what he can do. He
still washes bowls. Fair to the point of being cold, and very hard to fool.
Underneath, she cares more than she ever shows, and it only comes out in
what she does (she kept the note for sixteen years).

**How she talks:** short sentences, often with a number in them. Scolds
with facts. Never raises her voice.

- "Thirty-one children. One stove. Wash your bowl."
- "It's what was written. I don't change what's written."
- "Good. Again."

**Habits:** keeps the accounts by hand in a ledger and checks them twice.
Drinks tea from an old glass jar. Taps her glasses on the desk when she's
thinking.

**What she knows:** that Eros is far too smart and hides it badly. She
never asks him why.

**In Chapter 1:** registers his name from the note; catches him doing her
accounts in his head and gets him into school; tells him to finish his
porridge when he gives her investment advice at seven; gives him the
envelope with the note from her hospital bed in 2007.
