# Welt Yang

**Role (canon):** the second Welt. Born Joachim Nokianvirtanen in Finland.
Leader (Sovereign) of Anti-Entropy and the First Herrscher, the Herrscher
of Reason. From St. Freya's founding he is also its history teacher, hiding
in plain sight. Important, recurring.

**Age:** born 1947 (8 at the 1955 Thanksgiving Incident, 53 in 2000), so
63 in 2010. His body is the one he rebuilt with Reason after the Second
Eruption, and it looks about forty.

**Canon background:**
- As a boy he idolized Welt Joyce, the first Herrscher of Reason. In 1955
  Joyce, dying after fighting Schicksal, gave him the Core of Reason, the
  name "Welt", and the duty to protect the world.
- In the Second Eruption (2000) he fought Sirin, who tore the core out of
  him on the moon; he survived inside it, helped Siegfried break Sirin's
  dream world, and at the end spent the last of his energy shielding the
  wounded Siegfried. His consciousness slept in the core, which Siegfried
  sent back to Einstein. Rebuilding a body took him a long time.
- Taught for a while at Caltech, where Murata Himeko was his student
  (2005).
- After Theresa founded St. Freya he became its history teacher under his
  own name and face (Moegirl: 以历史老师的身份潜伏在学园中). Theresa met him
  during the Second Eruption but doesn't recognize him: Otto used a copy of
  Fenghuang Down to rewrite her memories and erased Anti-Entropy from the
  records of the Eruption.
- Later canon: gives Kiana a zero in history; in the "Anti-Entropy
  Invasion" arc kills a Welt Joyce clone in the school's woods, then coughs
  blood and hints to Einstein he doesn't have long (he's wrong). Eventually
  passes the Core of Reason and his mission to Bronya.

**Why he's at St. Freya (our ruling):** canon never says. In this story he
is recovering. The rebuilt body is still fragile, Otto rarely looks closely
at Theresa's school, and the principal can't remember him. It also lets him
watch Schicksal's Far East from inside, and he likes teaching.

**Appearance (2010):** tall and broad-shouldered, more muscle than a
history teacher needs. Short brown hair, neatly combed, a little gray at
the temples. Brown eyes behind rectangular glasses. A long dark coat over a
waistcoat and tie, and a scarf even indoors in autumn. Moves carefully, a
man who's been badly hurt and remembers it; sits down slowly. Neat chalk
handwriting.

**Personality:** gentle, patient and serious, with a quiet, dry humor. A
natural father figure, protective of children. Carries enormous grief
(Joyce, the Second Eruption) without showing it. Principled; he hates
Otto, but he would never use a classroom for that. Strict about facts.

**How he talks:** calm, warm, unhurried, every sentence finished. Different
from Ōta (questions) and Theresa (bright and bossy).

- **Dates and facts first, then the people.** "February 2000. Siberia.
  Write the date down. Now let's talk about who was there."
- **Calls students by their family names** with "Miss" in class.
- **Never raises his voice.** When a class gets noisy he simply stops and
  waits, hands folded on the desk, until it's quiet.
- **Dry understatement.** "History is mostly people being brave at the
  wrong time."
- **Grades hard and says so kindly.** "Zero isn't a punishment, Miss
  Kaslana. It's a measurement."
- When something touches the Second Eruption he goes very still and
  answers in fewer words.

**Habits:** a cup of black tea on the desk he forgets to drink. Rubs his
left side under the coat when he thinks nobody is looking. Does sit-ups
in the staff room before class (a canon joke).

**The girls:** he's handsome and, apart from the odd visiting engineer,
the only man in the school, so the students adore him. History has the best
attendance and the best marks at St. Freya; girls redo their hair before
his class and draw his initials in hearts in their margins. He appears not
to notice and never encourages it; it stays one-sided and comic (see
[content-rules.md](../content-rules.md)). Theresa works it out in 2010
("Ah. That's why.").

**What he knows about Eros:** nothing, in 2010. He notices a young engineer
at the back who looked at him for a second too long, as if he'd seen him
before.

**In Chapter 2 (September 2010):** teaches the first-year history class
Eros sits in on, with Theresa watching from the back. When a girl asks
about the S-rank who died in Siberia, he looks to the principal, and
Theresa answers.

**Later:** after 2014, gives Kiana her zero. A dangerous, careful presence
for Eros: Anti-Entropy's leader inside Theresa's school, who might one day
notice the engineer knows too much.
