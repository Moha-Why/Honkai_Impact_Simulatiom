# Sin Mal

**Role:** minor. A girl at Cocolia's orphanage in Loop 3's childhood log.
Canon character (the Azure Waters manga).

**Canon:** an orphan in Cocolia's house by Lake Baikal. Jealous of how
close Bronya and Seele are, she breaks Bronya's legs. Seele's dark side
pushes her down a flight of stairs in return.

**Age (story):** born 1997. She's 15 in 2012, her only year on the page,
and always under 18. She appears only in the childhood log, never with the
adult Eros, and is never described in the world's fashion.

**In Chapter 3 (Loop 3):** Seele's shadow since they were small. In autumn
2012 she stops speaking to Seele and watches Bronya and Seele on the
landing. In November she waits at the top of the cellar stairs and Bronya
falls the whole flight (both legs, the left one twice). The boy sees her
waiting and says nothing, because Eros wants Bronya's legs gone before
August, as in canon. A week later she falls down the same stairs with
Seele standing at the top, eyes the wrong color. In December she's sent to
a house in Irkutsk with her arm in a cast and doesn't come back.
