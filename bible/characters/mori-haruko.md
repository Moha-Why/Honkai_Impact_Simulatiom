# Mori Haruko

**Role:** Eros's mother in Loop 6 of Mei's run only. Minor. (No relation to
Dr. Mori on the island.)

**Age:** forty in 2004.

**Appearance:** broad, strong arms, an apron over everything.

**Background:** the Raidens' housekeeper since before Mei was born. Lives
with her son in the staff cottage at the back of the estate. Her son is
raised beside Mei, and Mr. HOMU teaches both children the sword from 2001.

**Personality:** brisk, devoted to the family, frightened of Raiden Ryoma's
silences.

**How she talks:** "Shoes off. Hands washed. Young lady first."

**In Chapter 3:** her son dies on the road in 2004, in the car with Mei and
Mr. Tanabe when the girl is taken.
