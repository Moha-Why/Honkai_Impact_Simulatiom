# Theresa Apocalypse

**Role:** S-rank Valkyrie. Principal of St. Freya and head of the Far East
Branch from June 2009. Canon character; details below follow the official
wiki unless marked as a story change.

**Origin (canon):** A-310, one of Otto's A-3 clones from 1972, made from
Kallen Kaslana's genes plus the Emperor-class beast Vishnu's. The only one
of her batch who passed Otto's tests; she spared her rival, A-303. Otto named
her Theresa and adopted her as his granddaughter. Vishnu's genes give her
superhuman strength and fast regeneration.

**Age:** created 1972, so 37 in 2009 and 41 in 2014.

**Appearance (story change):** in canon her body stopped at about twelve.
In this story she stopped growing as a **petite adult**: very short (about a
head and a half shorter than Eros), with an adult face. Otherwise canon:
waist-length white hair, usually in a low side ponytail, bright blue eyes,
cute, frilly clothes, a little nun-like.

**Rank and history (canon):**
- B-rank in 1973, when she killed the Emperor-class Parvati alone on her
  first combat mission. The lowest-ranked Paladin (Overseer's Guard) ever.
- S-rank by the Second Eruption (2000). Fought Sirin there with her Divine
  Key, Judah's Oath, whose hidden 0th ability nullifies Honkai energy and
  can kill its user.
- Best friend of Cecilia Schariac, and calls herself Kiana's aunt.

**Tension with Otto (canon and user ruling):** after the Second Eruption she
learned how Cecilia died and turned on Otto. She tried to get Kiana back
from him with Siegfried and failed. Because Otto holds "Kiana", she can't
leave Schicksal, so on June 21, 2009 she transferred to the Far East Branch
and founded St. Freya, on a reclaimed island Otto gave her. In 2009 to 2014
she and her grandfather are at odds, and she doesn't trust anything sent
from HQ.

**Personality:** kind, protective and stubborn. Will do anything for her
students' safety. A very capable principal, with a fondness for childish
things: sweets, HOMU merchandise, manga she pretends she doesn't read.
Calls herself the World's Cutest Valkyrie and uses her looks to her
advantage. Hates being called small. Afraid of ghosts. Loves bitter melon
juice.

**How she talks:** bright and bossy, quick to puff up, quick to forgive.
Refers to herself as Teri sometimes. Insults enemies in cute ways.

- "Who wrote this? I want a name."
- "I'm the principal. I'm allowed to be in here."
- "The World's Cutest Valkyrie says thank you. You may tell people."
- "Grandfather's people sent me your file. I don't read files from HQ
  anymore."

**Habits:** keeps sweets in her pockets and hands them out. Walks very fast
for someone with short legs.

**What she knows about Eros:** after Chapter 2, his name, his face, and that
he fixed her students' battlesuit bug in ten minutes.

**In Chapter 2:** on January 15, 2014 she visits the anomaly watch room,
remembers him ("The weather data boy!"), hears the Nagazora data, decides
to send Himeko quietly without telling HQ, and orders him to eat a lemon
candy. Earlier, she storms onto the eleventh floor in summer 2009 over a
training-battlesuit bug that hurt one of her first students, and meets
Eros when he fixes it.
