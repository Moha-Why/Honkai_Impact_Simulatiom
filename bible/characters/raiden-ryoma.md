# Raiden Ryoma (Mr. HOMU)

**Role:** Mei's father. In Chapter 3, mostly through Mei's memories and the
news; Eros tries to reach him in Loop 1. Canon character.

**Appearance (canon):** purple hair, short at the sides with a waist-length
ponytail, violet eyes. Dark suit and overcoat, yellow shirt, purple tie.

**Background (canon):**

- Head of ME Corp, a weapons and electronics maker that works with
  Anti-Entropy, and an Anti-Entropy scientist himself. Reveres Welt Yang;
  distrusts Siegfried Kaslana.
- His wife died of cancer when Mei was two. He sealed Mei's stigmata as well
  as he could after it woke.
- Too busy to be the father he wanted to be, so he taught Mei the family
  kenjutsu himself, in disguise, as "Mr. HOMU", in a full Homu mascot suit (yellow, rabbit-shaped, long ears). She never knew.
  He taught her that a strong heart is what gets you through a fight.
- Arrested November 16, 2013, for economic fraud, framed by Cocolia. Held
  without bail after that.
- Older Guns GirlZ lore says Cocolia led him into putting the Gem in his
  daughter (treat as rumor).

**Personality (canon):** calm, rational, serious about his work. A family
man who loves his daughter and is strict with her.

**How he talks:** measured and formal. "Mei. Again. Your feet first, then
the blade."

**In Chapter 3:**

- **Loop 1:** in May 2003 he answers an eight-year-old's letter from Malmö
  addressed to "the boss of the glass tower" ("I have a daughter your age.
  She is learning kendo, and she is better at it than I was. I hope your
  cat comes home."). In November 2013 the secretary puts the grown boy
  through because of it. Ryoma listens to him name Cocolia and says "Thank
  you. Don't call this number again." The arrest happens anyway.
- **Loop 2:** thanks the chauffeur's nephew for telling him about the
  burst bulb, and in 2010 pays his Chiba Academy fees "for October." Is
  Mr. HOMU; the boy knows from the shoes.
- **Loop 4:** in 2003 interviews the port home's clever boys for the ME
  Corp foundation, in a plain suit, and sees that Eros failed on purpose.
  Sets up the 2012 scholarship.
- **Loop 8:** in 2005 the nurse's son asks why he's always tired.
  "Because I'm keeping something asleep." On November 16, 2013, being led
  out, he sees the boy beside her in the dojo and is relieved. In December
  he writes to her from detention: "Keep him close, Mei. Let him."
- **Every loop:** the arrest on the news on November 16 (a fixed node).
- **Loop 6:** teaches two children the sword as Mr. HOMU, his daughter and the housekeeper's boy.
- **Loop 8:** Mei finds the Mr. HOMU costume in January, tells Eros, and
  teaches him the family sword because she has no one else to give it to.
