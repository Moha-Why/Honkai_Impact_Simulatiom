# Raiden Ryoma (Mr. HOMU)

**Role:** Mei's father. In Chapter 3, mostly through Mei's memories and the
news; Eros tries to reach him in Loop 1. Canon character.

**Appearance (canon):** purple hair, short at the sides with a waist-length
ponytail, violet eyes. Dark suit and overcoat, yellow shirt, purple tie.

**Background (canon):**

- Head of ME Corp, a weapons and electronics maker that works with
  Anti-Entropy, and an Anti-Entropy scientist himself. Reveres Welt Yang;
  distrusts Siegfried Kaslana.
- His wife died of cancer when Mei was two. He sealed Mei's stigmata as well
  as he could after it woke.
- Too busy to be the father he wanted to be, so he taught Mei the family
  kenjutsu himself, in disguise, as the masked "Mr. HOMU". She never knew.
  He taught her that a strong heart is what gets you through a fight.
- Arrested November 16, 2013, for economic fraud, framed by Cocolia. Held
  without bail after that.
- Older Guns GirlZ lore says Cocolia led him into putting the Gem in his
  daughter (treat as rumor).

**Personality (canon):** calm, rational, serious about his work. A family
man who loves his daughter and is strict with her.

**How he talks:** measured and formal. "Mei. Again. Your feet first, then
the blade."

**In Chapter 3:**

- **Loop 1:** Eros calls his office and writes to him trying to warn him
  about Cocolia. It goes nowhere; the arrest happens anyway.
- **Every loop:** the arrest on the news on November 16 (a fixed node).
- **Loop 4:** Mei tells Eros about Mr. HOMU in January, after she finds the
  costume, and this time he lets her tell him instead of already knowing.
