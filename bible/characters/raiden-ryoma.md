# Raiden Ryoma

**Role:** Mei's father, ME Corp executive and Anti-Entropy member. His
November 16, 2013 arrest is the public event at the center of Chapter 3's
school reversal. Cocolia frames him; these fraud charges are not proof
of his guilt.

**In the chapter:** appears through news, Mei's recollections and indirect
contact. Does not know Eros is reincarnated or that simulations exist.
His arrest and the company's resources provide conditions Cocolia uses.

**Limits:** the chapter does not invent a resolution to his case or give
Eros access to all his private research. The Gem is already in Mei by
this school period; incompatible implantation dates are left outside
this chapter's claims.
