# Hamada Tetsuo

**Role:** senior field technician at the Far East Branch technical center.
He looks after the "birds", the island's and the region's sensor masts and
Honkai energy detectors. Minor, recurring from 2011.

**Age:** 60 in 2011. Japanese, from a harbor town in Hiroshima.

**Appearance:** small, brown and deeply creased from forty years outdoors.
A gray rain jacket with the hood always half up. An unlit cigarette behind
his right ear, always the same brand, never smoked indoors (and, people
suspect, never smoked at all). Thick fingers that can still thread a
connector in the rain.

**Background:** worked on dredgers for twenty years. He was on the crew
that pumped up the technical center's island in 1998 to 1999, and on the
one that built St. Freya's island two hours south in 2006 to 2008. He
stayed on after the first one as a technician because he liked the work
and had nobody waiting for him on the mainland. Widower; his wife died in
1994. No children.

**Personality:** patient, stubborn and unhurried. Treats machines like
animals: they get sick, they get hungry, things nest in them. Has no
interest in what Schicksal really does and makes a point of not asking. He
notices everything outdoors and nothing indoors.

**How he talks:** gravelly, slow, in short statements, as if reading out a
weather report. In detail:

- **Calls the detectors "the birds"** and talks about them as if they were
  alive. "That one's deaf." "That one's been dead since 2007. Nobody asked."
- **Never agrees that he was wrong.** If it's a crab and he said birds, the
  crab is a bird.
- **Answers questions about Schicksal with the weather.** "What do you think
  they're for?" "Rain by four."
- **Predicts the next problem as a joke** ("The one at the north end's got
  an eel in it, probably") and is wrong in a better way.
- Lets his enka radio finish a line before he answers anything.

**Habits:** drives a small electric cart with a toolbox in the back and a
radio that only gets an enka station. Takes the cigarette out, looks at it,
puts it back when he's thinking. Puts every animal he finds in a detector
back where it came from.

**What he wants:** for the birds to work, and to be left alone with them.

**In Chapter 2 (2011):** drives Eros round the 43 dead detectors for the
anomaly watch; the crab, the wasps' nest; tells him about the dredger and
the "present" for a rich man's granddaughter.
