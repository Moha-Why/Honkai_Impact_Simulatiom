# Hoshino Kei

**Role:** a freelance business reporter in Nagazora. Loop 1 of Mei's run
only. Minor.

**Age:** thirty-four.

**Appearance:** short hair, a man's raincoat a size too big, ink on her
fingers, a phone that's always on two percent.

**Background:** covers ME Corp for a business weekly. Has been told twice by
her editor to drop anything about the Raidens.

**Personality:** sharp and tired. Interested in anything that smells like a
cover-up, and not brave enough to be stupid about it.

**How she talks:** fast and dry.

- "Who gave you this?"
- "Kids don't bring me folders. Kids bring me nothing, that's what's nice
  about kids."
- "If this is real, you shouldn't have given it to me. If it isn't, you
  shouldn't have given it to me."

**In Chapter 3:** in November Eros hands her a folder that names Cocolia.
She takes it. A week later she stops answering her phone, and the story
never says why.
