# Kiana Kaslana

**Role:** future heroine and the combat lead in Chapter 3's Mei run.
Romantic competition from Eros's perspective, with her own affection for
Mei, choices and developing friendship with him. Canon character; story
changes are marked.

**Age (story change):** born December 7, **1995** (canon 1998), so 18 from
December 7, 2013. In this story she enrolls at Chiba Academy in mid
December 2013, after her birthday.

**Appearance (canon):** slender, white hair, blue eyes, average height. Hair
usually in braids, with a cowlick (ahoge) sticking up on top. Fights with
dual pistols (not at school).

**Who she really is (canon, unknown to her):** K-423, a clone made by Otto,
with the damaged core of Sirin, the Herrscher of the Void, inside her. She
believes she is Siegfried's daughter and is looking for him. Her hidden
core makes her immune to Honkai corruption. (The K-423 timeline with the
1995 birth is still an open question in the bible.)

**Personality (canon):** spunky, lighthearted, honest to a fault, says
whatever is on her mind without thinking. Hungry all the time. Bad at
written tests, brilliant in a fight. Brave and kind. Openly drawn to Mei.

**How she talks:** loud, fast, cheerful, lots of exclamations. Announces
herself.

- "I'm Kiana Kaslana! I'm going to be the strongest Valkyrie in the world!"
- "Are you going to eat that? You're not. I can tell."
- "You're poor too? Finally, someone normal at this school!"

**Habits:** eats anything offered, and some things that aren't. Falls
asleep in class. Talks about her father as if he'll turn up any day.

**Planned Chapter 3 (not drafted canon):** arrives at Chiba Academy on
December 16, 2013, already eighteen. Befriends Mei without sharing the
school's treatment of her as disgraced. Competes and argues with Eros
without accepting that either of them owns Mei's answer.

She is obstructed by Eros in loop 2 and used too much as a tactical piece
in loop 3. In the final loop she becomes his necessary ally and someone
he wants alive. She does the fighting, refuses to kill Mei and opens the
brief opportunity for contact. Eros intercepts a physical attack intended
for her while she is committed to holding that opening.

She supports him after the wound and continues containing the danger;
the story does not replace her contribution with his kiss. Fainter echoes
from the successful final loop may reach the real Kiana afterward. No
discarded-loop memories or controlled Void powers are granted.
