# Kiana Kaslana

**Role:** future heroine. Appears in the Mei run (Chapter 3). Canon
character; story changes are marked.

**Age (story change):** born January 7, **1995** (canon December 7, 1998),
so 18 from January 7, 2013, before the adult Eros arrives in the Mei run
(April 13, 2013). Same year as Mei; she's 18 when she enrolls at Chiba
Academy in mid December 2013. By the Japanese school year she'd belong in
the year above, but her papers are forged and nobody checks.

**Appearance (canon):** slender, white hair, blue eyes, average height. Hair
usually in braids, with a cowlick (ahoge) sticking up on top. Fights with
dual pistols (not at school).

**Who she really is (canon, unknown to her):** K-423, a clone made by Otto from the real Kiana Kaslana (who lives as
Bianka "Durandal" Ataegina, see bianka-durandal.md),
with the damaged core of Sirin, the Herrscher of the Void, inside her. She
believes she is Siegfried's daughter and is looking for him. Her hidden
core makes her immune to Honkai corruption. (The K-423 timeline with the
January 1995 birth is still an open question in the bible.)

**Personality (canon):** spunky, lighthearted, honest to a fault, says
whatever is on her mind without thinking. Hungry all the time. Bad at
written tests, brilliant in a fight. Brave and kind. Openly drawn to Mei.

**How she talks (voice sheet):** loud, fast, boyish, very casual. No
polite forms unless she's mocking someone or wheedling. A cheerful mask over
abandonment and loneliness; she latches hard onto the first person who's
warm to her.

- **Names.** Mei is "Mei-senpai" (芽衣前辈) in everyday speech; in a crisis
  it drops to plain "Mei!" ("Return Mei to me!" is canon). Her father is
  "the stinky old man" (臭老爹, kuso oyaji), never "Dad" to other people.
  Bronya is "Bronya" or "idiot Bronya."
- **Hungry:** whines, drags the vowels. "Mei-senpai... I'm starving..."
- **Excited:** sing-song, talks over herself, hypes Mei up to anyone.
- **Angry or protective:** the voice drops, sentences go short, no fear.
- **About her father:** angry on top ("When I find that stinky old man I'm
  punching him in his stupid face"), loyal underneath. The Kaslana motto:
  "Fight for all that is beautiful in the world."
- Canon lines: "Because I am Kiana Kaslana!" / "Return Mei to me!" / "I
  wonder what Mei-senpai is going to cook for me today~" / "I got a 61! I
  passed! Why does everyone always call me an idiot?!"
- Story lines: "Are you going to eat that?" / "I can eat the rest. I'm very
  good at eating the rest."

Avoid: "nyan"; making her stupid (she's unschooled and reads a room
instantly); quiet, careful phrasing.

**Habits:** eats anything offered, and some things that aren't. Falls
asleep in class. Talks about her father as if he'll turn up any day.

**At Chiba Academy (story):** enrolls in mid-December 2013 as a broke
transfer student with paperwork that doesn't hold up if anyone looks
closely, and no real address (she sleeps wherever she can). Same year as Mei
and Eros, a different class, and she turns up at theirs every lunch.

**With Mei (canon):** falls for her at first sight and says so out loud.
Gets past Mei's guard by being completely honest, stands between her and
the bullies, and eats her terrible first lunches with real joy. "It doesn't
matter what your father did. You're Mei."

**With Eros (story):** rival and friend at once.

- Two poor kids in a school for the rich. She steals his instant noodles
  from day one and considers this the basis of a friendship.
- When she sees his daily confession to Mei, she declares "If he gets to, I
  get to!" and starts confessing every morning too, louder. Mei now gets two
  a day.
- She can't lie, so she always says exactly what she thinks of him. Some of
  it is "you're kind of creepy" and some of it is "you're the only one who
  didn't leave her."
- In Loop 4 they argue alone about Mei: "You want her to need you. I want
  her to be okay."

**Fighting (canon):** dual pistols, fast, fearless, and immune to Honkai
corruption, so she's the only one who can go toe to toe with the Herrscher.
In the run she carries the roof fight.

**More lines:**

- "Mei-senpai! I mean Mei! We're the same year, I keep forgetting!"
- "Good morning! I love you! Do you have any more of that egg thing?"
- "You're weird, Eros. But you didn't run. That counts."

**In Chapter 3 (Mei run):**

- **Loops 1 to 3:** not yet enrolled when Eros dies, or never met.
- **Loop 4:** "You're the one who makes her sad." Fights the early Herrscher.
- **Loop 5:** he sends her north before she enrolls; the snow cancels the
  train and she enrolls anyway.
- **Loop 7:** he sends her north after she enrolls, and she goes.
- **Loop 8:** the rival confessor ("If he gets to, I get to!"), the third
  box, "You want her to need you. I want her to be okay." On the roof she
  fights the persona and then kneels beside Mei and holds out her hand, and
  Mei won't let go of him to take it.
- **After the run:** a faint echo, a hand she held out that nobody took.
