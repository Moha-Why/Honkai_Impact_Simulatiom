# Oda Fumi (born Sato Fumi)

**Role:** Eros's wife in Run 3. From the next village down the valley.

**Age:** 22 when they marry in 1917; outlives him.

**Appearance:** tall for the time and place, strong shoulders from farm work,
a quick crooked smile, hands that are never still.

**Personality:** steady, sharp-tongued and warm. Laughs at him when he's
being too serious, which is often. Not afraid of the forest or of him.

**How she talks:** teasing, direct.

- "You talk to the trees more than to me."
- "Come back by dark, or I'll come and find you, and you'll be sorry."

**In Chapter 2 (Run 3):** marries him in 1917; three children (log lines);
holds his hand when he dies of pneumonia in 1956.
