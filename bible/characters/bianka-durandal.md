# Bianka Ataegina ("Durandal")

**Role:** future heroine. In Chapter 2, glimpsed in December 2011 and a
major presence in the 2013 HQ secondment. Canon character; story changes
are marked.

**Age (story change):** to match the other heroines.

- **Records ("chosen") birthday:** January 1, **1995** (canon: January 1,
  2000).
- **Real birthday, secret:** December 7, **1995** (canon: December 7, 1998).
  It's the same day as Kiana's, because Kiana (K-423) is her clone.
- **Content rule:** her real age governs. She is 17 until December 7, 2013.
  Everything with Eros during the 2013 secondment is platonic only.

**Who she really is (canon, unknown to her and to almost everyone):** the
real Kiana Kaslana, daughter of Cecilia Schariac and Siegfried Kaslana. She
lost her memory and woke in an orphanage. Eros knows from the game and can
never say it.

**Appearance (canon):** 165 cm, fair skin, light blue eyes, strawberry-blonde
hair fading to light pink at the ends, usually worn down, sometimes in a high
ponytail. In 2013, Schicksal training gear and a new battlesuit (his
firmware). She fights with a lance.

## Background (canon, shifted to the 1995 birth)

- Her earliest memories are of waking with no past in an orphanage in Sochi
  among Second Eruption orphans (story: about age 4). She chose her own name.
- Otto was her doctor from childhood, one of the first people she remembers.
  He told her the story of Medea and told her to remember there's still
  kindness in the world. She's deeply attached to him.
- Ragna, the Assault Squad captain, visited the orphanage and taught the
  children gardening. Ragna died in the 2010 Assault Squad disaster.
- **Late 2011 (story: age 16):** Himeko comes to recruit for St. Freya.
  Bianka is furious at her for seeming heartless about Ragna. Himeko tells
  her to honor Ragna by fighting herself. Bianka joins Schicksal but goes
  straight to HQ, because St. Freya is too slow.
- **2012:** the only survivor of her squad on her first mission, at Agra
  Fort. On another mission she watched her teammates die in a wave of Honkai
  radiation and then slept for a week.
- **2013:** a rising B-rank at HQ, and later (canon) an A-rank squad leader,
  and in time Schicksal's strongest S-rank.

## Personality (canon, young)

- Ambitious, brave and kind. She risks her life to save people even when
  the odds are bad.
- Impatient and hot-headed. Acts first and gets told to think.
- Knows she's a genius and that ordinary people can't reach her level, and
  chose "the lonely path" to honor the dead. Trains every day so hard she
  calls it "a little like OCD". Looks down on people who won't leave their
  comfort zone.
- Believes the world values results above everything.
- Speaks English, German, Russian and French.
- Sleeps terribly. Head under the pillow, kicks the sheets off five times a
  night, drools. (Rita's report.)

## How she talks (young)

Fast, sure of herself, competitive, a little imperious. Blunt about other
people's limits.

- "Your simulation is too easy. Make it harder."
- "I don't care how long it took you. Does it work?"
- "St. Freya is too slow. I don't have time to be slow."
- "Again."

## With Eros (story)

- **Their first meeting (2011 corridor):** he sees her arguing with Himeko
  and knows whose daughter she is.
- **2013:** he writes the firmware for her battlesuit. She breaks his
  training simulations because they're too easy, so he makes them harder
  until she can't, and that's the only kind of respect she gives. She treats
  him like an annoying engineer, then a useful one. To him she's an annoying
  genius little sister.
- **What he carries:** the weight of knowing she's the real Kiana, that a
  clone of her is about to enroll at a school in Nagazora, and that her
  father is alive. Every time she says she has no family, he has to let it
  go.

**Relationships (canon):** Otto, her doctor and the person she trusts most.
Rita, newly assigned to her in 2013 as her adjutant, who will become her
closest companion.
