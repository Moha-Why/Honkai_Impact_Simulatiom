# Schicksal staff: minor characters (Chapter 2)

## Ms. Hanne Vogel (HR, Far East Branch)

**Role:** the human resources officer who does Eros's onboarding on his
first day (Chapter 1).

**Age:** about 50. German.

**Appearance:** cropped silver hair, a cardigan over a uniform blouse, a
fountain pen she lends to nobody.

**Personality:** brisk, efficient, not unkind. Has onboarded hundreds of
people who just learned the world is ending, and has a script for it.

**How she talks:** lists. "Salary. Leave. Cover story. Phones. Questions at
the end."

- "Assume everything you type on the staff network is read, because it is."
- "Your family will be told... ah. No family. That makes this shorter."

**What she sets out (Chapter 1):** €78,000 a year, paid monthly, with hazard
and secrecy allowances. 25 days of leave. The cover story: a software
engineer for a German industrial automation firm, with a mailing address in
Hamburg. A ferry to Shiomi on the mainland twice a week, and staff flights
from the runway. Junior quarters until promotion.

**Later:** in 2012 she processes his promotion (€140,000, 35 days of leave,
a staff flat) and says, "Try to actually take the leave this time."

## Professor Ōta Shigeru (archivist, Far East Branch)

**Role:** keeper of the branch archive. Teaches the three-afternoon history
course every new staff member takes (Chapter 2, March 2009). Recurring in
small ways.

**Age:** 74 in 2009. Japanese, born in Kyoto in 1935. Joined Schicksal in
1958 as a translator of old Latin and Greek records at HQ, and spent thirty
years there before he was posted east.

**Appearance:** tiny and bald, with huge black eyebrows that move on their
own and do most of his talking. A brown tweed jacket worn in any weather,
elbow patches, a fountain pen clipped in the breast pocket. Holds his slide
clicker like a conductor's baton. Reading glasses on a cord that he puts on
to look at people, not at text.

**Personality:** an old storyteller who loves Schicksal's history and knows
precisely which parts of it he's allowed to tell. Proud of the institution,
quietly sad about it. Kind to slow students and very interested in fast
ones. He notices who isn't taking notes. Nobody suspects him of anything,
and he likes it that way.

**How he talks:** formal, slightly old-fashioned English learned from
British books in the 1950s ("I dare say", "one supposes", "a great many").
Never raises his voice. In detail:

- **Answers questions with questions.** "How many centuries?" "How many
  would you like?"
- **Asks the room things he already knows the answer to**, and watches who
  answers too fast.
- **Pauses** and lets the eyebrows finish the sentence. "The Overseer has
  led us for a very long time." (Pause.) "A very long time."
- **Tests memory.** If someone isn't writing, he asks them to repeat what
  he said ten minutes ago, word for word.
- When something touches him personally, he becomes very brief.

**Secret weight:** at HQ in the 1980s he taught history to a boy named
Siegfried Kaslana, who asked better questions than anyone before or since.
That's why "He left" is all he'll say.

**What he wants:** for the stories to be kept, even the ones he can't tell.

**In Chapter 2 (2009):** tests Eros for not taking notes; answers the
Kaslana question with "There is one... He left."

## Klaus Hoffmann (engineer, HQ)

**Role:** Eros's colleague in the Battlesuit Division during the 2013 HQ
secondment.

**Age:** 34. German.

**Appearance:** big, bearded, cycling clothes under his lab coat.

**Personality:** cheerful, loud, gossipy, very good at his job. The
opposite of Mori. Tells Eros everything about HQ that nobody else will,
including which corridors not to stand in.

**How he talks:** loud English with German sentence order.

- "This floor, the Overseer sometimes walks. You stand straight, you say
  nothing, you live long."
- "The little Ataegina breaks every suit we give her. Now she is your
  problem. Congratulations."

## Captain Nadia Rahman (A-rank instructor, Far East Branch)

**Role:** a survivor of the Ganesha incident (2012). Minor.

**Age:** 31. Bangladeshi.

**Appearance:** a burn scar down one side of her neck, a cane she hates.

**Personality:** proud, blunt, grieving two friends.

**In Chapter 2 (2012):** insists on running the unfinished Ganesha
simulation Eros is building, fails it, and thanks him. "Make it worse. It
was worse."

## The 2009 intake (orientation course, Chapter 2)

Eleven new staff, three afternoons with Professor Ōta. Most are background.
The ones who speak:

### Chen Jianhong ("Lao Chen"), cook

- **Age:** 46. From Tainan, Taiwan. Twenty years in hotel kitchens before
  Schicksal hired him for the cafeteria.
- **Appearance:** round, bald on top, forearms covered in old burn
  scars, always too hot. Takes his sweater off within ten minutes of
  entering any room.
- **Personality:** warm, loud, practical, unafraid of looking stupid.
  Measures every new fact against food and feeding people.
- **How he talks:** asks the question everyone else is too embarrassed to
  ask, out loud, immediately. Switches to Mandarin with Eros the moment he
  hears his accent and calls him "xiao di" (little brother). Taiwanese
  Mandarin, slow and broad, against Eros's quick Hangzhou Mandarin.
  - "Do they eat? The Honkai. Do they eat anything?"
  - "Thirty thousand years and nobody wrote down a recipe."
- **Later:** runs the cafeteria line. The reason Curry Tuesday never
  changes. Gives Eros extra rice without asking.

### Hirano Saki, accounts

- **Age:** 22. From Saitama. Recruited out of a bank because Schicksal's
  books are complicated in ways it can't explain to normal accountants.
- **Appearance:** small, neat, a dark cardigan buttoned to the top,
  colored pens lined up by the notebook.
- **Personality:** frightened and stubborn about it. Cried for a whole day
  after intake and came to the course anyway. Writes down everything.
- **How she talks:** apologizes before every question. Asks for spellings.
  - "Sorry. Sorry, is that Honkai with a K?"
  - "Is this going to be on a test?" (It isn't. She studies anyway.)

### The Varga twins, Bence and Bálint, security

- **Age:** 28. Hungarian, ex-army. Identical; only their watches differ
  (Bence wears his on the right).
- **Appearance:** very big, crew cuts, arms folded, never both looking at
  the same thing.
- **Personality:** bored by everything that isn't a threat. Loyal to
  Schicksal in a flat, uncomplicated way.
- **How they talk:** almost never, and one word at a time. When one speaks,
  the other nods. "Traitor." (About Siegfried, as if it were the weather.)

## Sigrun Aasen (medical, Far East Branch)

- **Age:** 38. Norwegian. C-rank Valkyrie field medic who now runs the
  branch's orthopedics; she "does knees".
- **Appearance:** tall, sunburned, white-blonde braid, rides a bicycle
  everywhere in a full combat harness because she's on call.
- **Personality:** loud, blunt and cheerful. Shouts across any distance.
  Keeps a mental list of every missed appointment on the island.
- **How she talks:** loud, plain, no small talk.
  - "Kudo! Thursday! You missed Thursday!"
- **In Chapter 2 (2009):** passes Asami on the seawall on her bicycle and
  shouts about her knee appointment.

## Mao and Ji-woo (logistics, Far East Branch)

Asami's friends and her usual company at the harbor bar. Both D-rank
Valkyries.

### Higa Mao

- **Age:** 24 in 2010. From Okinawa. Helicopter pilot.
- **Appearance:** tall, long black hair in a low tail, flight jacket.
- **How she talks:** slow, deadpan, says little. Argues with Ji-woo about
  whether helicopters or trucks are more dangerous, every time.

### Bae Ji-woo

- **Age:** 23 in 2010. From Busan. Drives the trucks.
- **Appearance:** short and round, a permanent sunburned nose, laughs with
  her whole body.
- **How she talks:** fast, loud, talks over everyone, heart broken weekly
  by men she never actually dated.
