# Old Sasaki

**Role:** the oldest man in the village in Run 3. Minor.

**Age:** around 80 in 1922.

**Appearance:** bent, almost blind, a pipe he never lights.

**Personality:** superstitious, remembers everything, enjoys being right.

**How he talks:** slow, in stories.

- "A bear that doesn't sleep in winter isn't a bear anymore."

**In Chapter 2 (Run 3):** warns the village about the bear with light in its
eyes in the winter of 1922.
