# Hayato Natori

Status: retired Collateral-outline concept. Not introduced in drafted
prose and not part of the current Until Tomorrow plan.

**Role:** local asset custodian at the ME Corp records depot. Director of a
recovery contractor working for a Cocolia front company. Human antagonist.

**Age:** 42. Japanese.

**Appearance:** carefully fitted work jacket, bright ties, polished shoes
that are wrong for the loading floor. Carries a tablet and a paper folder
because he does not trust the depot's printers.

**Personality:** friendly, impatient and transactional. Wants a clean
handover, his commission and no publicity. Treats detention as another
part of arranging transport. When his economical solution fails, he
becomes visibly angry rather than remaining mysteriously calm.

**Leverage:** armed ordinary security, transfer documents and authority over
the depot's systems. His purchaser requires the original contractor's
completed integrity audit before payment, so he tries to buy Eros's
cooperation before resorting to force. Eros becomes expendable once the
handover is complete.

**Limits:** knows the local transfer order and his approval contact, not
Cocolia's entire plan. No Honkai abilities, battlesuit or omniscience.

**How he talks:** casual, uses first names too quickly, makes a demand sound
like a favor that will let everyone go home.

- "Finish the audit, Eros. Then I can get everyone paid."
- "We have a car for Miss Raiden. You're making transport complicated."

**Retired Collateral outline:** buys Eros's first progress report; uses it to isolate
Mei; pressures the contractor to complete the handover; loses local custody
during the escape. His sponsor and the off-site authorization remain
unresolved.
