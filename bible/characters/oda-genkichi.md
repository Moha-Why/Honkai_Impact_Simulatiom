# Oda Genkichi

**Role:** a hunter in a settler village in the hills of Hokkaido, 1908. Eros's
father in Run 3.

**Age:** 48 in 1908.

**Appearance:** small, wiry, weathered brown face, a beard he trims with a
knife. Missing two teeth. Moves through the forest without a sound.

**Personality:** gruff, silent, patient. Teaches by doing and expects the
boy to watch. Proud in ways he never says.

**How he talks:** a few words at a time, usually orders.

- "Watch. Again."
- "The forest tells you everything. You don't listen."

**In Chapter 2 (Run 3):** teaches Eros the bow, the knife and tracking; dies
in 1919 (log line).
