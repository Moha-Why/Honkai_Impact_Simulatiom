# Duty Officer Yoon Seo-yeon

**Role:** senior duty officer of the Far East Branch's anomaly watch room
(ninth floor). Minor, recurring.

**Age:** 34 in 2014. Korean.

**Appearance:** short, neat, hair scraped back in a bun, reading glasses on
top of her head that she actually remembers are there. A Schicksal uniform
with the sleeves pushed up. Always holding a mug.

**Personality:** precise, unflappable, chronically short of sleep. Respects
Eros because his code has never lied to her. Doesn't gossip, but always
knows who's been sent where.

**How she talks:** short, factual, a little dry.

- "The principal's asking for whoever built this. That's you."
- "Murata left for Nagazora this morning. Don't tell anyone I told you."

**In Chapter 2:** calls Eros down when Theresa visits the watch room; later
mentions that Himeko has gone to Nagazora.

**In the Chapter 2 rewrite:** meets Eros in 2011 while he builds the anomaly
watch; they argue about thresholds, then about coffee, and become friends.
In October 2011 she shows him the report on the first outbreak the watch
caught early (a fishing town on the Korean coast): no civilian deaths.
