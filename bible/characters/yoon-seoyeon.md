# Duty Officer Yoon Seo-yeon

**Role:** senior duty officer of the Far East Branch's anomaly watch room
(ninth floor). Minor, recurring.

**Age:** 34 in 2014. Korean.

**Appearance:** short, neat, hair scraped back in a bun, reading glasses on
top of her head that she actually remembers are there. A Schicksal uniform
with the sleeves pushed up. Always holding a mug.

**Personality:** precise, unflappable, chronically short of sleep. Respects
Eros because his code has never lied to her. Doesn't gossip, but always
knows who's been sent where.

**How she talks:** short, factual, a little dry. Different from Asami's
teasing: Yoon is exact, not playful. In detail:

- **Duty-log voice.** Times to the minute, numbers with units. "Oh-four
  seventeen. Nought point six HW. Then nothing."
- **Corrects people**, including herself, mid-sentence. "Three days. Two
  and a half."
- **Says "No"** as a complete review. Says "Hm" when something is right,
  which is rare and means a lot.
- **Her jokes are literal.** She answers the exact words of a question,
  not the meaning, with a straight face.
- **Brings up her mother** (who wants her married and back in Daegu) only
  in the third person, as a data point. "My mother says the night shift
  is why I'm not married. My mother is correct."
- Polite to Theresa and nobody else.

- "The principal's asking for whoever built this. That's you."
- "That's all we've got near Nagazora. Two birds, both outside. It's ME
  Corp's town. They run their own network inside it, and they don't share."
- "Standby. Twelve hours' notice is now two. The principal's orders,
  oh-five-hundred."

**In Chapter 2:** calls Eros down when Theresa visits the watch room; later
tells him the Hyperion is on two hours' standby.

**In the Chapter 2 rewrite:** meets Eros in 2011 while he builds the anomaly
watch; they argue about thresholds, then about coffee, and become friends.
In October 2011 she shows him the report on the first outbreak the watch
caught early (a fishing town on the Korean coast): no civilian deaths.
