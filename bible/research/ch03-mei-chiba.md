# Research Notes: Chapter 3, Until Tomorrow

These notes distinguish source material from the novel's chosen adaptation.
The outline, specific bullying scenes, life-node interface, four loops,
Eros's intervention and the kiss are original story choices, not canon claims.

## School reputation and the arrest

Mei's biography places Ryoma's arrest on November 16, 2013, and attributes
the charges to Cocolia's framing. Her social isolation afterward is part
of the background used here. The outline expands that reversal into its
own scenes; individual acts of bullying are not claimed as official events.

Source: [Mei's story, community-maintained wiki](https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Raiden_Mei/Story).

The detailed origin story in which many boys confess, receive severe
rejections, and spread the Lightning Queen nickname is especially visible
in material explicitly labeled Houkai Gakuen 2. Do not silently treat every
detail as independently verified Honkai Impact 3rd canon. This novel
deliberately adopts the admired, difficult-to-approach school queen
reputation requested by its author. It is a social nickname before the
eruption, not public knowledge of a Herrscher identity.

Source: [Mei's Houkai Gakuen 2 biography](https://bkso.baidu.com/item/%E9%9B%B7%E7%94%B5%E8%8A%BD%E8%A1%A3/15284591).

## Cocolia in the period covered

At the point covered by the run, Cocolia is an Anti-Entropy executor,
Ryoma's factional rival and the operator behind the corporate takeover
and artificial outbreak. Her later detention is irrelevant to her status
in late 2013 and early 2014.

Ryoma's framing removes Mei's protection and facilitates Cocolia's control
of ME Corp. The outbreak is an engineered Honkai event intended to awaken
the power associated with Mei's Gem of Conquest. Bullying and distress
shape Mei's vulnerability and the persona's hostility; they are not the
entire physical mechanism of the eruption.

Sources:
- [Ryoma's biography](https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Raiden_Ryoma).
- [Archives timeline, which describes a Honkai bomb](https://honkai-impact-3rd-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline).
- [Chinese timeline discussion](https://news.17173.com/z/bh3/content/11202021/194133466_all.shtml).
- [Cocolia's Chinese biography](https://bkso.baidu.com/item/%E5%8F%AF%E5%8F%AF%E5%88%A9%E4%BA%9A/22938115).

Accounts of exactly when and how the Gem was implanted vary across older
summaries. This chapter does not invent a newly verified implantation date:
the Gem is already in Mei by the school period. The particular device
cover story, local operatives, redundant trigger and response to Eros's
interference are this novel's inventions. No real-world construction
details are needed.

Bronya's assignment to recover Mei belongs to Cocolia's operation. The
novel keeps that recovery pressure outside the immediate rooftop climax;
Kiana and Eros's confrontation occurs before a later recovery could play
out. It does not transplant every subsequent manga event into one fight.

Source: [Escape from Nagazora summary](https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Escape_from_Nagazora).
Official publication: [Escape from Nagazora](https://manga.honkaiimpact3.com/book/1015).

## The early Herrscher persona

The early persona is proud, harsh toward humans and capable of cruelty.
She also has attachments and can cooperate or yield control; she should
not be written as an interchangeable embodiment of destruction.

The rooftop accounts describe Kiana drawing her interest and the persona
relinquishing control. Later game material shows cooperation with Mei in
trying to protect Kiana. Use that as a characterization basis, not as
permission to give a newly awakened persona every later ability or bond.

Sources:
- [3rd Herrscher profile](https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/3rd_Herrscher).
- [Chapter VIII synopsis, including cooperation against the Herrscher of the Void](https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_VIII).
- [Lightning Empress dorm description, game text reproduced by BWIKI](https://wiki.biligame.com/bh3/%E5%AE%B6%E5%9B%AD%EF%BC%9A%E9%9B%B7%E7%94%B5%E5%A5%B3%E7%8E%8B%E7%9A%84%E9%AC%BC%E9%93%A0).

Her specific relationship with Eros, shared awareness of the current
loop's courtship, and final decision to stop escalating are adaptations.
She cannot read his System or remember discarded loops. Her observations
come from Mei's current-loop experiences and what she can see.

## Chinese fanfic reading

I inspected accessible chapters from:
- [Honkai 3, but I Am Ren Amamiya!, chapter 9](https://www.trxs.cc/tongren/8773/9.html).
- [In Honkai 3, Being Their Hero, chapter 3](https://wap.faloo.com/1369369_3.html).

These provide examples of fanfic emphasis on former admirers turning
hostile, bystanders ignoring exclusion, and the contrast between ordinary
attention and treatment of a disgraced heiress. They are inspiration for
tone and social context, not evidence of canon events.

Do not copy their dialogue, rescue sequences, particular humiliation
set pieces or crossover explanations of the Herrscher. The new outline
uses its own rumor, video, group-project and disciplinary scenes.

## Access and certainty

The official manga landing page and the two fanfic chapters were readable.
Several detailed wiki pages could be searched through indexed excerpts
but their full-page opens were blocked or unavailable. Claims above are
kept to what the retrieved material supports; incompatible continuities
are identified rather than blended invisibly.

The novel already uses adult ages for Mei and Kiana. School meetings before
the eruption and the final kiss are deliberately alternate-universe events.
