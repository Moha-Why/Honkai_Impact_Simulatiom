# World fashion (story change)

In this world, adult women dress in vulgar, kinky clothing as everyday wear:
harnesses, latex, rope, collars, cuffs, garters, sheer panels, fetish
accessories, body markings. Everyone treats it as normal. It's tied to one
of the novel's secrets (see future-arcs.md when that is written). Men dress
normally.

## Rules

- **Adults only, by each woman's own birthday.** A girl wears ordinary
  clothes until the day she turns 18 and switches after it. Chiba Academy
  students under 18 wear the normal uniform; students who are 18 wear what
  they like at school.
- **Nothing in a childhood log.** The Life Path childhoods (for Mei's run,
  1995 to April 13, 2013) describe no one's clothing in this way, adult
  women included. The fashion starts on the page at the takeover.
- **Theresa is included.** In this story she stopped growing as a petite
  adult with an adult face and body (story change; see her profile), so the
  fashion applies to her like any other adult woman. She's always written
  and drawn as clearly adult: short, never childlike.
- **Rotation.** Adult women own several outfits and switch between them by
  occasion: school or work, private, formal, field. The outfits say who
  they are. No micro bikinis.
- **Private areas are always covered** (straps, panels, lacing, rope wraps).
  It's vulgar and kinky, never nude.
- **Class shows in it.** Rich women wear tailored latex and silk; poor ones
  wear thrifted vinyl and tape. After November 16, Mei's tailor stops
  coming, and the girls who leave her turn up in matching new sets.

## Eros and the fashion

- He's from Earth and he's a pervert, so he looks every time. The narration
  says so plainly: what he looks at, for a line or two, then on with the
  scene. Liar's Face keeps his face clean while he does it.
- He files it with the world's other differences from the game (its own
  ages, its own faces). Now and then it tugs at him as a thing that doesn't
  fit, a hook for the secret.
- Nobody else ever comments on it.
- **His voice, not a thug's.** His lust is written as he thinks: dry,
  specific, a fan's and an engineer's eye (the fan-art folder, the banner at
  3 a.m., patch notes, crash logs, a screenshot before a crash). Blunt words
  are rare so they land. It changes with his soul: eager and half in love in
  Loop 1, taking stock in the middle loops, flat and clinical when Torn
  (his body "the one process still running at full speed"). The System
  answers it differently each time, never with the same dick joke.
