# Simulation System

The Imaginary Simulation System tells Eros the simulations aren't real. They are:
each run writes a branch into the Imaginary Tree.

## Simulation types

- **Normal simulations:** classic life simulations. The run plays out, mostly
  as log lines, until Eros dies; then he settles rewards based on what the run
  achieved. Death ends the run.
- **Heroine simulations:** built around one heroine, with meters and
  objectives. Death resets the run at a soul cost. Failing the objectives
  lets him end the run or restart it.

## Starts

- Every heroine run gives Eros an identity and a starting point that is
  normal or bad, usually with a hidden route toward the heroine.
- **Immediate start:** he arrives in his identity with his memories intact.
- **Childhood start:** he's born into the run. His memories stay sealed until
  the heroine turns 18, and a real child lives the years. The childhood is
  told as detailed log lines, like a normal run, with full scenes at the
  moments that matter. Nothing romantic happens before 18.
- Meters and objectives only activate once the heroine is 18 or older.

## Meters

- Heroine: **Affection**, **Trust**, **Corruption**, shown as named levels,
  not numbers:
  - **Affection:** Stranger, Noticed, Friendly, Close, Fond, In Love
  - **Trust:** Wary, Polite, Open, Confides, Relies
  - **Corruption:** Untouched, Bending, Compromised, Fallen. Her devotion
    turning dark for him. Higher Corruption raises the grade (see
    [gifts-and-rewards.md](gifts-and-rewards.md), Heroine runs).
- World: **Fate Deviation**, which measures how much he has changed
  history. The higher it climbs, the harder the world pushes back.

## Objectives

- Objectives are the conditions for finishing the run, nothing more.
  Several run at once and completing one is enough.
- The grade comes from two pillars, Love and Corruption, and S also needs
  most of her hidden objectives (see gifts-and-rewards.md). How many
  objectives he finished doesn't change it.
- Once at least one objective is complete, his next death ends the run as a
  success instead of resetting it.
- **Each heroine run also has a set end,** the point where her arc closes.
  If he's still alive then, the run ends there. Mei's run ends when the
  Hyperion arrives over Nagazora.

## Life Path (heroine runs)

Heroine runs with a childhood start play the life from birth as a story,
with forks.

- **The log:** young Eros's life runs as detailed log lines in the second
  person, year by year, as in a normal run: family, school, the people
  around him, what happens to him. Full scenes at the moments that matter.
  Not a summary.
- **Forks:** many of them. Birth, then every moment that matters in that
  life (Loop 1 of Mei's run has eleven; most of her loops have six to ten;
  a life that ends at nine has six). The System stops the log and offers
  options, usually three, sometimes two or four, in the log format:

```
[2007, February. Your mother is dying. Your father calls from Nagazora.]
Go to him / Stay with your grandmother / Run away from both of them
```

- **Who chooses:** adult Eros, from the white, outside the life. The child
  doesn't know a choice was made. He carries it out and lives with it. Some
  forks are traps, worded by the System to sound safe, and some kill him as
  a child.
- **Not every fork is his.** Some answers change nothing (a stepfather
  who asks permission and marries her anyway: "Some of them are just
  manners"), and some deaths come with no fork at all.
- **Eros can hand a fork to someone in the life** ("Ask the night
  porter"); the log follows that person's answer.
- **What changes:** every fork changes the rest of the log: where he lives,
  who he knows, who he becomes, how he reaches the arc. Options marked ???
  open only after a loop shows him something.
- **Takeover:** on the heroine's 18th birthday (for Mei, April 13, 2013) his
  memories return all at once, into a body with a whole lived life in it,
  and he plays the rest of the run scene by scene.
- **Repeats:** when he makes the same choices as an earlier loop, the log
  can say so ("The same as last time, until 2004") and pick up where it
  changes.
- **Fixed nodes** stay gray: the canon events the run is built around (for
  Mei: her father's arrest on November 16, Kiana enrolling in December, the
  Eruption at Chiba Academy in February, and his place in her class). Some
  fixed events aren't shown as fixed (for Mei, the 2004 kidnapping). He
  learns by pushing on them, and they push back through Fate Deviation and
  correction events.
- **Gifts:** one 5-keep-2 draw at the start of the run, kept through every
  loop.

## Crossroads (normal runs)

Normal runs **react** to a life instead of planning it. Not available in
Chapter 2. It unlocks as a reward after Eros's first completed heroine run
(Mei's), announced by the System like a patch note.

- No screen before the run. The scenario is still random unless he buys the
  scenario pick in the shop (300).
- While the log lines run, the System stops at **one to three forks per
  run** and offers two or three options, shown in the log format:

```
[1999, July. A truck is going south tonight.]
Stay / Steal a seat / Report the driver
```

- Every fork is final. Death still ends the run, and there are no resets.
- Some forks are traps, worded by the System to sound safe.
- Fixed history still stands. A fork can change his life, never the big
  canon events.

## Bait objectives

The System may list an objective that can't be completed because it runs
against a fixed node (for Mei: "Keep Raiden Ryoma out of prison"). It shows
a large reward to tempt him into poking the fixed node. Asked about it later
it says, "I never said it was possible. I said it was worth a lot."

## Aftermath

- After a heroine run, Eros's identity in it is treated as dead or missing.
  A later run with the same heroine continues from there.
- Failed runs reset completely. He can retry later from a different start.
- **Pre-era heroines:** a completed run brings her back to the present with
  memories of the final loop only. She arrives after a delay, often
  somewhere dangerous, and weak at first.
- **Rewritten history:** a completed pre-era run rewrites the history of
  everyone Eros dealt with in it, not only the heroine. They remember him
  from the final loop. Kevin lived through the pre-era, so he will know
  Eros, and will recognize his face when they meet in the present. The same
  goes for Otto if a run ever touches his past.
- **What holds:** the big events of history still happen as in canon unless
  Eros changed them directly. What changes is the heroine's fate and who
  remembers him.
- **Items from a living heroine's run are her real things (hidden; only
  the System knows).** When he takes her personal item at settlement, the
  real heroine loses it the same night. For Mei: from February 5, 2014 she
  wears one hair pin, can't remember losing the other, and Eros has it. He
  believes it's something the System made.
- **Living heroines:** a completed run collapses into her as echoes (dreams,
  déjà vu, a sense she knows him), stronger with each objective completed.
  The echoes only surface once the real timeline reaches the run's start.
  Mei's run is set in her third year, which is already happening when the
  System binds, so her echoes can surface at once.
  People Eros was close to in the run (Kiana in Mei's run) may get fainter
  echoes.

## Entering a run

- He enters through sleep. His body stays put, and one run takes about one
  night however many years pass inside.
- One normal run per day is free in Fate Fragments, offered every night.
  Unused runs don't stack. The real price is the soul: about one run a week
  is sustainable (see [soul-and-fate.md](soul-and-fate.md), "The real limit
  on runs"). Extra runs from the shop (100) cost the same soul.
- Heroine runs unlock when the System decides and announces it.
