# Simulation System

The Imaginary Simulation System tells Eros the simulations aren't real. They are:
each run writes a branch into the Imaginary Tree.

Its voice around the simulations is cunning and playful, drawing Eros
toward the next choice with teasing, deflection and enticing rewards.
Keep costs, warnings and settlement rules clear. Its manner can change
without changing what a run does. See
[characters/the-system.md](characters/the-system.md) for dialogue guidance.

## Simulation types

- **Normal simulations:** classic life simulations. The run plays out, mostly
  as log lines, until Eros dies; then he settles rewards based on what the run
  achieved. Death ends the run.
- **Heroine simulations:** built around one heroine, with meters and
  objectives. Death resets the run at a soul cost. Failing the objectives
  lets him end the run or restart it.

## Starts

- Every heroine run gives Eros an identity and a starting point that is
  normal or bad, usually with a hidden route toward the heroine.
- **Immediate start:** he arrives in his identity with his memories intact.
- **Childhood start:** his memories and the System stay sealed until the
  heroine turns 18. He lives the childhood as a real child her age, the
  years are summarized in log lines, and nothing romantic happens.
- Meters and objectives only activate once the heroine is 18 or older.

### Life nodes before immersion

Before full immersion, Eros observes the character's earlier life in log
form and chooses at available decision nodes. Some profile and scenario
nodes are fixed; others offer ordinary alternatives. The choices lead to
the immersion point and can change preparation, contacts, housing and
earlier decisions, not force another person's affection.

- Fixed nodes are labeled. There is no choice that secretly changes them.
- Changeable nodes show available options, without revealing unplayed
  future outcomes. Past observed consequences can inform another attempt.
- On a reset within the same heroine run, he may revisit unlocked nodes.
  The run's gift draw stays locked; this is not a free reroll.
- Choices do not provide permanent gifts, free superior bodies or a
  successful relationship. Any new skill retention still follows settlement.
- These choices are included in the heroine scenario, with no additional
  fragment fee. They are separate from buying a normal scenario.
- Eros selects from outside the summarized life. Childhood remains
  age-appropriate; full romantic immersion starts only at adult ages.
- A run identity replaces his personal history within that branch. There
  is not a second Eros simultaneously living his real-world biography.
- Fixed setup is not an excuse to erase credible actions during immersion.
  Other people and factions can react, changing how future events unfold.

For the planned Mei run, full immersion begins as an eighteen-year-old
transfer student on October 1, 2013, before Ryoma's arrest. Each reset
reaches that same entry through the chosen available nodes.

## Meters

- Heroine: **Affection**, **Trust**, **Corruption**, shown as named levels,
  not numbers:
  - **Affection:** Stranger, Noticed, Friendly, Close, Fond, In Love
  - **Trust:** Wary, Polite, Open, Confides, Relies
  - **Corruption:** Untouched, Bending, Compromised, Fallen
- World: **Fate Deviation**, which measures how much he has changed
  history. The higher it climbs, the harder the world pushes back.

## Objectives

- Several run at once. Completing one is enough; each extra one raises the
  reward's size and rarity.
- Once at least one objective is complete, his next death ends the run as a
  success instead of resetting it.

## Aftermath

- After a heroine run, Eros's identity in it is treated as dead or missing.
  A later run with the same heroine continues from there.
- Failed runs reset completely. He can retry later from a different start.
- **Pre-era heroines:** a completed run brings her back to the present with
  memories of the final loop only. She arrives after a delay, often
  somewhere dangerous, and weak at first.
- **Rewritten history:** a completed pre-era run rewrites the history of
  everyone Eros dealt with in it, not only the heroine. They remember him
  from the final loop. Kevin lived through the pre-era, so he will know
  Eros, and will recognize his face when they meet in the present. The same
  goes for Otto if a run ever touches his past.
- **What holds:** the big events of history still happen as in canon unless
  Eros changed them directly. What changes is the heroine's fate and who
  remembers him.
- **Living heroines:** a completed run collapses into her as echoes (dreams,
  déjà vu, a sense she knows him), stronger with each objective completed.
  The echoes only surface once the real timeline reaches the run's start.
  The planned Mei run starts at Chiba Academy on October 1, 2013 and
  completes after four loops. Its echoes require both completion and the
  real timeline reaching that start, so they can appear on the planned
  February 6 morning. Only the successful final loop supplies those
  impressions; discarded loops do not become the heroines' memories.
  People Eros was close to in a completed run may get fainter echoes.

## Entering a run

- He enters through sleep. His body stays put, and one run takes about one
  night however many years pass inside.
- One normal run per day is free.
- Heroine runs unlock when the System decides and announces it.
