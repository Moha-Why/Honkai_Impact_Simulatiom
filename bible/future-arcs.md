# Future arcs

Planned beyond the current chapters. Not locked; change as the story grows.

## Tsugumi (after the Third Eruption)

See [characters/mizuki-tsugumi.md](characters/mizuki-tsugumi.md).

1. **The healing months.** Mei's run leaves Eros's soul Torn, so heroine runs
   are locked for months and only normal runs are open. Those months are the
   romance: Shiomi, the print shop, her family, the game they build together
   from the story he "makes up".
2. **The moral knot.** While he's with her, the real Mei is at St. Freya with
   echoes of a boy who confessed to her every morning. Eros tells himself the
   runs aren't real, so they aren't cheating. The merges keep bleeding
   feelings into him anyway.
3. **The outbreak nobody wrote.** A sudden Honkai outbreak hits Shiomi. It is
   in no part of the game's story, so Eros has no warning.
   - **Cause (hidden):** the global Fate Deviation meter. His runs and
     real-world changes have pushed the present away from canon, and this
     is what that looks like. The System knows and says nothing. The reader
     can suspect it. Eros finds out much later, and then "the simulations
     aren't real" becomes the cruelest lie in the story.
   - **Eros is there,** on leave, at her apartment. Five years of training to
     run away, and the one time he has to run carrying someone, he can't.
     His anomaly watch flags the town, too late, on the island's screens.
   - **She dies for real.** No revival. If the System ever dangles a way
     back, it's a lie or a trap, and the answer stays no.
4. **The heartbroken heroine run.** The day his soul heals enough for heroine
   runs again, she's already gone. His first heroine run back is played by a
   grieving man:
   - Reckless, near-suicidal "fuck around, find out".
   - The cold, power-first self with nothing warm left to cover it.
   - The System at its worst: gentle, understanding, kind, pushing him in.
   - The heroine should be one whose story is about loss (Kiana or Bronya are
     candidates; decide later).
5. **Threads it leaves:**
   - **Her drive,** with `HONKAI_bible.txt` and the whole story in his words.
     Schicksal's cleanup crews collect electronics from outbreak zones;
     Brenner closed Eros's file in 2013, and this could open it again.
   - **Her family** in Shiomi.
   - **His power hunger.** After her, it's no longer about not dying. It's
     about never being the one who has to run.
