# Image prompts

Detailed prompts for the story's important characters, taken from their
profiles. Each one has a shared style line, the prompt itself, and a negative
prompt. Every heroine is an adult (18 or older) and is drawn with adult
proportions.

**Shared style (put this at the start of any prompt):**
> high-quality semi-realistic anime illustration, modern action-RPG splash
> art style, clean line art, soft cinematic lighting, detailed fabric and
> hair, expressive eyes, painterly background, 4k

**Shared negative prompt:**
> childlike proportions, chibi, deformed hands, extra fingers, extra limbs,
> blurry face, low resolution, watermark, text artifacts, nsfw, revealing
> clothing, oversexualized pose

## Eros (protagonist, 22, January 2014)

> A tall, slim young man of 22 with ambiguous East Asian features. Handsome
> in a tired way. Black hair worn a little too long, falling over his
> forehead and sticking up slightly on one side because he forgets haircuts.
> Dark eyes with faint shadows under them, the look of someone who codes
> until 3 a.m. Calm, mild, harmless-looking expression, with something
> watchful and cold just behind it. He wears a dark gray hoodie over a plain
> white shirt, black trousers and worn running shoes. A Schicksal lanyard
> hangs at his neck, its laminated badge reading SENIOR ENGINEER. He holds a
> can of coffee in one hand. He stands on the balcony of a small top-floor
> staff flat at night, leaning on the railing. Behind him a glass door opens
> into a room with a gray sofa and a large potted plant. In front of him a
> black sea and, far across the water, a thin line of town lights. Hanging in
> the air in front of his face, visible only to him, is a faint translucent
> pale panel with a thin glowing border and lines of text. Cool blue
> moonlight, warm light from the room behind him, sea wind in his hair.

**Variant, at 18 (March 2009):** same face, younger and thinner. A cheap
student jacket, one bag over his shoulder, a laminated badge reading
SOFTWARE ENGINEER, standing in a white corporate corridor under a huge
gilt-framed portrait of a blond man in white and gold.

## Theresa Apocalypse (head of the Far East Branch, S-rank)

> A very petite adult woman, about a head and a half shorter than an average
> man, with a grown woman's face: small, sharp-featured and clearly adult, not
> childlike. Long white hair tied in a low ponytail draped over one shoulder.
> Bright blue eyes. She wears a frilly white and blue dress that resembles a
> nun's habit designed by someone who loves lace, with a white veil-like
> headpiece, blue trim and a small cross. Mid-pose, doing her signature move:
> one hand on her hip, chin tipped up, a playful wink, and the other hand
> flicking a wrapped strawberry candy in pink plastic onto a desk like a
> magician finishing a trick. A bright, sing-song, mischievous expression.
> Setting: an underground open-plan engineering office with rows of monitors,
> a stunned engineer at a desk beside her, and a broken white training
> battlesuit gauntlet on the desk. Warm office light, a sparkle effect around
> her.

**Variant, stern:** the same woman standing on a cafeteria chair holding up a
single carrot, face cold and commanding, a huge chef shrinking back.

## Raiden Mei (heroine, 18, Chiba Academy, autumn 2013)

> A tall, elegant 18-year-old Japanese woman (172 cm) with a swordswoman's
> straight posture. Extremely long dark purple hair with a blue-violet sheen,
> reaching her thighs, in a hime cut with straight bangs and long side
> strands, half of it tied up behind with simple hair pins. Indigo eyes. Her
> expression is polite, composed and closed off, a faint distance in her
> gaze, beautiful but untouchable. She wears an elite private-school uniform,
> a navy blazer with a crest, a white blouse, a neat ribbon tie, a pleated
> skirt and black stockings, worn exactly right. She carries a wooden bokken
> in a cloth bag over her shoulder. Setting: an upscale academy corridor at
> golden hour with tall windows and long shadows, other students blurred in
> the background glancing at her. Soft warm light, a faint crackle of static
> in the air around her hair.

**Variant, after November 16:** the same uniform, alone at her desk in an
empty classroom, an unopened homemade bento in a plain box in front of her,
"criminal's daughter" scrawled faintly on the desk, rain on the windows,
gray light.

## The Herrscher of Thunder (Mei's Herrscher persona)

> The same tall young woman, transformed and terrifying. Her dark purple hair
> has grown even longer and floats behind her like a curtain in the static.
> Two dark red horns rise from her head. Her eyes are violet with hot pink
> pupils, glowing. A single huge half-wing made of crackling purple lightning
> spreads from one shoulder. Her school uniform is torn and singed at the
> edges. Her expression is regal, cold and contemptuous, a faint cruel smile.
> She hovers above the rooftop of a school at night with one hand raised,
> lightning crawling over her skin and arcing across the roof. Below and
> behind her is a city with fires and dark smoke, and a storm overhead lit
> from inside by purple lightning. Strong purple and magenta rim light,
> sparks and ozone haze, dramatic low angle.

## The System

> An abstract visual of an AI interface only one man can see. A crisp,
> translucent pale panel hanging in the air with a thin glowing border, the
> word Status at the top and neat lines of text below. Faint branching lines
> like the roots and limbs of a vast tree spread out behind it into darkness,
> each branch tipped with a tiny glowing scene of a different life (a snowy
> outpost, a hospital corridor, a hillside village, a school rooftop). A
> small musical note symbol glows in one corner. Dark background, soft
> white-blue glow, elegant, mysterious and a little too friendly.
