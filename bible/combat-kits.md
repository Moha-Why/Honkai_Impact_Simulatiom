# Combat Kits

What each fighter can do in a written chapter, how it looks, and what stops
it. Write or update a kit before any fight scene (see
[fight-writing.md](fight-writing.md)). Names in brackets are what the System
calls a move; they only appear in Eros's point of view.

## Eros, Mei run, Loop 4 (age 18, Nagazora, December 2013 to February 2014)

**Rating: Civilian.** A thin scholarship boy who has never trained his body.
Everything he has comes from gifts and one experience package. The System
names it all, and none of it makes him strong.

- **Body.** Honkai Resistance (Low D): the bad air doesn't turn him and he
  doesn't cough pink. It still goes into his lungs like ground glass.
- **The timer.** About ten fast meters, or a minute of running in Honkai
  air, and his lungs close. Then he's on one knee for half a minute.

| Move | What it is | Looks like | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Sprinter] | A straight burst over ten meters | The floor blurs and he's simply there | Once a fight, twice if he's lucky. It spends the lungs |
| [Quick Reflexes] | His body moves before he decides: a flinch, a slip, a catch | He's already moving when the hand closes | Reaction, not skill. He gets out of a grab and can't follow it up. He also flinches at every gunshot |
| [Field Craft: Deer Cut] | Forty years of dressing game: the back of the knee, opened in one move | Low, past the reaching arms, the blade still moving | Drops a turned adult to one leg; doesn't kill it. He has to be close. His thumbs are eighteen and slip, and the blade sticks |
| [Field Craft: Joint Cut] | The tendons on the inside of a wrist, cut across, so a grip opens | The hand on him goes loose all at once, like a dropped glove | He has to turn into the grab, not away, to reach the wrist. It frees him; it doesn't stop the thing |
| [Field Craft: Tracking] | Reads prints, scuffs, drips and blood | He has the count before the radar does | Needs a still minute and some light |
| Pistol (Quick Learner) | Kiana's spare, after the deal. Her drills in a week; he strips it faster than she does by the second Saturday | Arms locked, weight forward, breathes out before he squeezes | Hits what's close and slow. One magazine and no spare unless she hands him one. Never at school |

- **How a fight with one goes:** he turns into the grab, cuts, cuts again, and finishes it with the pistol if he has it. Running is a last resort, not his move.
- **Also:** the box cutter from the station, always in his coat; what the
  game taught him about turned people, which he uses and can't explain.
- **Can't:** kill a turned adult one on one with a blade; fight two at once;
  last; fight alongside Kiana as a team; touch a Herrscher except once,
  desperately, and it costs him.

## Kiana Kaslana, Nagazora (December 2013 to February 2014)

**The strongest thing in the port at night that isn't turned.** Trained by
her father since she could walk, and immune to Honkai corruption (the core
she doesn't know about).

- **Weapons.** Her father's two USP pistols, and his spare, which she lends
  Eros. She counts rounds; ammunition is money she doesn't have.
- **Body.** Immune to the bad air; falls well (she taught him); fast,
  fearless and reckless.
- **Named moves.** She makes them up as she goes and renames them every
  time; "Kaslana Special" is whatever she's doing when she shouts it. What
  she actually does:
  - two-gun fire, fast, from anywhere (on a counter, off the top of a
    container, upside down);
  - the flying kick her father made her practice ten thousand times on a
    tire;
  - the slide, under things and through legs;
  - dropping onto things from above (the drain);
  - fighting dirty: knees, elbows, her forehead.
- **Limits.** Ammunition and reload time; she's one girl; she charges; she
  isn't technical (when the radar breaks she stuffs foil in it); she doesn't
  stop to breathe, so she doesn't notice when he can't.
- **The bat.** From the new year she carries a dented aluminum bat out of a
  skip behind the batting cages, pushed through her backpack straps like a
  sword, to save rounds. It isn't with her at school; on the stairs during
  the Eruption she uses a fire extinguisher instead.

## Turned people (Nagazora, 2013 to 2014)

People the bad air has turned: dockworkers, night crews, squatters, and
during the Eruption, students and teachers. Fresh Honkai zombies in the
terms of [honkai-beasts.md](honkai-beasts.md).

- Pink light in the eyes. They cough pink first, then stop coughing and
  stand up.
- Stronger than they were, and they feel nothing. A grip that doesn't let
  go.
- They shamble, then run in a straight line once they've seen you. They go
  for noise and movement.
- They stop when the legs go (then they crawl) or the head does. Bullets to
  the body only slow them.
- No tricks, no learning. Dangerous in numbers, in the dark and in small
  spaces.
- Kiana drops one with two shots. Eros can put one on one knee.

## The Herrscher of Thunder, newborn (Loop 4, the roof, February 4)

See [characters/herrscher-of-thunder.md](characters/herrscher-of-thunder.md)
and the canon beats from the author's research. She uses her power like a
child with a hammer at first and learns fast. She speaks little, in
commands and contempt, and never explains herself. The System puts up her
big moves when Eros sees them; she names them herself in a word or two.
Names marked (Novel) are taken from the author's Novel repo, with the
author's approval.

| Move | What it is | Looks like / sound | What gets past it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrath of the Violet Heaven (Novel) | The storm coming off her body | Sheets of lightning crawling over the concrete; the wire fence glows and runs like wax. *KRZZZT* | No aim; it's weather |
| Thunderbird | Her opener. One huge bolt shaped like a bird, out of the purple cloud | White and screaming, wings folded back. It can pull out of its dive and chase. *KRAKA-THOOM* | Lead it into something big (in Loop 4, the water tank) |
| Violet Sanctum (Novel) | A reflex barrier a meter out from her body | Bullets burn up in it like moths in a lamp | It burns what's fast. Something slow and held gets through, and the metal heats and the hand burns |
| Crescent | The wing sweeps the roof | A purple crescent, low and flat; vents and fence posts cut like paper. *SHHAANG* | Low and flat; you can slide under it |
| The catch | Takes hold and drains (a leg in Loop 4, a throat in Loop 5) | Lightning up the caught limb into the body | Kiana's blood drinks it |
| Blackout | She drinks the city: every light in Nagazora goes out street by street and the power rises into her | The city going dark from the harbor up, a thin rain of sparks rising into the storm | Nothing on the roof stops it. It mends her wing and charges her finisher |
| Purple Lightning, First Seal: Heaven Strikes the Unworthy (Novel) | Her finisher, after "Then die as one." Two hundred bolts out of the violet sky onto one spot | The whole sky lights up from inside in a net of white, every strand turns to one spot, then comes down. *KRAKA-THOOOOM* | One spot. Whatever is pushed out of it lives |

- **The wing root** is her weak point (canon: Kiana tears the wing off).
- Her lightning is a soul attack (see
  [soul-and-fate.md](soul-and-fate.md)).

## The Raiden sword (story)

The Raiden house's sword is a battlefield art, centuries older than kendo,
made to kill armored men in one or two cuts. It doesn't block and it doesn't
wait for an opening: every form is an attack, and a form thrown against an
incoming blow cuts through it. Kendo is what it became once nobody needed
it. Raiden Ryoma taught all of it to Mei as Mr. HOMU. With the Herrscher's
power behind it, the same forms are lightning.

| Form | What it does | Looks like / sound |
|---|---|---|
| First Form: Shiden (Purple Lightning) | The draw: one cut from the sheath or from low, through the throat. The fastest kill in the style | A line of light where the blade was; the cut lands before the step does. *SHHK* |
| Second Form: Ikazuchi (Thunderbolt) | Two hands, straight down through whatever is raised against it (an arm, a pipe, a sword) and on into the collarbone | Like a dropped guillotine. *GOOOM* |
| Third Form: Narukami (Thunder God) | Low and forward, under the ribs to the heart through the gap in armor, and a turn | Through. *THK* |
| Fourth Form: Hekireki (Thunderclap) | Three cuts in one step: throat, wrist, thigh. One sound made of three | Mei's. *KRAK* |
| The last form | Her father never finished teaching it to her | |

## Eros, Mei run, Loop 5

**Rating: Civilian.** The same body, the same gifts and the same Field Craft
as Loop 4, but no hunts and no gun: he never goes where Kiana goes. What's
new is the Raiden sword. Mei taught him the shapes of the first three forms
at ten, in a hospital corridor at midnight with rolled-up magazines, and he
practiced them every night for six years in Osaka. From September 2013 he
trains with her at dawn in her house dojo, and joins the club in October.
In January she teaches the forms as what they are, with her father's sword
on straw: he cuts like a boy with a magazine, stopping at the skin, until
"Through. Not to. Through." Quick Learner and the years give him the
shapes; nothing gives him the arms. By February 4 he can kill with the
first three forms.

| Move | Limit |
|---|---|
| [Raiden, First Form: Shiden] | Fast for a boy, slow for the style. With wood it crushes instead of cutting |
| [Raiden, Second Form: Ikazuchi] | All his weight in one cut; for a breath afterwards his arms are lead |
| [Raiden, Third Form: Narukami] | Needs his whole body behind it. With a practice sword it breaks ribs instead of going through them |
| Hekireki and the last form | Out of his reach |

- **Weapons:** an umbrella when that's what he has; from January a practice
  sword in a cloth bag, carried every day. Wood breaks bones, then breaks
  itself, and it burns.
- **His body is the limit,** not the forms: three forms in a row and his arms
  shake. Every move he makes is an attack; there's no waiting.
- **Still:** Quick Reflexes, Field Craft (no box cutter at school), Sprinter
  for one burst.
