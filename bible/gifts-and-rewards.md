# Gifts and Rewards

## Gifts

Gifts are traits, never upgrades. A gift gives him a trait he didn't have;
it never "raises" a stat. Items use the same color tiers.

| Color | Meaning | Draw rate | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| White | A normal human trait | 50% | Light Sleeper, Iron Stomach |
| Green | A talented human trait, top 1% | 28% | Quick Learner, Perfect Pitch |
| Blue | Peak human, borderline superhuman | 14% | Photographic Memory, Pain Tolerance |
| Purple | Superhuman, Valkyrie-tier | 6% | Honkai Resistance, Danger Sense, Minor Regeneration |
| Gold | Exceptional, S-rank-adjacent; defines a fighting style | 1.8% | Lightning Affinity, Sword Saint's Eye |
| Red | Herrscher-adjacent; touches reality itself, usually with a drawback | 0.2% | A fragment of an authority, Imaginary Sense |

- **Hidden gifts** show as ??? until a trigger reveals their color.
- **Draws:** 5 at the start of each run; he keeps 2 for that run. In a
  heroine run he chooses once, at the start, and keeps that build through
  every loop and reset. He doesn't rechoose.
- **No limit on permanent gifts.** He can keep as many as he earns. The
  grade of each run is what holds him back (see Settlement).
- **Upgrades:** merging two copies of the same gift raises it one color.
  Merging can reach Gold but never Red; Red can only be drawn.
- **The male ceiling:** physical gifts can't push him past the rank his
  Resistance (or Adaptability) allows.

### Honkai Resistance (gift)

A trait like any other: his body withstands Honkai energy at that grade.

| Color | Resistance |
|---|---|
| Blue | Low D: the lowest Valkyrie-tier body; hours in Eruption-level air |
| Purple | Low B: a frontline body |
| Gold | Low A: the top of what resistance can reach |

- There is no White or Green version, and no gift gives C. C comes from an
  artificial stigma lifting him (see [power-scale.md](power-scale.md),
  "Artificial stigmata and Resistance").
- Merging two copies gives the next color as usual.
- It's a ceiling, not power. With B Resistance and no training he's still a
  Civilian, only one who's allowed to grow into a frontline Valkyrie.
- With an artificial stigma, Resistance can climb to A by training and
  combat anyway. The gift's grade decides how cheap that climb is (see
  [power-scale.md](power-scale.md), "How he climbs").

### Honkai Adaptability (Red gift)

- **Effect:** wakes Honkai Adaptability at C. From there it grows through
  exposure and fighting like anyone's, and his dormant natural stigma can
  wake with it.
- **Drawback:** the Honkai starts to notice him. Honkai activity drifts
  toward wherever he is, beasts single him out, and the higher his
  Adaptability climbs, the closer he gets to being a vessel.
- **Rarity:** Red, so it can only be drawn (0.2%) and only kept from an
  S-grade run. It can't be made by merging.
- Together with body modifications (a MANTIS fusion or a fused core), it's
  one of the only two ways a man gets Adaptability.

## Settlement

Every run ends with a grade from F to S. The grade decides everything he
can take out: how many picks, how rare a gift can be, how deep a skill goes,
which items are allowed, and how many Fate Fragments he's paid.

### What decides the grade

- **Normal runs:** how long he lived, what he achieved, how much he changed
  in that life, and any hidden events he triggered (witnessing or surviving
  something historic, killing something far above his level).
- **Heroine runs:** how many objectives he completed and how high the
  meters ended (see Heroine runs below).

### What each grade gives

| Grade | Typical run | Picks | Gift colors he can keep | Experience depth | Item colors | Fate Fragments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | Died almost at once, did nothing | 1 of 3 | White | **Scraps:** a few days of know-how | White | 10–20 |
| E | Short life or early death, little done | 1 of 3 | White, Green | **Basic:** a trainee's level, weeks of training | White | 20–40 |
| D | A modest life, or a real survival | 1 of 3 | Up to Green | **Competent:** a year or two of real work | Up to Green | 40–80 |
| C | A long life, or a real achievement, or a hidden event | 2 of 3 | Up to Blue | **Professional:** years of practice | Up to Blue | 80–150 |
| B | A remarkable life that changed something; several hidden events | 2 of 4 | Up to Purple | **Expert:** decades, the top of a field | Up to Purple | 150–300 |
| A | An extraordinary life that changed history | 3 of 5 | Up to Gold | **Master:** among the best who ever lived | Up to Gold | 300–500 |
| S | A life that reshaped its whole era; almost never happens | 4 of 6 | Up to Red | **Legend:** a level nobody in that world reached | Up to Red | 500–1000 |

- **Gifts above the grade's color are never offered.** If he drew a Purple
  and the run ends at D, the Purple is gone.
- **Red gifts** can only be drawn, and can only be kept from an S-grade run.
- **There are no stat raises.** Resistance and Adaptability change only
  through gifts and body modifications.
- **Fate Fragments** are always paid. One of the cards can instead be
  **double payout** for that run.
- **What's on the cards:** the gifts he held during the run, the skills he
  built in it, and items he had on him. The offer is drawn from those, so a
  run where he learned nothing can't offer a skill.

### Heroine runs

- **Objectives** are only the conditions for finishing the run. Completing
  one means his next death ends the run as a success.
- **The grade** comes from hidden criteria the System never lists in
  advance. They measure how deep he went with her, not whether he
  finished:
  1. **Love:** how far she fell for him (Affection).
  2. **Corruption:** how far she'd go for him. The darker she is, and the
     more she's his, the higher the grade.
  3. **Staying:** whether he's still alive at her side after the objective.

| Grade | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| F–E | The objective barely made it. She hardly knows him. |
| D | She likes him, and that's all. |
| C | Fond or In Love, but still Untouched, and he died right after the objective. |
| B | In Love and Bending: she'd break rules for him. He lived past the objective. |
| A | In Love and Compromised: she'd turn on other people for him. He stayed with her. |
| S | In Love and Fallen: she's his completely. Both alive and together at the end. |

- **Hidden events:** a few moments per heroine that the System loves. Each
  one hit raises the grade a step, two steps at most.
- **Settlement** then uses the same table as normal runs (picks, colors,
  experience depth, Fate Fragments).
- **Extras on the cards:** a **bond gift** unique to her (its color follows
  Love and Corruption) and her personal item.
- **Corruption is non-explicit.** It's devotion turning dark (jealousy,
  obsession, choosing him over everyone and everything), shown on the page
  through what she does, never as sexual content.

## Reward types

- **Keep a gift:** add one gift from the run to his permanent gifts.
- **Experience package:** skills and knowledge from the run (swordsmanship,
  languages, combat instinct) arrive as muscle memory, at the depth the
  grade allows. Technique can exceed what his body can actually execute.
- **Item:** an object pulled out of the run. One per pick, nothing alive,
  nothing larger than he can carry. Divine Keys and Herrscher cores can't be
  taken.
- **Body modification:** a surgery or fusion he went through in the run and
  was still carrying when it ended (an artificial stigma, a World Serpent
  chimera procedure, a MANTIS fusion, a fused beast core). It arrives
  already inside his body, healed and working. Rules:
  - Everything it costs comes with it: an artificial stigma's shortened
    life and cascade risk; a MANTIS fusion's beast traits, visible changes
    (Elysia's ears) and instincts.
  - His real body has to be able to survive it. If it can't, the card shows
    grayed out ("Requires Honkai Resistance E"). He sees it and can't take
    it.
  - Colors: standard Schicksal artificial stigma (grade C) Purple; top
    quality (grade B) Gold; World
    Serpent chimera Purple to Gold; MANTIS fusion Gold, Red if the beast was
    bound to a Herrscher. Grade caps apply as for items.
  - A natural stigma, a Herrscher core and a Divine Key can't be taken.
  - It shows on scans.
- **Double payout:** twice the run's Fate Fragments.
