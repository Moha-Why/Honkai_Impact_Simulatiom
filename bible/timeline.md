# Timeline

Canon dates with this story's changes. Later dates get filled in as the
story reaches them.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1452 or 1477 | Otto Apocalypse is born (sources disagree). He takes over Schicksal after Kallen Kaslana's death and is over 500 years old by 2009. |
| 1972 | Otto's A-3 experiments merge the Emperor-class beast Vishnu's genes with Kallen Kaslana's cells (canon). |
| 1973, Christmas Eve | The Emperor-class beast Parvati appears. Theresa, rated B, kills it alone (canon). |
| 1991, March | Eros is found as a newborn at the gate of the Willow Street Children's Home, Hangzhou. Director Zhou registers him as "Eros". |
| 1995 | Kiana, Mei, Bronya, Seele and Bianka (Durandal) are born (moved earlier from canon so they are 18 by the Third Eruption). Rita Rossweisse is born March 1 (canon year). Mei: April 13. Bronya: August 18. Kiana (K-423): December 7. Bianka: December 7 is her real, secret birthday (she is the real Kiana Kaslana); her records say January 1, 1995. |
| 1997 | Eros, six, is caught doing Director Zhou's accounts in his head. She gets him into school early. |
| 2000, February 16 | Second Eruption, Siberia (canon date). Sirin, the Herrscher of the Void, spreads it across Siberia; fewer than 100 residents survive. Cecilia Schariac acts against it the next day and dies in it. The Emperor-class Ashvins and the Judgment-class dragon Benares are born on that battlefield; Welt Yang kills the Ashvins. Chinese news reports a disaster in northern Russia with a closed zone; Eros is eight. |
| 2006 | Canon: Murata Himeko receives artificial stigmata and joins Schicksal's Valkyries, later the Valkyrie Assault Squad. |
| 2006 | Eros starts university in Hangzhou at fifteen. Professor Han becomes his advisor. |
| 2007 | Director Zhou dies of a stroke. She leaves Eros the original note. |
| 2008 | Eros starts the anomaly program. |
| 2009, March | Eros, eighteen, uploads his findings. Schicksal drafts him the next evening and flies him to the Far East Branch technical center on St. Freya's island. On his first day the core gives him a shock and nothing else. |
| 2009 | Theresa (S-rank) and her grandfather Otto are at odds. |
| 2009, June 21 | Canon: Theresa transfers to the Far East Branch, away from Otto, and founds St. Freya (some sources say St. Freya is older). |
| 2007 | Canon: Ragna saves 11-year-old Rita in a Honkai incident in Manchester. |
| 2010 | Canon: the Valkyrie Assault Squad (Himeko's squad) is nearly wiped out on a mission and disbanded. Ragna dies. |
| 2011, late | Himeko recruits Bianka (real age 16) for St. Freya; she chooses HQ instead. Eros glimpses her on the eleventh floor. |
| 2012 | Canon: the Emperor-class beast Ganesha appears in India; two A-ranks die. Bianka is the sole survivor of her first mission (Agra Fort). Eros is promoted to senior engineer and moves to a sea-view staff flat. |
| 2013, January to July | Eros's secondment to Schicksal HQ (Germany): Rita Rossweisse is his liaison; he writes Bianka's battlesuit firmware; Rita is newly assigned to Bianka. Rita turns 18 on March 1. |
| 2013 | The four heroines turn 18. Mei is in her third year of high school in Nagazora. |
| 2013, November 16 | Canon: Raiden Ryoma, CEO of ME Corp, is arrested for fraud, framed by Cocolia. Mei cuts herself off. Kiana enrolls at Chiba Academy afterward (in this story, mid-December, after her 18th birthday). Canon: she gets past Mei's guard, defends her from bullies and eats her (bad) first homemade lunches. |
| 2014, mid-January | The System binds to Eros during a core surge as Honkai readings climb around Nagazora. Run 1 that night (Siberia, March 1999 to the Second Eruption). |
| 2014, January 15–16 | Theresa visits the anomaly watch room and decides to send Himeko to Nagazora quietly. Runs 2 and 3. |
| 2014, late January | Himeko leaves for Nagazora. Run 4 (January 25). |
| 2014, February 3, 1:11 a.m. | The biggest Nagazora spike yet. Mei's heroine simulation unlocks. Eros doesn't start it. |
| 2014, February | Third Eruption, Nagazora (canon date), after Mei's partial awakening as the Herrscher of Thunder. Himeko confronts Mei; Theresa later brings Kiana to St. Freya. Canon: Cocolia and Bronya set off an artificial outbreak at Chiba Academy to wake the Gem of Conquest in Mei. Mei's run is set before it: Chiba Academy, Nagazora, her third year, starting September or October 2013 depending on the loop. |
