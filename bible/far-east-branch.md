# The Far East Branch

How Schicksal's Far East Branch is laid out and how advanced it is. Theresa
Apocalypse heads the whole branch from June 2009.

## Two sites

- **The technical center island.** Reclaimed from a reef in 1998 to 1999.
  About 5 km by 2 km, seawall all round, about 400 staff. R&D (battlesuit
  firmware, simulation), the anomaly watch (ninth floor), logistics, the
  computing core in the basement, a runway, a helipad, a harbor, and from
  2013 a mooring tower for the Hyperion. Cover story: a wind research
  station; staff are "Lindgren Automation" engineers. Ferry to Shiomi
  (1 h 20) on Tuesdays and Fridays.
- **St. Freya's island.** Built for Theresa by Otto in 2006 to 2008, two
  hours south by helicopter. The academy opens in September 2009. Students
  are 16 to 18, in three years (first intake placed by age). Every new
  intake spends a week at the technical center for suit fittings and tests
  first. Eros's team builds and maintains St. Freya's training simulators.

## The world's technology (everyone, not just Schicksal)

The HI3 world is ahead of real-world Earth, and the gap grows every year:
a few years further on for each year that passes. Civilian life too:
phones, computers, cars, media, games. Rough guide:

- **2000:** about where Earth was in the early 2000s, a little ahead.
- **2009:** civilian tech feels like Earth around 2014 to 2015.
- **2011:** like Earth around 2017 (motion-rig arcades, handhelds ahead of
  Earth's 2011). Physical media is mostly memory sticks, not CDs.
- **2014:** close to light science fiction for civilians.
- Eros died on Earth in his own era (the 2020s), so in 2009 the world still
  feels slightly behind what he remembers; by 2011 to 2012 it has caught up
  with his first life, and by 2014 it has passed it.
- Schicksal's own tech (below) is further ahead again.

## Technology level

Schicksal is decades ahead of the 2009 world in anything that fights the
Honkai, and deliberately ordinary in the staff's daily life. Both are on
purpose.

**Ahead:**
- **Honkai detection:** mature. Detectors along the coasts and the seismic,
  atmospheric and satellite feeds catch every outbreak the moment it
  starts; nobody misses a big one. The Honkai itself stays unpredictable;
  nothing tells you where or when the next one will begin. The weak point
  in 2010 is triage: about 400 alarms a week, mostly storms, ferries and
  industry, checked by hand, so small real events sit in the pile until
  they're not small. Eros's anomaly watch program (live September 2011) is
  a sorter: it fuses the feeds and says within a minute whether a small
  signal is a real event. It does not predict. At Pohang (October 2011) it
  flags a small real event offshore three hours before the beasts come
  ashore.
- **Simulation rooms:** empty white halls that the core fills with hard
  light and shaped Honkai energy. Terrain and beasts are solid; you can
  stand on them, break them and bleed on them. HQ's rooms are the same kind.
- **The computing core:** the island's brain. Runs the simulation rooms,
  the anomaly watch models and suit firmware updates. "Faster than anything
  you've ever used by a stupid amount" (Mori, Chapter 1).
- **Battlesuits:** built on gantries by crane-sized robot arms that thread
  nerve-like fiber through the joints; test suits walk the track empty.
- **Valkyrie transports:** unmarked dark gray tilt-rotors, much faster than
  any civilian craft; Korean civilian radar logs one as a weather fault
  (Pohang, 2011). Cargo still goes by heavy helicopter.
- **The Hyperion:** the branch's flying battleship. Built at HQ in the
  open-sky dock on the industrial island (Eros sees it in its cradle in
  January 2013 and watches it leave in May), then moored at the technical
  center's north tower. Theresa calls
  it hers; HQ calls it theirs. Canon: sent to Nagazora in February 2014 to
  investigate the spike, and Kiana, Mei and Bronya are taken aboard.
- **Stigmata:** artificial stigmata are given by transplant surgery, once,
  to girls whose natural compatibility is too low. They shorten the
  lifespan. Battlesuits feed them through the suit's reactor.

**Deliberately ordinary:**
- **The ferry:** off-duty staff travel by ferry because Shiomi's harbor
  office logs every boat, and a "wind research station" whose staff fly
  around in tilt-rotors would end up in the newspapers.
- **Money:** everything on the island is paid with the staff badge. The
  one ATM (harbor building) exists only for cash to spend in Shiomi. Eros
  draws the same amount every Friday out of his own paranoia; as far as he
  knows, nobody checks.
- **Supply requests:** everything physical that arrives or leaves goes
  through a request with a stated reason (the supply officer's screen
  stamps a red seal, an old IT joke). Ordinary bookkeeping.
- **The ferry list:** everyone's departure and return time is written
  down, for the boat's head count.
- **The staff network** is monitored (Vogel, Chapter 1). Mail goes via the
  Hamburg cover address and is read.

## Schicksal HQ (for reference)

- **Current HQ:** a cluster of giant floating islands above the
  Mediterranean (canon, Moegirl: 地中海上空巨型浮空岛群). The administrative
  center and core research facility. The main island has a runway, towers
  of glass and steel, and a cathedral on its highest ground with Otto's
  portrait in the main hall; smaller islands (residential, industrial) are
  joined by skybridges, with transports running between them like buses.
  Several thousand people live and work there; about sixty new secondees
  arrive in January 2013 alone. The Battlesuit Division (about 200
  engineers) is here; Eros is one of twelve in Klaus's simulation group,
  with Permafrost (Siberia), Kangaroo (Oceania) and Sand (North Africa
  labs) as the other newcomers. Eros's 2013
  secondment is here; he runs the eight-kilometer rim path.
- **Old HQ:** Kolosten town, outside Vienna, Austria (canon:
  奥地利维也纳柯洛斯滕镇). On land. A cathedral and cloister on a hill
  above the town, kept by a small staff. The archive and the old portraits,
  including the Kaslana row and Siegfried's empty frame, stayed here. Rita
  flies Eros down to it in February 2013.
- Staff cover story for HQ postings: "Germany" (Lindgren Automation,
  Hamburg).

## Valkyrie life

- Valkyries live and fight almost entirely among women, through life and
  death, so relationships between them are common and ordinary. On the
  technical center island about half the Valkyries are with another girl,
  and nobody remarks on it. Asami: "You fly into a burning city with
  somebody and you fly back out... Who else are you going to love? A man
  from Accounts who's never been shot at?" The ones who marry men mostly do
  it after they leave the front line.
- Canon couple on the page: Mao and Ji-woo (adults), together from the
  winter of 2011 to 2012.
- Adults' relationships are shown through affection (arriving together, a
  hand in a back pocket, sharing a chair); anything more stays off the page.
  St. Freya students (16 to 18) get crushes and YA-level romance at most,
  and only with each other (see [content-rules.md](content-rules.md)).
- What it means for Eros: a desk engineer who's never been shot at is
  "safe to drink with", not someone to fall for. Being handsome gets him
  glances, not love.

## How closely Schicksal watches Eros (ruling)

Not much, and that's the point. He's a junior engineer recruited after a
data leak. The staff network is monitored for everyone. Brenner checks in
once, in March 2010 (the Kaslana question), and his four-year review of
Eros as a leak recruit ends routinely in March 2013 with a one-line email.
Rita becomes his liaison at HQ because she has just been assigned to
Bianka and he writes Bianka's suit firmware, not because Otto is watching
him. Otto glances at him once, as he glances at everyone.

The paranoia is Eros's own. He's a fan who knows what Otto is, so he hides
from a man who hasn't noticed him. Keep it that way until the System,
Fate Deviation or his runs give someone a real reason to look; that moment
should land because nothing like it came before.
