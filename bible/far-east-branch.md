# The Far East Branch

How Schicksal's Far East Branch is laid out and how advanced it is. Theresa
Apocalypse heads the whole branch from June 2009.

## Two sites

- **The technical center island.** Reclaimed from a reef in 1998 to 1999.
  About 5 km by 2 km, seawall all round, about 400 staff. R&D (battlesuit
  firmware, simulation), the anomaly watch (ninth floor), logistics, the
  computing core in the basement, a runway, a helipad, a harbor, and from
  2013 a mooring tower for the Hyperion. Cover story: a wind research
  station; staff are "Lindgren Automation" engineers. Ferry to Shiomi
  (1 h 20) on Tuesdays and Fridays.
- **St. Freya's island.** Built for Theresa by Otto in 2006 to 2008, two
  hours south by helicopter. The academy opens in September 2009. Every new
  intake spends a week at the technical center for suit fittings and tests
  first. Eros's team builds and maintains St. Freya's training simulators.

## Technology level

Schicksal is decades ahead of the 2009 world in anything that fights the
Honkai, and deliberately ordinary in the staff's daily life. Both are on
purpose.

**Ahead:**
- **Simulation rooms:** empty white halls that the core fills with hard
  light and shaped Honkai energy. Terrain and beasts are solid; you can
  stand on them, break them and bleed on them. HQ's rooms are the same kind.
- **The computing core:** the island's brain. Runs the simulation rooms,
  the anomaly watch models and suit firmware updates. "Faster than anything
  you've ever used by a stupid amount" (Mori, Chapter 1).
- **Battlesuits:** built on gantries by crane-sized robot arms that thread
  nerve-like fiber through the joints; test suits walk the track empty.
- **Valkyrie transports:** unmarked dark gray tilt-rotors, much faster than
  any civilian craft; Korean civilian radar logs one as a weather fault
  (Pohang, 2011). Cargo still goes by heavy helicopter.
- **The Hyperion:** the branch's flying battleship. Arrives from the yards
  in May 2013 and moors at the technical center's north tower. Theresa calls
  it hers; HQ calls it theirs. Canon: sent to Nagazora in February 2014 to
  investigate the spike, and Kiana, Mei and Bronya are taken aboard.
- **Stigmata and genetics:** artificial stigmata are grown and fitted in
  labs.

**Deliberately ordinary:**
- **The ferry:** off-duty staff travel by ferry because Shiomi's harbor
  office logs every boat, and a "wind research station" whose staff fly
  around in tilt-rotors would end up in the newspapers.
- **Money:** everything on the island is paid with the staff badge, and
  every payment is in a ledger Security can read. The one ATM (harbor
  building) exists only for cash to spend in Shiomi. Regular withdrawals are
  visible, which is why Eros draws the same amount every Friday.
- **The paper trail:** everything physical that arrives or leaves goes
  through the supply office on paper forms in triplicate, with a stated
  reason and a stamp, because paper can't be hacked. One copy goes to
  Security.
- **The staff network** is monitored (Vogel, Chapter 1). Mail goes via the
  Hamburg cover address and is read.
