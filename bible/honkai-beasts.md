# Honkai Beasts

Built from Moegirl's Chinese entry and the Honkai Impact 3rd Archives wiki
(which quotes the in-game Collection entries). Story rulings are marked.

## What they are

- **Agents of the Will of Honkai**, alongside Herrschers and Honkai zombies
  (死士, "Deadites").
- **Energy bodies.** In 3rd, beasts are condensed Honkai energy. They are
  silicon-based, unlike carbon-based humans, and their shells are very hard
  to break.
- **Look:** usually white armor with glowing pink highlights. Frost variants
  have black armor with cyan highlights.
- **Herrschers multiply them.** When a Herrscher is born, the number of
  beasts in its area rises, and beasts are completely loyal to their own
  Herrscher. Even with two Herrschers in one area, neither can command the
  other's beasts.
- **Origins (older lore):** animals mutated by Honkai energy. The Archives
  wiki calls these primitive "ancient Honkai beasts", seen as far back as
  2717 BC.
- **Cores:** Emperor-class and above have a core. Judgment-class beasts have
  several cores, and destroying one doesn't stop them.
- **They grow.** A beast's class reflects its strength when it was assessed
  or killed. Vishnu was classed Emperor only because of its strength when it
  was killed; its real ability was devouring other beasts and taking their
  power.

## Honkai zombies

Corpses reanimated by the Honkai as disposable infantry. The weakest
Honkai-powered enemies; C-rank Valkyries dispatch them easily. Types include
scythe-wielders (Vigiles, Praetorian), archers (Ranger, Archer), ninja
(Jun-Ninja, Kunoichi), summoners (Revenant, Lich), clawed (Razor Claw) and
others. A Valkyrie with poor Honkai adaptability can turn into one (the
Honkai cascade).

## Lower beasts (no core)

**Classed by form, not strength.** There's no strict order between lower
classes; canon says a Hasty-class isn't necessarily weaker than a
Knight-class. Each form has weak and strong variants.

| Form | What it is | Canon notes |
|---|---|---|
| Archangel types (Seraph-class) | Small, common flying beasts that come in crowds. | Called the weakest class; B-rank Valkyries handle them. Baradiel, the ice variant, was everywhere in Siberia during the Second Eruption. |
| Hasty (突进级) | Fast charging beasts. | Includes Hyper Hasty and elemental variants. |
| Knight (骑士级) | Mobile beasts with lances and multi-stage attacks. | Paladin (flaming lance), Frost Knight, Nocturnal Knight. "Avoid head-to-head confrontation." |
| Ballista (弩炮级) | Ranged siege forms that lob shots (the Catapult types). | Flame, Frost and Lightning variants. |
| Chariot (战车级) | Heavy, slow beasts with a charged attack. | **The most common Honkai beast**, often in contaminated cities. B-rank Valkyries handle them. Alloy Chariot (pounce, deafening roar), Blazing Chariot, Frost Chariot (cold regions). |
| Floating, Tidal | Floating and water forms. | Little detail in canon. |
| Templar (圣殿级) | Shielded beasts whose shields block physical attacks. | B-rank squads or a single A-rank. "Valkyries below A-rank should engage them in squads." Storm Templar is an evolved type. |
| Thor-class (雷神级) | Lightning forms. | Little detail. |
| Warlord (督军级) | Commander forms. | Little detail. |
| Honkai Emperor (崩坏帝王) | The apex of the lower beasts. | A separate lower-beast type. **Not** the same as Emperor-class. |

## Upper beasts (have a core)

### Emperor-class

Huge, cored, made purely of Honkai energy, and far stronger than any lower
beast. Once a beast is judged Emperor-class, Schicksal sends an A-rank or
several Valkyries together; the Archives wiki says a lineup of A-ranks.

| Beast | Canon |
|---|---|
| Parvati | First sighted Christmas Eve 1973. Controls ice and snow. Killed alone by Theresa while she was B-rank, after she refused to retreat. An A-rank named Kali fought it too. |
| Ashvins | Appeared on the Second Eruption battlefield in Siberia, 2000. Splits into two, Son of Dawn and Son of Darkness. Strong enough to match Siegfried Kaslana; killed by Welt Yang. |
| Vishnu | In 1972 Otto merged Vishnu's genes with Kallen Kaslana's cells in the A-3 experiments. Devours other beasts and takes their power. |
| Ganesha | First sighted in India, 2012. Killed two A-ranks and badly wounded twelve Valkyries. "Requires special forces or S-rank Valkyries to handle." |
| Raziel, Ramiel | Their cores were made into Schicksal's Cross weapons. |
| Soma | Caused heavy casualties fighting the Dawn and Dusk squad. |
| Others | Assaka (born in the Second Eruption, splits in two), Imperator, Queen, Flame Emperor, Frost Emperor, Padishah, Asura, Mahamayuri, Sumo. |

### Judgment-class

Can cause disasters on the scale of natural catastrophes, with bodies far
beyond any known species. They have several cores, and only Divine
Key-level weapons and a large team can destroy all of them. The Archives
wiki says they need teams of Valkyries, ideally with a few S-ranks.

Only two are confirmed:

- **Chiyou:** appeared in China about 5,200 years ago and kept swallowing
  cities. Sealed at the cost of two lives. Kevin absorbs it in 2017.
- **Benares:** a dragon born with Sirin, the Herrscher of the Void, in the
  Second Eruption, and her loyal subordinate. Reawakens in 2016 and dies
  fighting Husk: Nihilius in Nagazora (canon dates).

### Vipralopa-class (末法级)

A pre-era classification only. Maheśvara (fused with Elysia) and Vishnu
(fused with Kosma, and considered Vipralopa in the pre-era because of its
devouring ability). In the current era, Vishnu's class can rise as it eats
other beasts.

## Using beasts in the story

- **Story ruling, Run 1:** the Second Eruption has crowds of Baradiel, and
  Eros is killed by a Frost Chariot. The Ashvins and Benares are on that
  battlefield too, out of his sight.
- **Lower beasts** are the everyday threat in a run, and cause most of
  Eros's ordinary deaths early on.
- **An Emperor-class beast** is a boss for a whole arc. Before Eros reaches
  A rank, he can only survive one by avoiding it, outsmarting it, or getting
  someone else to kill it.
- **Cores** are among the few items that can raise Honkai Adaptability. They
  are also among the most likely to kill him.
