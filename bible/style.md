# Style

- Written in English. No Chinese text; genre terms are written in English
  (for example, "[Ding!]" for System notices).
- First person, from Eros, past tense.
- Comedic on the surface, with a dark undertone.
- This is a personal novel written for its author, who is its only reader.
  The author's enjoyment sets the pacing and structure.
- Long, immersive chapters are intentional. Preserve their length and scope;
  do not split or shorten chapters merely to fit publishing conventions.
  Develop scenes and transitions within the long chapter as needed.
- The System is cunning and playful: teasing, evasive about itself, and good
  at making the next run appealing. It usually uses no form of address;
  avoid repetitive "Host" endings. See [characters/the-system.md](characters/the-system.md).
- Power progression is central: every run should move his strength forward.
- The shura field, Eros surviving the heroines finding out about each other,
  is a major source of tension and comedy.
