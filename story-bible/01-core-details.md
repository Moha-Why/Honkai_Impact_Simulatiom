# Story Bible — Core Details (draft, pre-premise)

Status: details are still being chosen. No premise yet. Items marked **[DECIDE]** are open.

---

## 1. Eros — the protagonist

**Previous life (our Earth)**
- Software engineer, 29, and a long-time Honkai Impact 3rd player who knew the lore well. His dream was to win every heroine.
- **Death:** he collapsed at his desk at ~3 AM during a release crunch, with HI3 open on the second monitor. Overwork, heart failure.
- **The hook:** the **last ~24 hours before his death are missing** from his memory. He can't remember that day at all. That gap is connected to the System and its creator, and it pays off much later.

**New life (the Honkai world)**
- **Born 1991 in Thessaloniki, Greece.** Father is a Greek structural engineer; mother is a Chinese translator. He grows up bilingual (Greek/English + Mandarin), which later makes him useful to both Schicksal HQ in Europe and the Far East Branch. The mixed heritage also helps explain the looks.
- **Age 9 at the Second Eruption (Siberia, 2000).** The public only gets a cover story. **He believes this is a normal Earth**, and the odd differences (brands that don't exist, slightly different history) he writes off as a Mandela effect.
- He never wrote down any notes about the game. When the truth hits, he has to dig up 20-year-old memories of the lore. They are patchy and sometimes wrong.
- **The genius path:** university at 14 (computer science), a master's degree by 18, and by 20 he is known for an open-source distributed-systems / cryptography project and a string of competition wins.
- **The draft (2012, age 21):** a recruitment offer arrives from a shell company. During the interview he sees the Schicksal eagle crest. Inner monologue: *"Oh no." …then… "Oh. YES."*
- **Body:** handsome, physically ordinary. Training gives him normal-person gains and nothing more. The System changes that, later.
- **The Secret:** a running gag for now (medical intake exams, combat-suit fitting problems).

**Personality**
- Composed and polite on the outside. Inside, his narration is a constant stream of fan commentary.
- Confident and a bit of a schemer, as you'd expect from someone who has "read the script". He plans long-term: get stronger first, then get close.
- **Arc:** he starts with "collect the heroines" and has to face the fact that they were never characters. The System's lie forces that moment on him (see 3).

**Timeline anchor**
- 1991: Eros born
- 1995–1997: Mei (1995), Kiana (1996), Bronya and Seele (1997) born
- 2000: Second Eruption (Eros 9)
- 2012: drafted (Eros 21); System activates
- 2014: turns down a transfer to the Hyperion; stays at HQ
- ~2015: main story begins (Eros ~24; Mei ~20, Kiana ~19, Bronya/Seele ~18)

## 2. His Schicksal role

- **Schicksal HQ, Technology Department, Combat Simulation Section.** He writes the software for the Valkyrie VR training simulators: combat data pipelines, Honkai-energy sensor firmware, battlesuit telemetry.
- The irony carries the theme: he builds fake battle simulations for Schicksal during the day, and at night he runs real ones inside his own head.
- **He refuses the Hyperion on purpose.** He knows what is going to happen aboard it, and he doesn't want to be swept into canon before he is ready. His plan:
  1. **Stay at HQ and keep a low profile.** He is a useful engineer, nothing more.
  2. **Use his "harmless" System to grow strong in secret.** He believes it's all fake, so it's a risk-free training ground.
  3. **Only then move toward the heroines, on his own terms**, before the big canon events hit.
- **Why HQ still gives him contact:**
  - **Rita** serves Otto at HQ.
  - **Durandal** is HQ's top Valkyrie, and his simulators are her training tools.
  - **Theresa** and other branch Valkyries visit for evaluations and simulator time.
  - The **HQ archives** hold pre-era relics and records, which unlock pre-era Bond Missions.
- **The cost of this plan:** it keeps him right under Otto's nose.
- **Danger:** Otto Apocalypse reads everyone. A self-taught prodigy with oddly accurate intuitions about classified projects will catch Otto's attention. If Otto ever learned that Eros can *simulate the past*, Otto would do anything to get it (Kallen). That is a slow-burn threat that lasts the whole story.
- Anti-Entropy (Tesla, Einstein, Welt) might also try to poach a software genius. This is a possible subplot.

## 3. The System — working name: **Imaginary Branch Simulator** (虚数枝模拟器)

**Voice:** a cold web-novel UI. Terse brackets, no personality, purely matter-of-fact. That flatness is what makes "Nothing inside is real" believable.
- **But it slips, very rarely.** Once in a long while it shows a line it shouldn't have: `【…Interesting.】`, or a reward note phrased a little too kindly. These are hints that someone is behind it (and they tie into his missing last day on Earth).

**What the System says:** "Simulations are deductions. Nothing inside them is real."

**The truth: it is lying.**
- **Standard Simulations** really are dead branches (lives Eros *could* have lived). This half-truth makes the lie believable.
- **Bond Missions** run on **live branches** of the Imaginary Tree. When a mission is completed, the branch **merges** into the main timeline. Eros will eventually describe it in software terms: *branch, commit, merge, merge conflict.*
- **Why it lies:** if Eros knew it was real he would hold back, and the System wants to see what he does when he thinks nothing counts. The moral gut-punch is that every dark method he used "because it's fake" turns out to have happened to a real woman. A second layer: whoever (or whatever) built the System has its own agenda. **[DECIDE later]**
- **The reveal:** the first time a merged heroine shows up in the present and calls him by name.

**Merge rules (balancing pre-era heroines coming back):**
1. **The recorded past is not overwritten.** The history of the Flame-Chasers stays the same. Her death becomes a *disappearance* (sealed in an MEI Lab cryo-vault, drifting in the Sea of Quanta, preserved inside the Elysian Realm, etc.), and she **wakes in the present**.
2. **There is a delay.** A merge "compiles" over days or weeks, so returns happen at dramatically useful moments rather than right away.
3. **She returns weakened.** Her Herrscher core, Flame-Chaser powers or body are sealed or damaged, and recovering them becomes her own arc. Nobody comes back at full power.
4. **The meters carry over.** She returns as the woman the branch made her. A high Corruption heroine arrives corrupted. A high Obsession heroine arrives obsessed. *How* he won her decides who comes home.
5. **Memory:** she fully remembers the **final, successful loop**. The failed loops leak through as nightmares: she has seen him die in front of her, and she doesn't know why.
6. **Merge conflicts:** high Fate Deviation means the world pushes back. The Will of Honkai takes notice, and paradox side effects appear (a person who no longer exists, a battle that went differently).
7. **She is a strategic asset and a target.** Otto, Kevin (who *will* recognise Elysia and MEI) and Anti-Entropy will all want to know why pre-era women are walking around. Every heroine he brings back makes Eros more valuable and more hunted.
8. **The heroines know about each other.** Pre-era heroines knew one another, and each remembers a version of him. "You told *me* that too."
9. **No foreknowledge cost.** Merges do not erase his game knowledge. The balancing comes from rules 3–8.

**Present-day heroines** follow the same rule. A completed mission set in a living heroine's past (or a what-if branch) merges, and she now *remembers* a man she knew back then.
- **The "vanished boy" rule:** if the mission had him growing up in her past, the merge does **not** overwrite Eros's real life in Greece. Instead, the world now holds a **boy with his name and face who existed in her past and then vanished** (missing, presumed dead). The real heroine meets the real Eros and sees *him*.

**Activation (2012, first month at Schicksal):** a containment failure during a live test of his combat simulator. A captured Honkai beast sample breaks loose in the lab and Eros is caught in the burst of Honkai energy. He is dying on the lab floor, his soul starting to come apart, when the System boots:

```
【Abnormal soul fluctuation detected.】
【Imaginary Branch Simulator — binding…】
【Binding complete. Host: Eros.】
【Reminder: simulations are deductions. Nothing inside them is real.】
```

He survives, barely, and wakes in the infirmary with a blue panel only he can see.

**Interface:** the classic Chinese-web-novel log style, cold and matter-of-fact:

```
【Imaginary Branch Simulator — Lv.1】
Charge: 1/3
Available: Standard Simulation ×1
Bond Missions: locked (requires: first contact)
```

### 3a. Standard Simulations (the classic 模拟器流 loop)

- At the start, 3 random **talent cards** are drawn, ranked **F → E → D → C → B → A → S → SS → SSS**. He picks one.
- The simulation plays as a **fast text log**: *"Year 1: You wake up in a refugee camp outside…"*. He gets choices only at key nodes.
- **Death ends it normally.** There is no penalty beyond the run being over.
- **Settlement:** pick **1 of 3** rewards. Examples: knowledge from that life, skill proficiency (marksmanship, swordwork, field medicine), talent cards, small physical-fragment rewards (locked at first).
- This is his main way to grow "safely" and to scout lore he has forgotten.

### 3b. Bond Missions (the special heroine missions)

- **Fully immersive.** He lives it in first person in real time (subjectively). The real world sees only one night's sleep.
- **The start is normal or bad, but the timeline always holds a path to her.** The System places him somewhere that is rough now but *can* lead to the heroine if he finds and follows the right route (e.g. Rita: he starts as an orphan in her childhood, and one path makes him her childhood friend; years later they are the perfect maid and the perfect butler).
- **Missions can span years.** He can grow up alongside her.
- **Under-18 rule:** nothing romantic or explicit is ever written while a heroine is under 18. She **can** develop feelings for Eros early (a crush, admiration, attachment), but Eros keeps it platonic, and romance-route conditions only count once she is 18+. Even after 18, intimacy stays non-graphic.
- **Branch timing:** a Bond Mission's branch can run **up to the real present date, never beyond it.**
- **Core goal of every mission: become irreplaceable in her life, by any method.** Light or dark, it all counts.
- **Several objective routes are open at once.** Completing **one** clears the mission, and each extra route raises the grade (see 3g).
- **Death → RESET** to the mission's start, with a cost (Memory Integrity, below).
- **Failing the objective without dying** → choose **[End Simulation]** (settle at a reduced grade) or **[Restart]** (costs Charge).

### 3c. Meters (per heroine, per mission)

| Meter | Meaning | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Affection** 好感 | How she feels about him | Mainly drives rewards |
| **Trust** 信任 | Kept separate from affection. She can like him and still not trust him (Rita, Mobius) | Low trust locks key choices |
| **Corruption** 堕落 | How far she drifts from her "script": duty, ideals, self-restraint, *because of him* (Durandal skipping orders, Aponia breaking her own discipline) | High values push toward unique endings, including bad ones |
| **Obsession** 执念 | Dependence and possessiveness | Above ~80 triggers "possessive events" that can kill him, and that counts as a death |
| **Fate Deviation** 命运偏移 | How far the *world* has strayed from canon | High values make the Will of Honkai "correct" things: unknown, harsher events |
| **Sincerity** ??? | **Hidden.** Is he really engaging with her, or running a game strategy? | Revealed later. It caps how high the other meters can go |

**Eros's own bars (inside a mission):**
- **Memory Integrity** (starts at 100%)
- **Honkai Erosion**, his own infection level in high-Honkai eras

### 3d. The death cost: Soul Damage → Memory Loss

There is no fixed number per death. Memory loss comes from **damage to his soul**, and the damage depends on **how** he died.

**Ordinary deaths: a small bite, every time.**
Blades, bullets, falls, beasts, starvation, a Herrscher crushing him physically: any death that only destroys his body takes **a small, steady bite** of his soul. It's the same every time, however he died and however often.
- **Dying on purpose is allowed.** Suicide to force a reset costs the same small bite. Whether it's *worth it* is Eros's own calculation: one more piece of his memory against a better run.

**Soul attacks: the real danger.**
Anything that strikes the soul itself takes **large chunks**. That includes a Herrscher's authority aimed at him, Void and Imaginary powers, Sea of Quanta erosion, Honkai erosion of the mind, and Kevin-tier existence-level attacks. The stronger the attacker's hold over souls or existence, the bigger the chunk. One bad soul-death can cost more than a dozen ordinary ones.

**Order of loss** (as integrity falls):
1. Game lore
2. Details of earlier loops (which shrink to gut feelings)
3. His current life
4. His Earth identity

**Recovery:** clearing an objective route mid-mission mends part of the damage.

**Integrity 0 → Ejection.** He lives on as a native until the branch closes, then gets thrown out with no reward, plus days of memory fog in the real world.

**Failed branches reset.** An ejection, or choosing **[End]** after failing, **wipes the branch clean.** Nothing merges and the heroine remembers nothing. The mission goes on **cooldown**. When he retries it later, **his starting point is different** (a new role, place or moment in her timeline). A choice of **[Restart]** right after an objective failure keeps the original start.

When a mission ends his memories come back, and the deaths stay as nightmares.

### 3e. Rewards and what they bring back

Settlement grade **S / A / B / C / F**, based on the final meter values. Then pick 1 of 3:
- **Skills or knowledge** from that era (pre-era technology, swordsmanship, Honkai theory)
- **Stigmata imprints**, abilities shaped by the heroine or the era (fits HI3's stigmata system)
- **Physique fragments**, the slow fix for his "normal man" body (locked until a later System level)
- (Replaced) There is no "Bond Imprint". A completed Bond Mission **merges**, and the heroine herself is the reward (see the merge rules).

### 3g. Objective routes (several open per mission)

| Route | Clear condition (general form) | What she brings home after the merge |
|---|---|---|
| **Heart** 心 | She chooses him, freely, over her deepest attachment or duty | Love, and a stable bond |
| **Fall** 堕 | She abandons her core ideal *because of him* | A darker, reshaped version of herself |
| **Chain** 锁 | She can no longer function without him: he is her crutch, her reason, her cage | Dependence, and dangerous Obsession |
| **Salvation** 救 | He breaks or rewrites the tragedy that defines her | Gratitude and a debt; she is freer |
| **Equal** 衡 | She sees him as her peer or rival, the one person at her level | Respect, partnership, friction |
| **Hidden** ？ | Unknown until he stumbles onto it | ??? |

**Grade:** 1 route = **C**; 2 = **B**; 3 = **A**; 4 = **S**; all open routes (including Hidden) = **SS "Perfect Merge"**. Both the grade and the reward pool scale with it.

**Conflicting routes:** Heart and Chain, and Fall and Salvation, pull against each other. Clearing both in one run is possible but extremely hard.

### 3f. Charge and progression

- Charge regenerates slowly (about 1 per week). Real-world milestones such as a first meeting, a shared crisis or a sincere conversation can grant bonus Charge.
- **Bond Missions for a heroine unlock only after first contact with her in the real world**, or, for pre-era heroines, through contact with their relics or legacy (the Elysian Realm, MEI's research files).
- System level-ups unlock longer missions, more meters, physique rewards and, much later, possibly multi-heroine missions.

## 4. Roster and the age line

**AU age shift:** Mei (1995), Kiana (1996), Bronya and Seele (1997) are all **18+ when the main story begins (~2015)**. Eros (1991) is ~24.

**AU Theresa:** an **adult woman with a petite, short build**. She is clearly grown up in body and face, just small. She is a full heroine.

Romance is only ever with characters who are adults *at that moment in the story*:
- **Kiana, Mei, Bronya, Seele:** full heroines. Their Bond Missions are set in their adult years, or in **what-if branches** where they are already adults (e.g. "a branch where Changkong happened four years later"). There are no missions in their childhood.
- **Other adults:** Theresa (AU), Himeko, Rita, Durandal/Bianka, Fu Hua, Tesla, Einstein; pre-era: Elysia, Dr. MEI, Mobius, Aponia, Eden, Vill-V, Pardofelis, Sakura.
- **Never romance targets:** Griseo, Sirin, and any child.

## 5. Sample Bond Missions (deeper, multi-route)

**Elysia — "Before the Thirteenth Flame"**
Start: you are the last survivor of a wiped-out conscript squad on a Collapse front, bleeding out.
- Heart: in the final war, she names *you* as the reason she wants to come back.
- Fall: she breaks her "everyone is beautiful" ideal for you, and hates or abandons someone because of you.
- Chain: she can't bring herself to go to her final fight without seeing you first.
- Salvation: she enters her last battle with something to live for, so her ending becomes a disappearance instead of a death.
- Hidden: ???

**Mobius — "Specimen"**
Start: you are her test subject.
- Equal: she admits your mind is as interesting as her research.
- Fall: she gives up an experiment, or her pursuit of "evolution", for you.
- Chain: she refuses to let any other researcher near you. (Obsession spikes, and so does the danger.)
- Salvation: her fear of being ordinary is answered, and *you* are the answer.

**Rita — "The Mark"**
Start: you are the man she has been ordered to quietly eliminate.
- Heart: she disobeys the order of her own free will.
- Fall: she lies to Otto for you.
- Equal: she can never quite out-maneuver you, and she enjoys that.
- Chain: she kills someone else to protect you, then can't explain why to herself.

## 7. Part One — Raiden Mei

**Why Mei first**
- She is **not** a "big gun" yet in 2014: just a civilian student in Changkong who doesn't know what's inside her. The mission is grounded and small until its climax.
- It plays to Eros's job (a Schicksal engineer on a joint tech project).
- The branch ends **before** Kiana arrives in Changkong, so the canon Kiana–Mei bond stays intact and becomes a dynamic later.
- She has a strong "favorable path" built into her life: a lonely girl who looks after everything and everyone, and that no one looks after in return.

**Timing:** Part One happens in **2014**. Eros has spent two years quietly grinding Standard Simulations, sticking to his "get stronger first" plan. Mei is 19.

**First contact (2014, Changkong):** Schicksal sends Eros to audit Honkai-sensor data at Raiden Ryoma's company. In the lobby, a polite young woman is asking reception to pass a lunchbox to her father, who is "too busy" to come down, again. Eros recognises her immediately and says nothing. The System pings:
```
【First contact established: Raiden Mei】
【Bond Mission unlocked: "Two Lunchboxes"】
【Reminder: simulations are deductions. Nothing inside them is real.】
```

**Bond Mission: "Two Lunchboxes" (branch 2010 → 2014)**
- **Start (bad):** he is a 15-year-old scholarship transfer to Mei's private academy. He is poor, an outsider, and on day one he gets on the wrong side of the school's elite.
- **The path:**
  - Mei is the student-council president who steps in.
  - He becomes her vice-president.
  - He is the one who notices she is carrying everything alone.
  - Every day she makes a lunchbox for her absent father, and it comes back uneaten. **One path makes him the one who eats it.**
- **Age rule in practice:** from 15 to 17 it's friendship and closeness. Her feelings can bud; his response stays platonic. Romance-route conditions only count once she's 18+ (2013–2014).
- **Climax (2014):** an accident in her father's Honkai lab. A core inside Mei starts to stir: the first spark of the Herrscher of Thunder, years before canon.
- **Routes:**
  - **Heart:** Mei, who never asks anyone for anything, asks him for help, and later chooses him freely.
  - **Fall:** she reaches for the lightning *for him*, and accepts the power on her own terms.
  - **Chain:** she cannot let him out of her sight. This is the 黑化芽衣 (dark Mei) direction the Chinese fandom loves.
  - **Salvation:** he protects her from her father's experiment and its consequences without her losing herself.
  - **Hidden:** ???
- **Deaths:**
  - Mostly ordinary early on (accidents, the lab incident's debris and infected): small bites.
  - **Herrscher lightning in the climax counts as a soul attack, and this is where the real danger is.**

**The merge, and the lie cracking (end of Part One)**
- Under the vanished-boy rule, real records now show a **2014 accident at Raiden Ryoma's lab with one missing student**: the vice-president who vanished saving the president.
- Real Mei remembers four years with him. A few days later, at the company, she sees Eros, the same face and name. She doesn't run up to him. She comes back the next day with **two lunchboxes.**
- **Consequences:**
  - Schicksal's report on the 2014 accident now contains a missing student who looks exactly like their engineer.
  - Raiden Ryoma notices his daughter has stopped waiting for *him*.
  - The Herrscher core is now stirring a year early.
  - Depending on routes, Mei comes back warmer, darker, or possessive.

**Mei's voice (working notes, to verify through research)**
- Gentle, polite and mature: the dependable one who cooks, cleans and organises everyone.
- Hides her loneliness and her own wants behind responsibility.
- Underneath, a very strong will and intense feelings. When she decides something, nothing moves her.
- Practises kendo and is calm under pressure, until the people she cares about are threatened.

**Later missions (not now):** Durandal ("The Blade's Keeper"), Rita ("Perfect Service": childhood friend → perfect maid and perfect butler).

## 8. The planned failed mission — Elysia

- **When:** after Part One, **once he knows the simulations are real.** That makes the failure devastating. If he fails, the real Elysia stays dead.
- **Why it fails:** he goes in trying to *play* her with game knowledge and a perfect "Elysia strategy", and she sees through scripted people better than anyone. Then the pre-era Herrscher war hits him with soul attacks. His lore goes first, and then everything else does too, all the way to **Ejection**.
- **Result:** the branch **resets.** She remembers nothing, and he remembers everything. The mission goes on cooldown.
- **The retry (a later arc):** a new starting point, and an Eros who has stopped performing. This becomes one of the high points of the whole story. It ties the failure to his hidden **Sincerity** meter.
