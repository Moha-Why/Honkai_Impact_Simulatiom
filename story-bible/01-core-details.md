# Story Bible — Core Details (draft, pre-premise)

Status: details are still being chosen. No premise yet. Items marked **[DECIDE]** are open.

## 1. Eros — the protagonist

**Previous life (our Earth)**
- Software engineer and a long-time Honkai Impact 3rd player who knew the lore well. His dream was to win every heroine.
- How he died: **[DECIDE]**. Options: overwork, an accident on the way to a version livestream, or something he can't remember at all (a hook for later).

**New life (the Honkai world)**
- **Born ~1993.** That makes him about 7 at the Second Eruption (Siberia, 2000) and early twenties when Part 1 begins in the mid-2010s.
- **He believes this is a normal Earth.** Honkai events are hidden behind cover stories like "industrial disaster" or "meteor strike". He notices small differences (brands that don't exist, a slightly different history) and writes them off as a Mandela effect.
- He never wrote any game notes as a child, because he didn't know he was in the game. So when the truth hits, he has to dig up 20-year-old memories of the lore. They are patchy and sometimes wrong, and that unreliability is useful for the story.
- **The genius path:** an adult mind in a child's body. He skips grades, starts university at around 15 in computer science, and builds a real reputation (competition wins, a well-known open-source distributed-systems or cryptography project). That reputation is what Schicksal finds.
- **The draft (~2011, age ~18):** a recruitment offer arrives from a shell company. During the interview he sees the Schicksal eagle crest. Inner monologue: *"Oh no." …then… "Oh. YES."*
- **Body:** handsome, physically ordinary. Training gives him normal-person gains and nothing more. He is not a Valkyrie and can't become one. The System is what eventually changes that, much later.
- **The Secret:** a running gag for now (medical intake exams, combat-suit fitting problems, and the fact that he can't just tell anyone what the System is either). Nothing explicit.
- **Birthplace / heritage: [DECIDE]**. Options: (a) Southern Europe, which keeps the Greek name natural and puts him close to Schicksal HQ; (b) China, near the Far East Branch and Changkong; (c) mixed heritage.

**Personality (proposed)**
- Composed and polite on the outside. Inside, his narration is a constant stream of fan commentary ("that's her S-rank pose, I'd know it anywhere").
- Confident and a bit of a schemer, as you'd expect from someone who has "read the script".
- **Arc:** he starts with "collect the heroines" and moves toward "I can't treat them as characters." The System quietly tests this (see the hidden *Sincerity* meter).

## 2. His Schicksal role

- **Schicksal HQ, Technology Department, Combat Simulation Section.** He writes the software for the Valkyrie VR training simulators: combat data pipelines, Honkai-energy sensor firmware, battlesuit telemetry.
- The irony carries the theme: he builds fake battle simulations for Schicksal during the day, and at night he runs real ones inside his own head.
- **Later posting [DECIDE]:** seconded to the Hyperion as systems/bridge officer. This gives natural contact with Himeko and Theresa and through them the Part 1 cast, all without him attending St. Freya.
- **Danger:** Otto Apocalypse reads everyone. A self-taught prodigy with oddly accurate intuitions about classified projects will catch Otto's attention. If Otto ever learned that Eros can *simulate the past*, Otto would do anything to get it (Kallen). That is a slow-burn threat that lasts the whole story.
- Anti-Entropy (Tesla, Einstein, Welt) might also try to poach a software genius. This is a possible subplot.

## 3. The System — working name: **Imaginary Branch Simulator** (虚数枝模拟器)

Alternatives: *Quantum Sea Simulator*, *Paradise Simulator*. **[DECIDE]**

**Origin (lore-friendly, kept vague):** each simulation is built from "dead branches" and echoes of the Imaginary Tree and the Sea of Quanta. The people he meets inside are **echoes** made from the world's records. They are not the real people, but they aren't entirely fake either. That ambiguity will matter later, with Elysia and the Elysian Realm in particular.

**Activation:** the first time he is exposed to concentrated Honkai energy after joining Schicksal (or the moment he understands which world he is in). **[DECIDE]**

**Interface:** the classic Chinese-web-novel log style, cold and matter-of-fact:

```
【Imaginary Branch Simulator — Lv.1】
Charge: 1/3
Available: Standard Simulation ×1
Bond Missions: locked (requires: first contact)
```

### 3a. Standard Simulations (the classic 模拟器流 loop)

- At the start, 3 random **talent cards** are drawn (rarity B / A / S / SS, after HI3's grades). He picks one.
- The simulation plays as a **fast text log**: *"Year 1: You wake up in a refugee camp outside…"*. He gets choices only at key nodes.
- **Death ends it normally.** There is no penalty beyond the run being over.
- **Settlement:** pick **1 of 3** rewards. Examples: knowledge from that life, skill proficiency (marksmanship, swordwork, field medicine), talent cards, small physical-fragment rewards (locked at first).
- This is his main way to grow "safely" and to scout lore he has forgotten.

### 3b. Bond Missions (the special heroine missions)

- **Fully immersive.** He lives it in first person in real time (subjectively). The real world sees only one night's sleep.
- **The start is never favourable.** Usually neutral, often bad. Examples: a pre-era conscript bleeding in a trench, a man someone was ordered to kill, a test subject.
- **Each mission has one stated objective**, which is not always romantic: "Make her remember your name", "Survive 30 days", "Change one decision she will make".
- **Death → RESET** to the mission's start, with a cost (Memory Integrity, below).
- **Failing the objective without dying** → choose **[End Simulation]** (settle at a reduced grade) or **[Restart]** (costs Charge).

### 3c. Meters (per heroine, per mission)

| Meter | Meaning | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Affection** 好感 | How she feels about him | Mainly drives rewards |
| **Trust** 信任 | Kept separate from affection. She can like him and still not trust him (Rita, Mobius) | Low trust locks key choices |
| **Corruption** 堕落 | How far she drifts from her "script": duty, ideals, self-restraint, *because of him* (Durandal skipping orders, Aponia breaking her own discipline) | High values push toward unique endings, including bad ones |
| **Obsession** 执念 | Dependence and possessiveness | Above ~80 triggers "possessive events" that can kill him, and that counts as a death |
| **Fate Deviation** 命运偏移 | How far the *world* has strayed from canon | High values make the Will of Honkai "correct" things: unknown, harsher events |
| **Sincerity** ??? | **Hidden.** Is he really engaging with her, or running a game strategy? | Revealed later. It caps how high the other meters can go |

**Eros's own bars (inside a mission):**
- **Memory Integrity** (starts at 100%)
- **Honkai Erosion**, his own infection level in high-Honkai eras

### 3d. The death cost: Memory Integrity

Each death removes a chunk (suggested: −15% to −20%) **for the rest of that simulation**. It is lost in this order:

1. **Game lore from Earth.** His biggest advantage goes first.
2. **Details of previous loops.** The conclusions survive as gut feelings ("Don't take the left corridor"; he doesn't know why).
3. **His current-life memories** (Schicksal, family, his real name and job).
4. **His Earth identity.**
5. **0%:** he forgets it is a simulation and lives on as a native until the simulation ends naturally. Then he is forcibly ejected with an empty reward pool, plus **real-world aftereffects** (days of memory fog, nightmares, flashes of a life he didn't live).

When a mission ends, his memories come back, **but the deaths stay as vivid dreams.** Trauma carries over.

### 3e. Rewards and what they bring back

Settlement grade **S / A / B / C / F**, based on the final meter values. Then pick 1 of 3:
- **Skills or knowledge** from that era (pre-era technology, swordsmanship, Honkai theory)
- **Stigmata imprints**, abilities shaped by the heroine or the era (fits HI3's stigmata system)
- **Physique fragments**, the slow fix for his "normal man" body (locked until a later System level)
- **Bond Imprint:** the *real* woman feels an unexplained familiarity or déjà vu around him. **Only familiarity, never feelings.** He still has to earn affection in reality.

### 3f. Charge and progression

- Charge regenerates slowly (about 1 per week). Real-world milestones such as a first meeting, a shared crisis or a sincere conversation can grant bonus Charge.
- **Bond Missions for a heroine unlock only after first contact with her in the real world**, or, for pre-era heroines, through contact with their relics or legacy (the Elysian Realm, MEI's research files).
- System level-ups unlock longer missions, more meters, physique rewards and, much later, possibly multi-heroine missions.

## 4. Roster and the age line

Romance (any meter beyond friendship) is **only with characters who are adults at that point in the story.**

**Adult heroines (eligible from the start):** Himeko, Rita, Durandal/Bianka, Fu Hua (adult-appearing immortal), Tesla, Einstein, pre-era: **Elysia, Dr. MEI, Mobius, Aponia, Eden, Vill-V, Pardofelis, Sakura**.

**Minors in canon during Part 1 (Kiana, Mei, Bronya, Seele, etc.):** non-romantic roles only (friend, mentor, big-brother figure) for as long as they are minors. Romance is possible only once they are adults later in the timeline. **[DECIDE]** whether to include them at that point.

**Never romance targets:** Theresa (child body, so she works as boss/comedic aunt figure), Griseo, Sirin, and anyone else who is a child.

## 5. Sample Bond Missions (examples of bad starts)

- **Elysia (pre-era):** you are a conscript, the last survivor of a wiped-out squad on the 10th Collapse front. *Objective: make her remember your name when the war ends.*
- **Dr. MEI (pre-era):** you are a junior software engineer at MEI Lab (your real job, in the worst era). *Objective: get her to look at something other than Kevin, just once.* Difficulty: extreme.
- **Rita Rossweisse:** you are the man she has been ordered to quietly eliminate. *Objective: survive 30 days.* Trust starts at 0 and Affection is irrelevant until it isn't.
- **Durandal:** a rookie in her unit during a Herrscher-class incident. *Objective: earn the right to spar with her, then make her call you by name.*
- **Fu Hua (ancient Shenzhou):** a soldier in a city that will fall. *Objective: be one person an immortal doesn't forget.*
- **Mobius:** test subject #… in her lab. *Objective: escape, or become "interesting".* Corruption and Obsession are both dangerous here.
- **Himeko:** the Changkong-era evacuation, with you as a civilian she has to protect. *Objective: keep her alive past a night she shouldn't survive.*
