morning of February 4 is rebuilt. **The ending stays as written:** from
the morning of February 4 through the roof, the settlement and the waking.
The only changes there are consistency edits (the loop number, the fan

[../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/](../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/). Spine

## What this loop is built on

- **It's Loop 5, straight after Loop 4.** He chose it in the white, cold,
  where he can still choose coldly: a boy who has loved Mei since he was
  nine, built fork by fork so that there's no room in him for anyone else.
  He loved Kiana in the last file and died for it. This boy won't.
- **The seal costs a stage.** He goes in Worn. Eighteen years sealed fade
  what he brings back, so he takes over Torn: his own files are facts, and
  the fan is gone until he wakes. The boy's eighteen years are experience.
- **The boy leads.** After the takeover there's no cold calculation. He
  means the confessions. He can't stop the cruelty and doesn't use it. He
  sends her out into the rain to Tanabe. He doesn't provoke Kuroda.
- **Kiana is a fact.** He has the fourth file as a list: a fryer by the
  station, a shrine, a roof. She walks into 3-A and says the same first
  words to him, and the boy loves Mei.
