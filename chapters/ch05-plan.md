# Chapter 5 plan: Liar's Face (Loop 5)

Status: plan v6, rebuilt from scratch to answer the new Loop 4. Replaces
"The Pin" (the ward childhood) as the last loop; the old version is in
[../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/](../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/). Spine and
rules: [ch03-plan.md](ch03-plan.md).

## What this loop is

Loop 4 died on a roof because Mei saw him confess to Kiana and ran before
Kiana said no. Loop 5 is the same year played as a lie. He goes back to the
same birth and the same desk, and decides, in the white, never to follow
Kiana, never to be alone with her, never to look at her where Mei can see,
and to learn to fight from Mei instead. He still loves Kiana. Liar's Face,
the gift he drew at the start of the run, finally does what it's for.

It's still a romance, and the action is still at the end. This time he goes
to Mei.

## Beats

1. **The white (end of Chapter 4).** Same birth. One fork changed: this time
   the boy tells Haru his mother is coming. [Liar's Face is going to have to
   work for a living.]
2. **The log.** "The same as the fourth file," then Haru: he waits at the gate
   happy all spring, is adopted anyway, and turns round at the gate to wave.
   A kind lie works. Eros notes it.
3. **The takeover.** Worn: the lighthouse, Kiana's "No," her arm, "Go to
   her," All of it as experience. The same apartment by the
   station. September.
4. **Every school day again.** The same words, and he knows her answers before
   she gives them. Guilt makes him kinder than last time; she notices.
5. **The sword.** In October he asks Mei to teach him. She says the club is
   for members. He joins, as the worst first-year anyone has seen at
   eighteen. She starts correcting him.
6. **November 16 and the bullying.** He knows every blow and when it lands,
   and he's there for each one: the shoes, the stairs (he's already on the
   landing), the bucket (he's at the door with a towel before it falls), the
   textbooks, the chat. He can't stop the node and can't stop all of it. What
   he stops, they do to him instead. She relies on him. Trust reaches Relies
   in December, and the lock-in notice comes that night: [Now that you've
   finished one, the next time you die, it's over.]
7. **December 16.** He knows the corridor and the minute. He's beside her when
   Kuroda knocks the box out of her hands, and he moves, and Kiana is faster
   (the meeting holds). The USP, the line, "Follow me," the roof. His first
   love walks past him close enough to touch and doesn't know him. His face
   holds. [You've stopped breathing.]
8. **The three of them, again.** Kiana's rival confessions; the marks in
   pairs; the third box. He laughs at the right moments and never looks at her
   when Mei can see. Kiana: "You're kind of creepy. Your face doesn't do
   anything. But you didn't leave her. That counts."
9. **The nights he doesn't go.** Kiana slips away after dark; he knows where.
   He doesn't follow. One night she asks him outright to come and see
   something at the port. He says no in front of Mei. That night Mei takes one
   of the two pins out of her hair and puts it in his hand. "So you'll stay."
10. **The family sword.** In January Mei finds the Homu suit and understands;
    she teaches him the Raiden sword at dawn, the doors shut against the cold.
    The ceiling tiles again, told to him as if for the first time; he lets her.
11. **The cruelty.** Kuroda's group, the pipe, a cracked rib. She straps it.
    In PE kendo she breaks Kuroda's wrist with a kote and says it slipped, and
    lets him see it didn't. (Compromised)
12. **Kiana on the roof before New Year.** "Do you like her? Actually like
    her?" "Yes." It's the best lie he's ever told and the face does nothing.
    "You want her to need you. I want her to be okay."
13. **The dream.** Mei dreams she killed him on a roof. [The runs leave marks.]
14. **February 4.** The roof. Kiana fights the Herrscher, and this time it
    turns from her to him: "You. The one she waits for... She's mine. You
    only rented her." In Loop 4 he ran to Kiana. This time he walks to Mei,
    and its hand goes into his chest. The pin: "You gave me this so I'd stay.
    I stayed." "Count with me." Mei surfaces and pulls the lightning out, but
    it's taken a stage with it (Torn). Kiana kneels and holds out her hand:
    "It doesn't matter what you did. You're Mei." Mei won't let go of him. "I'm
    sorry, Kiana-chan. No." Fallen. "I love you." The kiss. The Hyperion's
    light. She counts for him, and Kiana is the one holding his hand. He dies
    at thirty-eight.
15. **Settlement.** Four of eight, grade A. He takes Honkai Resistance, the
    pin and the Raiden sword, and leaves Liar's Face behind.
16. **The waking.** The pin in his hand, and a long moment with it before the
    engineer looks at it. Both Kianas come back with everything else. The
    watch room, 1,184 HW, the Hyperion leaving the north tower.

## Note on the ending

The old Pin's ending beats (the pin, "Count with me," Kiana's hand, the kiss,
thirty-eight) are kept and re-founded on this loop: the pin is given for
staying, and the counting is her own habit from the ward, which she told him
in both files. If the ending should change as well, this is the place to
say so.
