## What this loop is

Loop 4 died on a roof because Mei followed him to the lighthouse and ran
before Kiana said no. Loop 5 is the same birth and the same year, played as
a lie. In the white he decided never to go where Kiana goes and to learn to
fight from Mei. He still loves Kiana. Liar's Face finally does what he drew
it for.

Still a romance, with the action at the end. This time, on the roof, he
goes to Mei.

## How to write it (from the Chapter 4 rewrite)

- **Show, don't explain.** Feelings come out in what people do and say. The
  emotional peaks get the least narration.
- **Nobody reads minds.** Mei thinks he's like any other boy until November
  16 (now he's the scholarship boy who joined her club to get near her).
  Kiana sees only what anyone would see.
- **Every conversation comes out of something that happens.**
- **People act inside the relationships they have.** No visit to the house
  on the hill until she asks him in.
- **Out loud he's never heard of Honkai.** What he knows from Loop 4 shows
  only in where he stands and what he does, and when it shows, someone
  notices.
- **Repeated days don't get retold.** When a day repeats, write the one
  thing that's different and let the rest go by in a line.
- **Fights:** anime style, kits first, beat plans approved before writing.
  His own moves get named and a sound, never an impact frame.

## Beats

### Part 1: The same year (September to November 16)

1. **The log.** "The same as the fourth file." The one changed fork: the boy
   tells Haru his mother is coming. Haru waits at the gate all spring with
   his coat sleeves turned back, is adopted anyway, and turns round at the
   gate to wave. No comment from anyone.
2. **The takeover.** Worn: Loop 4 comes up in him as things, not as
   explanation (the lighthouse steps, her arm bent wrong, "Go to her!", the
   net of white over the roof). The same apartment by the station.
3. **The confessions.** The same ten words from his second week. He knows
   her answers before she gives them and gives nothing away. The marks on
   the board, Emi's book, Mei's same "I'm sorry. No."
4. **The kendo club (October).** He asks her to teach him; she says the club
   is for members. He joins, the worst beginner anyone's seen at eighteen,
   and she's harder on him than on anyone, because she assumes he joined to
   get near her. Every boy who joined for her quit by the summer tournament.
   He doesn't. The club basics are the first named moves of his he can do
   badly ([Kendo: Men], [Kendo: Kote]).
5. **The rank again (late October).** The reporter is waiting. The same
   bluff about the lawyer behind the black glass. When she asks if it's
   true, this time he gives her nothing she can hold against him later: "I
   don't know." Mr. Tanabe's eyes in the mirror again.
6. **November 15 and 16.** "Goodnight." Then the stall at ten past one,
   Daichi's "Poor kid." He knew the words before Daichi said them. He
   doesn't go up the hill.

### Part 2: The year as a lie (November 18 to January)

7. **The bullying.** He knows every blow and when it lands, and he's there
   first. He can't stop the arrest and can't stop all of it, and what he
   stops they do to him.
   - **The bucket that doesn't fall (Mei).** He's at the toilet door with a
     towel before Kuroda's friends come round the corner with it. They go
     away. She asks how he knew. "I heard them on the stairs." It's a good
     lie, and she doesn't quite believe it.
   - The stairs: he's already on the landing. The shoes, the textbooks, the
     chat, each in a line, each costing him something instead.
   - Trust reaches Relies in December, and the lock-in notice comes that
     night: [Now that you've finished one, the next time you die, it's
     over.]
8. **December 16 (Kiana).** The corridor, the USP, "five hundred
   centimeters," "Follow me." On the roof a boy who came up behind Mei-senpai
   goes white and looks at the floor until Kiana stops looking at him.
   [You've stopped breathing.]
9. **The three of them.** Kiana's rival confessions; the marks in pairs; the
   third box. He laughs at the right moments and never looks at Kiana where
   Mei can see. Kiana notices that much: "You never look at me. Do you hate
   me or something?" "No." "Okay. Weird. But you didn't leave her. That
   counts."
10. **Kiana hunting alone (Kiana, fight).** No map. Her father's radar with
    the foil still in it, cutting out. She misses one in the dark and pays
    for it. On top of a container afterwards she wonders why the quiet one
    has never once asked where she goes at night.
11. **The roof before New Year (Kiana).** "Do you like her? Actually like
    her?" "Yes." It's the best lie he's ever told, and his face doesn't give
    it away. "You want her to need you. I want her to be okay."
12. **The night he doesn't go (early January, then Mei).** At lunch, in front
    of Mei, Kiana asks him outright to come and see something at the port
    that night. He says no. That night, at her gate, Mei takes one of the two
    pins out of her hair and puts it in his hand. "So you'll stay."
13. **The family sword (Mei).** She finds the Mr. HOMU suit in the training
    room and understands her father was teaching her all along. She teaches
    Eros the Raiden forms at dawn, with the dojo doors shut against the cold:
    an old art for killing, not for sport, and she doesn't pretend otherwise.
    She tells him about the ceiling tiles as if for the first time; he lets
    her, and says nothing about sixty-one.
14. **Kuroda's pipe (Eros, fight).** Kuroda's group with a scaffold pipe, by
    the bike sheds after school. He has an umbrella. Shiden into the first
    boy's throat; Ikazuchi straight down through Kuroda's pipe arm, and the
    umbrella explodes and Kuroda's collarbone goes. [With a blade, that's two
    dead,] says the System. Then the other two take him from behind, and he
    comes away with a cracked rib. She straps it in the dojo without a word.
15. **The kote (Mei).** In PE kendo she breaks Kuroda's wrist with a kote,
    says it slipped, and lets Eros see it didn't. (Compromised.)
16. **The dream (Mei, late January).** She dreams she killed him on a roof,
    and wakes counting. [The runs leave marks.]

### Part 3: February 4

17. **The roof at lunch (Mei).** The three of them eat; Mr. Fujita keeps Eros
    back about the scholarship. Kiana goes to class. Mei stays with his box
    and waits for him past the bell, the first lesson she's ever missed,
    because he said he'd come. At one-forty the school goes off underneath
    her. The voice in her chest: "He's late. They're always late. Let me."
    She doesn't let go, and it's a fight inside her, written as one (the
    lights of the city answering her, the sky turning, what she holds on to
    this time) before it takes her anyway. Animated, never two lines.
18. **The stairs (Eros, fight).** Natsumi coughing blood; the windows. His
    practice sword in its bag, carried every day since January. Kiana on the
    stairs: "You can DO that?" The power fails, and the stairwell is lit only
    by her gunfire and the purple flashes. Shiden through a throat, Ikazuchi
    down through a reaching arm. Mr. Fujita, turned, comes up after them, and
    this time Eros does it himself, Narukami under the ribs.
19. **The roof (Kiana, then Eros; boss fight).** Kiana fights it first, and it
    never looks at her: her bullets and her thrown guns burn while its eyes
    stay on the door; she jumps from the water tank and it catches her by
    the throat behind its back without turning (impact frame); her blood
    drinks the voltage; the hug line; "Not you." Then it turns to him. "You." It speaks a word or
    two at a time and never explains Mei's heart ("Mine."). Her Shiden, the
    real one, lightning across ten meters; his Shiden, wood, the same form he
    learned from her, and the two cuts meet. The practice sword burns down to
    the handle in his hands, and he's still standing. He drops it and walks
    to her. "Kneel." He keeps walking. Her hand goes into his chest. He holds up the pin:
    "You gave me this so I'd stay. I stayed." "Count with me." Mei surfaces
    and pulls the lightning out, and it takes a stage of his soul with it
    (Torn). Kiana kneels and holds out her hand: "It doesn't matter what you
    did. You're Mei." Mei won't let go of him. "I'm sorry, Kiana-chan. No."
    (Fallen.) "I love you." The kiss. No ship comes. The purple goes out of
    the sky, and it's an ordinary gray afternoon over Nagazora. She counts
    for him, and Kiana is the one holding his hand. He dies at thirty-eight.
20. **Settlement.** Four of eight, grade A. He takes Honkai Resistance, the
    pin and the Raiden sword, and leaves Liar's Face behind.
21. **The waking.** The pin in his hand, and a long moment before the
    engineer in him looks at it. The watch room before dawn: 1,184 HW over
    Nagazora. Theresa asks HQ for clearance, and the Hyperion stays on its
    tower. He knows it'll be three or four days, and that Mei and Kiana are
    on their own in that city until then, and he can't tell anyone how he
    knows.
