## What this loop is

Loop 4 died on a roof because Mei followed him to the lighthouse and ran
before Kiana said no. In the white he remembered the room she told him
about on the dojo step, a hundred and twelve ceiling tiles and nobody else
awake, and took the birth that puts somebody awake in it: the night nurse's
son on the sixth floor of the ME Corp hospital, nine years before he meets
her.

The boy grows up beside her in the ward from nine, goes to Osaka at twelve
with one of her two pins, and comes back at eighteen. The adult Eros wakes
at the bottom of a boy who has loved her since he was nine, and he still
loves Kiana himself. Neither moves over. He plays 2013 as a lie: never
where Kiana goes, never looking at her where Mei can see. Liar's Face hides
Kiana. Everything he says to Mei is true.

Still a romance, with the action at the end. On the roof he goes to Mei,
and smiles at her with her hand in his chest.

## How to write it (from the Chapter 4 rewrite)

- **Show, don't explain.** Feelings come out in what people do and say. The
  emotional peaks get the least narration.
- **Nobody reads minds.** Mei has known the boy since she was nine. She
  goes by what people say, never by faces, and she knows his words: every
  kind lie he ever told her started with "I heard." Kiana sees only what
  anyone would see.
- **Every conversation comes out of something that happens.**
- **People act inside the relationships they have.** Childhood friends who
  talk over a garden wall. He goes over the wall once, on November 16, and
  into the house again only when she asks him in.
- **Out loud he's never heard of Honkai.** What he knows from Loop 4 shows
  only in where he stands and what he does, and when it shows, someone
  notices.
- **The log doesn't foreshadow.** A childhood beat comes out of what's
  happening that night and means what it means then. If it rhymes with
  2013, that's because the boy kept it, not because the child saw it
  coming.
- **Repeated days don't get retold.** When a day repeats, write the one
  thing that's different and let the rest go by in a line.
- **Fights:** anime style, kits first, beat plans approved before writing.
  His own moves get named and a sound, never an impact frame.

## Beats

### The log (1995 to April 2013)

Cold choices from the white; past each fork he can't steer the boy.

1. **Birth and the porter.** Born on the fourth floor at the end of a night
   shift, named from a crossword. A basket under the nurses' station; the
   night porter teaches him to count cars on the coast road (red ones are
   two points). The aunt's offer: "Ask the porter." "Stay. Who'd count the
   cars?"
2. **Ren (2003).** A boy of six who isn't going home asks if he is. "Yes."
   The face's first use, for something soft. Ren dies three weeks later
   with his shoes packed.
3. **Mei (September 2004).** Nine, two pins, not sleeping. "Sit with her."
   The boy, not Eros: a hundred and twelve ceiling tiles; "That's stupid."
   She falls asleep at thirty-eight.
4. **The ward years.** The car game; the second pillow; Nurse Kanda's "Ten
   more minutes"; the van, told once with her back to the radiator ("You're
   the only one who didn't say sorry"); Ryoma "keeping something asleep";
   New Year's Eve, when the nurses tease them and she says he hasn't even
   asked: he asks at the window and she says no; the next night he
   forgets, and at eleven she says "You didn't ask"; he promises he won't
   forget again, and asks every night he's there after that; the first three cuts with rolled-up magazines in the corridor at
   midnight, and the night nurse sitting down on the floor laughing.
5. **Osaka (2007).** "Go with her," for the gap with letters in it. On the
   floor under the window she takes out one of her two pins: "So you'll
   come back."
6. **The gap.** Dr. Matsuda ("No"; she marries him anyway); she wears one
   pin and says she lost the other; "Keep writing" ("Red ones are still two
   points"); Tokyo ("Don't go"; she loses the final by a kote in the last
   ten seconds; "You weren't there."); the porter's funeral ("Write to her";
   she counts for him and gives every red one four); the scholarship ("Sit
   it. Don't tell her." "Because the boy wants to see her face." "And so do
   I.").
7. **The takeover (April 13, Osaka).** Both loves in him, the ward and the
   roof, and neither moves over. The biscuit tin of letters, the pin in a
   handkerchief. Affection Close and Trust Confides already: "He did." The
   house next door, September.

### Part 1: Coming back (September to November 18)

8. **Mrs. Okada's.** The widow from Loop 4's paper boat lets rooms next door
   and has never met him; a paper boat on his windowsill, which he puts back
   exactly where it was. Two hundred and six strokes at five.
9. **The walkway.** The Lightning Queen goes out of her face. "You came
   back." He holds up the pin; she crosses in front of the whole line of
   cars and holds on. "You didn't tell me." "I wanted to see your face."
   (Fond.)
10. **The confessions.** "You're late." She laughs,
    then "I'm sorry. No." "Tomorrow, then." The marks on the board; Emi's
    money on a yes by Christmas. "That's nineteen." "Twenty." "You're
    counting." "So are you."
11. **The dawns.** Through the hedge at five. Quick Reflexes, then the
    feint: "You're very fast, and you're very stupid. Get up." Shiden from
    six years of practice with a magazine; "You kept practicing." "Five
    o'clock. Every morning. Don't be late."
12. **The club (October).** Rin hates him for a week, until he takes eleven
    head strikes. "Is it true? About the hospital." "Don't quit, then."
13. **The magazine men (late October).** Over the wall: "They say my
    father's going to be arrested. Is it true?" The boy would have said no.
    "I don't know."
14. **November 16.** From Mrs. Okada's window, the three cars and the
    cameras. Over the wall; she finishes her lunch because he told her to;
    "He had two mouthfuls." Her hand on the table, palm up, until the light
    goes.
15. **Monday.** The desks, the marker, and her "I'm sorry. No." said so the
    corridor hears it. "Tomorrow." The vote: fourteen to four, the key into
    Rin's hand, "Nobody checked."

### Part 2: The year as a lie (December to January)

16. **The bucket (Mei).** He's at the door with a towel. "I heard them on
    the stairs." Every kind lie he's told her since nine started with "I
    heard." "You won't need this."
17. **The bike sheds.** He could have fought with nothing in his hands, and
    they'd do it to her desk in the morning. He takes it. The first-aid box;
    she puts Rin on the boards nine times.
18. **December 12.** The receiver doesn't pay; the house is black. "Would
    you come in. Only until I find the candles." Her fingers on his sleeve
    in the dark, thirty-one candles, "Will you stay until I can go
    upstairs," with no please. (Relies; objective 2; [Now that you've
    finished one, the next time you die, it's over.])
19. **December 16 (Kiana).** The lunch knocked down, the gun, "Follow me."
    On the roof: "Who's this? Your boyfriend?" "He's my oldest friend." The
    boy who looks at the floor. [You've stopped breathing.]
20. **The three of them.** The marks in pairs; the third box; Mei always
    between them. "You never look at me. Do you hate me or something?" "No."
    "But you didn't leave her. That counts."
21. **Kiana alone (fight).** The fish market, the radar cutting out, the
    braid, the Kaslana Headbutt. Mei-senpai's oldest friend never asks where
    she goes at night.
22. **The last day of term (Kiana).** "Do you like her? Actually like her?"
    "Yes." It's true; the lie is elsewhere, and she can't find it. "You want
    her to need you. I want her to be okay."
23. **The port invitation.** In front of Mei: "Come to the port with me
    tonight." "No. I'm busy tonight." Under the boxes Mei's free hand stops
    holding her wrist.
24. **The pin at the gate (Mei, then Eros).** Everyone went, and a boy of
    twelve on the train to Osaka. "Do you still have it?" The handkerchief;
    two pins in her hair for a moment, as at nine; "You came back. So you'll
    stay." (In Love, Bending.)
25. **The family sword (Mei).** Mr. HOMU's suit on January 11. At dawn her
    father's sword through the straw: "It's the last thing they see." He
    has the shapes from the corridor and cuts like a boy with a magazine,
    stopping at the skin. "Through. Not to. Through." The fifth time; she
    turns away to the rack. Ikazuchi, Narukami; no fourth. The red post
    office van from the step: "Two."
26. **Kuroda's pipe (fight).** Four boys and a scaffold pipe in the sleet.
    The umbrella: Shiden into Goto's throat; Ikazuchi through Ishida's pipe
    arm into his collarbone, and the umbrella explodes. [With a blade,
    that's two dead.] Then Sato and Kuroda from behind, and a cracked rib.
    She straps it without a word.
27. **The kote (Mei).** PE kendo; Kuroda's wrist; "It slipped." She lets him
    see it didn't. (Compromised.)
28. **The dream (Mei).** She killed him on a roof; she wakes counting the
    ceiling boards. She tells him at the door. "Dreams are just dreams."
    [The runs leave marks.]

### Part 3: February 4

29. **The roof at lunch (Mei).** His box on her knees, the first lesson she's
    ever missed, because he said he'd come back up. The voice in her own
    words: "He's late"; two mouthfuls; "He went to Osaka." "He came back." "He didn't
    come to Tokyo." She has no answer to Tokyo. The rice on the concrete;
    "Yes." Her last pin leaves her hair.
30. **The stairs (Eros, fight).** The staff room windows; Natsumi; Kiana's
    "It's a STICK" and "You can DO that?"; the blackout lit only by her
    gunfire. Shiden, Ikazuchi, and Narukami for Mr. Fujita.
31. **The roof (Kiana, then Eros; boss fight).** Her bullets burn and it
    never looks at her; the catch by the throat behind its back; her blood
    drinks it; "Not you." Then "You." His Shiden meets hers, the line she
    taught him at ten; the practice sword burns to the handle. "Kneel,"
    twice, and he keeps walking. "Mine." The hand in his chest. He smiles at
    her, the face doing what he drew it for. The pin, the twin of the one in
    the rice: "A hundred and twelve. Ceiling tiles. In the ward. You fell
    asleep at thirty-eight." "You gave me this so I'd come back. I came
    back, Mei." Count with me; "Three" from inside; she pulls the lightning
    out and it takes a stage (Torn). Kiana's hand: "It doesn't matter what
    you did. You're Mei." "I'm sorry, Kiana-chan. No." (Fallen.) "I love
    you." The kiss. No ship comes. They count for him, and he dies at
    thirty-eight.
32. **Settlement.** Four of eight, grade A. He takes Honkai Resistance, the
    pin and Raiden Kenjutsu, and leaves Liar's Face: he used it on Ren, on
    Kiana and on a roof, and can't tell any more which of those were lies.
    Crossroads.
33. **The waking.** Doudou. The boy comes back with the ward and the tin of
    letters. "I didn't have a handkerchief." 1,184 HW over Nagazora; the
    Hyperion lit and tethered; three days, or four, and he can't tell anyone
    how he knows.
