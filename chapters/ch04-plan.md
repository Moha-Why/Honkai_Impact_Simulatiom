# Chapter 4 plan: Five Hundred Centimeters (Loop 4)

Status: written (plan v6, about 24,000 words in
[ch04-five-hundred-centimeters.md](ch04-five-hundred-centimeters.md)). A
romance first; the action comes at the end. Replaces "White Comet" (v5) and the eight-loop
Chapter 4 (in [../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/](../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/)).

Title: in the official text *And the Thunder Cried* (且听雷鸣), Mei says
"We were closest when the distance between us was only 500 centimeters."
In this file five hundred centimeters is the gap between Mei, behind a bait
shack at the end of the breakwater, and the place where Eros confesses to
Kiana.

## Points of view

Eros stays first person and carries the loop. Mei and Kiana get scenes in
close third person, past tense, one point of view per scene, each in her
own voice. They never know about the System. Every one of their scenes
shows something Eros can't see, and together they let the reader know
before he does that the run is real.

## Rules this loop runs on

- **The menu and the body.** In the white his forks are cold. Once he takes
  over, the boy's eighteen years are his, and he plays it straight.
- **Never Kiana.** He never moves against her. He knows what she is (K-423,
  Otto's, watched) and hides that he knows, even fighting beside her.
- **The bullying is shown.** Every blow is a scene with people in a place,
  it escalates, and it lands on him too.
- **Meters only at thresholds.** No objective may complete in this loop, so
  Trust never reaches Relies.

## Part 1: Every school day (September to November 15)

1. **The white.** Frayed, three files. His plan for Loop 4 is the first
   file's plan, done right: say it every school day, never use what he knows
   on her, never dig, stay off every list, and be alive in December. In the
   white it's a route.
2. **The log.** The port home, every fork cold, one line of reason each:
   the mandarin box; the Okadas (under the table); Homu Happy birthdays;
   Ryoma in a plain suit (wrong on purpose); Daichi's dinners (tell); Haru
   at the gate (the truth, early); Daichi at the noodle stall (say nothing);
   the pencils (keep both).
3. **The takeover.** The boy's eighteen years arrive as his own, and the
   forks with them, with everything the boy felt. The apartment two minutes
   from the station. September. Before school starts he thanks Daichi at the
   noodle stall, the first words between them in four years ("The Kobe place
   was all right. They had a pool table.").
4. **The gate and the next desk.** The fan's lurch at Frayed, delighted.
   Class 3-A, Mr. Fujita, the next desk, the lift smile.
5. **Every school day.** From his second week, at her desk at eight-twenty:
   "Raiden-san. I like you. Will you go out with me?" / "I'm sorry. No."
   The first day he says "Okay. I'll ask again tomorrow"; after that, just
   "Tomorrow." The marks in the corner of the blackboard. The class runs a book on how
   long he'll last.
6. **October: the man at the rank (one morning from Mei, then Eros).**
   - Mei's morning (the second Thursday): she has counted twenty-nine.
     Sayaka shows her the class chat (the chalk marks with a crown drawn
     over them); Natsumi works out that he's on her father's scholarship,
     "so your dad's basically paying him to do it." Mei thinks he's like any
     other boy who confessed to her: he wants her father's name or the
     bragging rights, and he has more reason than most. She expected him to
     quit after a week. What she minds is the show. She looks up early only
     to get it over with, and every morning nearly says "Please stop" (the
     pause Eros took for a good sign). Sayaka's phone clicks.
   - Fridays: kendo until six, the gate locked, so the car waits at the
     taxi rank by the station. His apartment is behind the station, so the
     hill is his way home; he stops crossing the road to avoid her.
   - The last Friday in October (the 25th): Nozaki from Weekly Nagazora is
     waiting at the rank. The prosecutors have been into ME Corp's head
     office twice; has anyone been to the house; will her father be
     arrested. Mei can't walk away from an adult asking her questions.
   - **Lie one:** Eros tells the reporter her father's lawyer is behind the
     car's black back windows, and she's a minor. Tanabe really is at the
     wheel on the phone (to the house), frowning, which sells it. The
     reporter goes.
   - Mei, at the open car door: "There's nobody in the back of the car." /
     "You didn't even stop to think about it." She still reads him as the
     scholarship boy: "If it's for my father, I won't tell him." He doesn't
     argue. Then the real question: is
     it true? He knows the date, and he knows from Loop 1 that the arrest
     can't be moved, so all he can choose is what her next three weeks are
     like. He tries true things first (the harbor master the magazine chased
     for a month and never proved; he leaves out that the man was guilty).
     She notices he hasn't answered: "That isn't what I asked."
   - **Lie two:** as few words as he can. "No. It isn't true." She believes
     him. (Noticed to Friendly.)
   - Monday, after her no, quietly: she telephoned her father; he says it's
     the tax office and the lawyers are dealing with it. "The real ones."
     Her father lied to her too. Emi underlines something twice.
7. **November 15, Friday.** Her "No" has a laugh in it. On the hill she says
   her father's coming home tomorrow for lunch, the first time in a month;
   she's going to make him sit down. Eros remembers telling her it wasn't
   true. "Goodnight," for the first time.

## Part 2: After the sixteenth (November 16 to December 15)

8. **November 16 (Mei, then Eros).**
   - Mei makes her father sit down to the lunch she cooked with the cook
     standing beside her (mackerel, the torn skin turned face down). "It's
     very good, Mei." Two mouthfuls, then the doorbell, which nobody rings
     at that house. He isn't surprised. "Finish your lunch. It's a
     misunderstanding. I'll be back for dinner." Four men on the steps, the
     cameras already at the gate (somebody told them when), an officer's
     jacket over his wrists. Tanabe with his gloves off: "Young lady. Come
     inside." She remembers the reporter, the boy who said it wasn't true,
     her father's "tax office." She eats all her lunch because he told her
     to, then washes up.
   - Eros at the noodle stall, ten past one, the arrest on the news. He
     knew; in Loop 1 he tried to stop it and the gray car came, and this
     time he did nothing but tell her it wasn't true. Daichi, as any
     stranger would: the scholarship ("What happens to that now?" "I don't
     know."), then "His kid goes to your school, doesn't she?" "She's in my
     class." "Poor kid." He doesn't go up the hill. He isn't family or even
     a friend, he doesn't have her number, and there'll be cameras at her
     gate. She never brings up what he told her at the rank.
9. **Monday.** Natsumi moves her desk first. "Criminal's daughter" on her
   desk in marker. Sayaka's line. Every other boy has stopped. He says it
   anyway, and her "I'm sorry. No." breaks in the middle. Nobody laughs.
   This is where she starts to change her mind about him: her father can't
   do anything for anyone now, and the scholarship boy is still standing
   there. The falling starts here, not before.
10. **The bullying, each a scene, two or three days apart:**

    - the kendo vote, and her bokken snapped in her locker;

    - his own desk ("the criminal's dog"), his bag in the bins, and Kuroda's

    (Trust Open, then Confides; Affection Close, then Fond; Corruption

11. **The empty house (Mei for the ceiling tiles).** The servants gone, the car gone. She starts cooking,
    badly, and makes two boxes, hers and his. On a night the power's cut she
    tells him, unasked, that at nine, in a hospital, she counted the ceiling
    tiles at night, a hundred and twelve, because nobody else was awake. She
    always fell asleep around thirty-eight.
12. **December 15, Sunday.** He walks her home. At the gate she says
    "Tomorrow," meaning his confession. She's waiting for it now. (In Love)

## Part 3: The girl with the USP (December 16 to January)

13. **December 16, the meeting (both canons together; Kiana, then Mei).**
    Kiana's first morning: forged papers, 3-C, a USP in her jacket, a signal
    on the hill she means to check after school. Mr. Fujita keeps Eros after
    the morning to talk about his scholarship. In the corridor at lunch
    Kuroda's group corners Mei: "Hey, Queen. Who's paying for your lunch
    now?" The homemade box goes out of her hands onto the floor, and she
    stands there and takes it. Kiana, transferred into 3-C that morning,
    kicks the group apart, takes a USP out of her jacket and points it,
    loosely, at nobody in particular, and they scatter. Then: "Hey! Your
    lunch looks delicious. Since you aren't going to eat it, give it to me!
    From now on, you're making lunch for me every day!" And: "Follow me." She
    takes Mei up to the roof with the rescued box. Eros arrives as they go up
    the stairs and follows. The fan knows the line about five hundred
    centimeters and the USP and "ten hours later," and watches it begin with
    himself in it. Out loud he's a boy who has just found a pistol on the
    school roof: he stops in the doorway. Kiana sees what he's looking at and
    zips it into her jacket: "You didn't see that." "Okay." "Who's this? Your
    boyfriend?" "No." "Not yet." "Then I've still got a chance!"
14. **The roof at lunch, three of them.** "If he gets to, I get to!" Two
    confessions a day, the marks in pairs. Kiana and Eros compete for Mei:
    the walk home, the seat beside her, who eats more of her cooking. Mei
    laughs for the first time since November. Kiana frightens the bullies off
    the corridor. Three boxes.
15. **The first hunt (Eros, then Kiana).** In the winter break he follows Kiana one night; he
    knows from the game she hunts Honkai in Nagazora, and he can't stand on
    a roof in February with nothing. The old port warehouses, a pocket of
    high Honkai, turned dockworkers. One comes for him. Forty years of knife
    work in a box cutter, eighteen-year-old thumbs; his body moves before he
    sees the lunge; ten fast meters and then his lungs. Kiana finishes it.
    Out loud he's a boy from the school on the hill who has never heard of
    Honkai (it's cover, but the shaking hands are real): "What were they?
    What was wrong with their eyes?" "You shot them. They were people."
    Kiana: "They weren't people anymore... My dad called it Honkai." She
    sees he isn't coughing and that the knee cut was too clean: "Why aren't
    you sick?" "Sick?" It tastes like batteries, that's all. His cover: he
    followed the port's blackouts. Her radar is her father's, left
    behind with the pistols, and she doesn't know how it works. It's an old
    Schicksal field detector with the logo scratched off (Eros knows the
    sensor head from Hamada's birds on the island, and doesn't tell her).
    The battery spring is gone and she's stuffed foil in, so it cuts out and
    wanders; he fixes it with a bent paperclip by the light of his phone.
    "You fixed it with a PAPERCLIP?" "I'm on a scholarship." She doesn't keep
    him. He's a liar who nearly got eaten in five minutes: "Thanks. Now go
    home." She walks him out to the road by his sleeve: "Don't follow me
    again. And don't tell Mei-senpai. Or anybody."
    - **The deal (Eros).** The paperclip falls out when she jumps off a
      container; with the radar dead she walks the port all night and then
      finds three at once. On the 30th she slams it down at the noodle
      stall: "Can you fix it or not?" A ballpoint pen spring will do it
      properly, and he won't pick it up until she takes him along. He
      carries the radar and finds them, she shoots, and she teaches him not
      to die. Her terms: he does what she says, every time. "You're the map.
      That's all. I'm the gun." She eats his noodles while he fixes it and
      comes for him at ten that night.
16. **The hunts.** She teaches him to fall, then the spare pistol; he learns
    the drills in a week and his lungs don't. From the new year she carries a
    bat to save rounds.
    - **The pachinko parlor (fight, Eros), January 4.** The radar says three;
      the dust says four ([Field Craft: Tracking]). Eros opens it with a plan
      of his own: the squatter left the power on, they go for noise, so he
      throws the breaker and four hundred machines light up and sing, and
      the turned walk into the light where Kiana is waiting on the counter.
      Her shots, then the bat (impact frame, *CLANNNG*). The plan breaks: the
      noise hides the fourth, which grabs him from between two machines. He
      fights it himself: [Quick Reflexes] into the grab, [Field Craft: Joint
      Cut], [Field Craft: Deer Cut], then the spare pistol. Kiana, caught
      watching him with her bat on the floor, finishes the third by shoving
      the end of a row over: forty machines go down like dominoes. "KASLANA
      SPECIAL!" Her reaction to him ("WHAT was that?" "Hunting videos.").
      "There's no such thing." "There is now." Four bruises on his shoulder,
      which Mei sees when his collar slips.
    - Before term starts: her shopping bag in the drainage tunnel, the night
      ferry terminal. Onigiri on the lighthouse steps; the stinky old man; he
      could tell her more about him than she knows and doesn't. At school
      they're tired and bruised and share jokes. "Where do you two go?"
      Kiana: "It's a secret!" Eros: "Shifts."
17. **He realizes.** Mid-January, in the tunnel, she throws herself on a thing
    that's got him by the coat, and they come up out of the water laughing.
    Shown, not said: he takes a strand of weed off her cheek before he can
    stop himself and his fingers stay a second too long; the first-life
    memory comes as one picture (a loading screen on a late train, the doors
    closing on his stop); Kiana stops laughing. "What's that face?" "Half the
    drain." He takes his hand back. [Oh dear.]
18. **Mei notices.** The bruises, the jokes she isn't in, the evenings.
    "Shifts" doesn't say where, and people who work shifts tell you where.

## Part 4: Five hundred centimeters (late January)

19. **Mei follows them (Mei).** Saturday, January 25. She's seen the bruises
    and heard "Shifts" too often. From the station she follows them along the
    port to the breakwater at dusk and stands behind the bait shack, five
    meters from the lighthouse steps. She hears him tell Kiana he likes her,
    and the part that damns him: he meant it with Mei once, he doesn't know
    when he stopped meaning it, and he didn't stop saying it. Kiana goes
    still, looks over her shoulder at the sea, and says "Me?" From five
    meters that's a yes. Mei runs, and a can she kicks rolls along the
    concrete. The dark comes up in her on the way home (they lied at every
    lunch; every "tomorrow" was a lie; at the rank in October he told her it
    wasn't true about her father, and she believed him because she wanted
    to; they'll leave her the way everyone left in November), and the
    streetlights go out as she passes under them.
20. **Kiana's answer (Eros).** He hears the can and thinks it's the wind.
    - The quiet: "Me?", very small, and her eyes go to the sea behind her as
      if he must mean someone standing in it. She's never been anyone's
      first choice, and she doesn't say so.
    - The mask, lightly: a grin goes on like a hat. "Huh. That's a first."
    - Then the strength: "You said it to Mei-senpai every morning. I was
      there for seventeen of them. You don't get to make me the one who
      wrecked it."
    - The punch, hard enough to sit him down on the concrete: "That's from
      Mei-senpai."
    - She sits beside him, legs over the edge, and for a while says nothing.
      Then she bumps his shoulder with hers. "And this is from me. No." He
      says it out loud: "You didn't say you don't like me." She can't lie, so
      she doesn't answer; she stands up.
    - The cold drop: she stands; her voice goes down and her sentences go
      short. "Monday, you say it to her. Like always." "Kiana..." "And you
      mean it this time." She walks back past the empty bait shack and
      doesn't look back.
21. **The aftermath (Kiana).** She walks the whole breakwater without hurrying,
    because he might be watching. Then she goes hunting alone, a signal in
    the warehouses she'd been saving for the two of them, and fights faster
    and meaner than she needs to, and empties a magazine into something
    already down, and is angry at herself for the wasted rounds; the stinky
    old man would have had her doing push-ups for that. On top of a container
    she eats the salmon onigiri he gave her at dusk, because she doesn't
    waste food. She counts Mei-senpai's people on her fingers (him, herself)
    and looks at the two fingers for a long time. In the notebook of things
    to tell her father when she finds him she writes: "A boy confessed to me.
    I said no. You'd have laughed." She crosses out the last part and writes
    "You'd have punched him. I did it for you." Under it she writes "I liked"
    and crosses it out until the pen goes through. Across the water there's
    one light on in the big house on the hill. She thinks Mei-senpai is
    studying. (No narration explains her reasons; the fingers and the
    notebook carry them.)
22. **Where they stand.** Two wrong secrets. Eros and Kiana think Mei is upset
    about the evenings they disappear, so they think the secret is the
    hunting. Mei is upset about the lighthouse. Kiana can't lie, and would
    answer honestly if Mei asked her straight out, so Mei never asks straight
    out: she's too proud to hear them lie to her face, and too afraid of the
    answer.
    - **Eros acts normal.** Kiana told him "like always," and he does it to the
      letter, with Liar's Face. He respects her no and still loves her, and
      doesn't know whether he means it with Mei.
    - **Kiana can't.** She can't lie, so her body can't fake normal either.
      She still shows up for everything (the lunches, the hunts, her morning
      confession, louder now), but around him she gives herself away: she
      stops stealing his food, the loudest tell there is, because she steals
      everyone's; she sits on Mei's far side; she jumps when their fingers
      touch over the thermos; she looks at the sea when he talks; once she
      calls him "Eros-san" and shouts "EROS" to cover it. She pushes him and
      Mei together, clumsily. [She's terrible at this. You're wonderful.]
    - **Mei reads them backwards.** Kiana can't lie; everyone knows it. So
      when Kiana can't look at him, that's the truth, and when he looks at
      Kiana as he always has, that's the lie. She holds on to him (he was
      hers first), goes cold on Kiana (the thief), and tests them both, never
      directly.
23. **Monday morning (Mei, then Eros).** At eight-twenty he says it, like
    always, with his face perfect, and she has heard him say he stopped
    meaning it. For the first time since September she says nothing at all.
    Then Kiana, from the doorway: "Mei-senpai! I love you! Go out with me!" Mei:
    "I'm sorry, Kaslana-san. No." Kiana flinches at the surname. The person at
    the back makes one mark and doesn't know what to do about the other.
    (Eros: Corruption Compromised. [Two steps in one night. What did you do?]
    "Nothing.")
24. **Monday lunch (Eros).** Two boxes, not three. "I'm sorry, Kaslana-san. I
    didn't have time." It's the first lie he's ever heard her tell; she cooks
    at four in the morning. Kiana laughs it off and reaches for his box; Mei's
    hand stops hers: "That one's his." Kiana eats convenience-store bread and
    talks for all three of them, from Mei's far side, and doesn't touch his
    box. He steals a piece of her bread, as he always has, and she flinches.
    The light over the stairwell door buzzes and goes out.
25. **Tuesday night (Kiana).** The hunt, by the rules. He's slow, and a turned
    dockworker opens his forearm. She tapes it on a container lid: "Don't make
    that face. You'll make it weird." "I'm not making a face." "Yes you
    are... no you're not. That's worse." He hasn't stopped liking her, and she
    lets him not say so. She decides Mei must know about the guns, and is
    scared for them both.
26. **The walks (Eros).** Mei waits at the gate every morning and at the shoe
    lockers every afternoon: "Walk me home." At her gate she asks where he
    goes at night. "Shifts." She thanks him for walking her. On Wednesday she
    sees the tape on his arm and asks nothing. On Thursday, holding his
    sleeve: "Would you tell me, if you were going to leave?" "I'm not going
    anywhere." It's true for him, and she hears a lie. "That's what everyone
    says." [She asks a lot of questions for someone who isn't curious.]
27. **Kiana asks (Kiana, then Mei).** In the girls' toilets Kiana corners her:
    "Did I do something? Tell me! I can't read minds!" "No. You can't." Then
    Mei's one near-question: "You'd tell me, wouldn't you, Kaslana-san, if
    you'd taken something of mine?" Kiana, thinking of food: "Is it the egg
    thing on Friday? I'm sorry! I'll make you one!" Mei walks out. Kiana's
    honesty never gets the question it would have answered.
28. **The corridor again (Mei).** With Kiana pushed away, Kuroda's group finds
    Mei alone on the east stairs, and it's December 16 the wrong way round.
    Kiana arrives and they scatter. "I didn't ask you to," Mei says, and goes
    down the stairs. Kiana stands there with her fists still closed.
29. **Kiana leaves them alone (Mei).** Kiana starts going away at lunch on
    purpose ("Toilet! Long one!"), and sends Eros to walk Mei to the station
    café. In the café: "Did Kaslana-san tell you to bring me here?" "She
    said the cakes were good." "She's never eaten here. She can't afford the
    tea." Then: "She's very generous, Kaslana-san." (Mei reads it exactly
    wrong, and the line shows it; no narration explains it.) That night from the station he sees every light on the hill
    come on at once.
30. **The wrong plan (Eros and Kiana).** On the lighthouse steps Kiana decides
    for both of them: Mei is scared because they keep vanishing, so they'll
    tell her about the hunting, the guns, all of it, together. "She deserves
    to know." He agrees. He means to tell her the other thing too, and doesn't
    say so.
31. **February 3 (Mei, then Eros).** At the shoe lockers: "Will you come to the
    roof tomorrow, at lunch? Just you." Her last test: if he tells her himself,
    alone, she doesn't know what she'll do. He says yes.

## Part 5: The roof (February 4)

32. **Lunch (Eros, then Mei at the stairwell door).** He goes up. Mei isn't
    there yet; Kiana is: "We tell her together. Both of us. She deserves to
    know." He can't make her leave. In the stairwell door, unseen, Mei sees
    the two of them on the roof she asked him to come to alone, and hears
    "We tell her together." She goes back down without a sound.
33. **The roof alone (Mei).** After the bell she goes back up instead of to
    class, the first lesson she has ever skipped, and sits by the water tank
    where the three of them ate, with the three boxes she made at four that
    morning without knowing why. At one-forty Cocolia's bomb goes off under
    the school: the burst, the purple air, the clouds coming down. The part of
    her that doesn't need anyone stands up, and its first act is a storm that
    burns every turned thing on the roof to ash.
34. **The stairs (fight, Eros).** Natsumi coughing blood; Mei's chair empty.
    Kiana catches him on the east stairs ("She never came back from lunch."
    "Then come on!"). He leads for the first time:
    - First landing: the baseball-club boy (*BANG BANG*, "Sorry! Sorry,
      sorry!").
    - The second-floor corridor is full and she has two magazines. His plan:
      every alarm on his phone at once, skidded down the corridor to the
      music room; thirty of them run after it. He goes first. The girl from
      3-D at Kiana's back, [Field Craft: Deer Cut].
    - The plan breaks: somebody stands on the phone, and thirty of them come
      back for the only noise left. "Ears!" Both magazines (impact frame:
      plaster, brass rain, the sky violet in the window); the fire alarm,
      the sprinklers. Last magazine, not fired. "Kaslana Snowstorm!"
    - Emi from above, with the cat notebook: his hand stops. Kiana, out of
      bullets, comes up out of the white and swings the extinguisher. The
      notebook open at the boxed total, the ink running.
    - Up the last flight on her sleeve; the door.
35. **The warning (Eros).** The storm comes off her; the title card
    (Herrscher of Thunder); [Wrath of the Violet Heaven]. She talks little and
    never explains Mei's heart: "Humanity... Your sins are unforgivable." "And
    you came up together. Even now." "Give me back my Mei!" "Your Mei?" and a
    look at the three boxes. Kiana: "I said no! I hit him for you! We were
    coming to tell you about the guns!" "'We tell her together.'" Eros: "It
    was only ever me. She said no." "Then you're both liars, and one of you is
    better at it." Turned students on the stairs; he holds the door.
36. **The fight (Kiana).** Canon beats with new moves between them.
    "Thunderbird": the bird pulls out of its dive and chases her along the
