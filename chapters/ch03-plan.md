# Chapter 3 Plan: Transfer Student

- **Length:** about 30,000 words.
- **Narration:** first person, past tense.
- **Covers:** the night of February 6, 2014 (real world), the whole Mei run
  (four loops, October 2013 to February 7, 2014 inside the simulation), and
  the morning of February 7 in the real world.
- **Time skip rule:** opens on February 6 with his status panel.

## Run setup

- **Identity:** Eros, 18 (the simulation makes him eighteen), scholarship
  transfer student from Hangzhou, joins class 3-A at Chiba Academy on
  October 1, 2013. Cheap apartment, instant noodles. Immediate start,
  memories intact.
- **Hidden route:** the empty desk next to Mei's. She can't stand watching
  someone eat badly.
- **Gift draw (kept for the whole run):** Calm Voice (White), Honest Face
  (Green). Passed on: Early Riser, Thick Skin, Iron Stomach.
- **Objectives:**
  - **Love:** receive Raiden Mei's first kiss, freely given.
  - **Fate:** bring Raiden Mei back from the Herrscher of Thunder.
  - **Hidden:** revealed in loop 2. Raiden Mei chooses you over Kiana
    Kaslana. (Never completed.)
- **Canon anchors inside the run:** Mei's father arrested November 16; Kiana
  enrolls December 16; the Eruption hits Chiba Academy on February 7 at
  1:40 p.m.

## Loops

1. **Loop 1, the fan.** First day, the seat next to Mei, "That is actually
   Mei." Instant noodles; the bento. Walking her to Mr. Tanabe's limousine.
   His slip ("Hokushin Itto-ryu") drops Trust to Wary. November 16: he says
   sorry; "Don't. You'll say you're sorry, and you'll mean it for a week."
   December 16: Kiana eats his noodles; the broke transfer-student pair.
   Mei won't meet her. February 7, sixth period: Mei stands up in class, the
   lightning, everyone's eyes light up. He feels himself turning. Kiana runs
   past toward the roof. Death by Honkai corruption (one whole memory).
2. **Loop 2, the planner.** He knows the date and time now. No slips;
   Honest Face. He hacks his way toward the evidence against Raiden Ryoma,
   hoping to give Mei her father back. Introduces Kiana to Mei at lunch;
   the hidden objective appears and makes him feel cold. December 19: the
   man in the gray car shoots him (a scattered detail). Frayed.
3. **Loop 3, the rush.** Roof lunches become their place. After the arrest
   he doesn't say sorry; he sits with her and brings notes, and Trust rises
   to Confides. February 5: he tries to kiss her and she turns away ("Not
   because you feel sorry for me"). February 7 on the roof: the Herrscher
   takes over; he tries to talk; its lightning kills him (Herrscher-level
   soul attack). Worn.
4. **Loop 4, the final run.** Starts Worn: most of loops 1 to 3 is gone; he
   remembers facts, not experiences; the log reads "Cause of death: ███".
   Colder and methodical. Asks her to teach him the sword. After the arrest
   he walks her home every day. He records Sayaka and Natsumi gossiping
   and lets Mei hear it (Corruption: Bending). Introduces Kiana anyway,
   because Kiana is immune and will be there after; declines the hidden
   objective with a cold reason. Kendo lessons in the Raiden dojo; she fixes
   his feet. Late January, snow, the dojo: she kisses him. [Love complete.
   Your next death will end the simulation.] February 7 on the roof: he
   walks through the lightning with Calm Voice, forehead to forehead (the
   gesture is Fumi's), "Come back." Mei comes back. Kiana bursts onto the
   roof. "She makes too much bento. Help her finish it." He dies.

## Settlement and morning

- **Grade C.** 2 objectives (Love, Fate) = 2 picks, at least Blue. Offered:
  Adaptability raise (F to E), bond gift Thunder's Echo (Blue), Hokushin
  Itto-ryu (Basic). He takes the Adaptability raise and Thunder's Echo.
  +650 Fate Fragments (1,000 total). Fate Deviation: Unstable. His identity
  in the branch: dead on the roof.
- **Soul: Torn** (two Herrscher-level soul attacks). Heroine runs locked for
  months; normal runs only, with large gaps.
- **Morning, February 7:** every loop comes back at once. Nagazora readings
  climbing. He knows the time from the run (1:40 p.m.). In the watch room
  with Yoon; Theresa hasn't slept. He sets a timer for 1:40 and tells no
  one.
