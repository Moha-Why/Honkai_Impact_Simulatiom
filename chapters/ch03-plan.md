# Chapter 3 plan: The Lightning Queen (Mei's heroine run)

Status: plan, version 3 (the Life Path is his whole life before 18). Waiting
for approval before the chapter is rewritten. Target length: 42k to 48k
words.

## The spine

Eros plays Mei's run as the corruption route, on purpose. He leans villain,
he believes the System when it says none of it is real, and the System pays
for Corruption. He already knows the worst months of Mei's life to the day:
her father's arrest, the friends who leave, the bullies, the vote, the empty
house. To a villain that isn't a tragedy, it's a funnel.

The Life Path lets him plan his whole life in the run, from birth. So he
fucks around with it: different births, different childhoods, different
doors into her life. Every choice finds out something, and most of them kill
him. The last loop is the one where he gives up control and lives a
childhood with her for real, and then, when his memories come back at 18,
uses it.

Underneath: the thing he builds is the thing he wants. Two lives without a
family, and he engineers someone who needs him completely. It works, and it
feels good. That's the horror of the chapter. It isn't a punishment and it
isn't a redemption.

## The rules it runs on

- **Objectives** only finish the run. Once one is complete, his next death
  ends it. The System doesn't mention that until it happens.
- **The grade:** Love and Corruption, and S needs at least five of Mei's
  eight hidden objectives (her profile).
- **Gifts:** chosen once, before the first loop: Honkai Resistance (Blue)
  and Liar's Face (Green), over Danger Sense.
- **Death resets the loop.** Herrscher lightning is a soul attack and drops
  him a whole stage. Ordinary deaths cost a scattered detail each; enough of
  them wear on him, but they don't drop a stage by themselves.
- **Corruption feeds the Herrscher.** The System never mentions it.
- **Fixed:** the arrest on November 16, Kiana enrolling in December, the
  Eruption at Chiba Academy in February, his place in Mei's class. Canon's
  2004 kidnapping of Mei is fixed too, though the screen doesn't say so.
- **Schicksal** doesn't enter Nagazora before the Eruption.
- **The run ends** when the Hyperion arrives over Nagazora.

## The Life Path (as written)

Every loop opens with his childhood from birth as detailed log lines, like
a normal run. The child lives sealed; Eros watches from the white, and the
System stops the log at forks (usually three options). He picks; the child
carries it out; the rest of the log changes. He takes over on April 13,
2013, and plays the third year scene by scene. Repeat loops say "The same
as the fourth file" and pick up where they change. See simulation-system.md,
Life Path.

Births on offer: Malmö (an ME Corp man), the port children's home,
Mr. Tanabe's sister, a Siberian village, the Raidens' staff cottage, the ME
Corp hospital (a night nurse), and Willow Street as the System's joke.

Loop 2's failure is now a fork: at thirteen he has the nephew tell Ryoma
about Mei's bursting lights, breaking a promise, and she never forgives
him.

### Loop 1: "The Lightning Rod" (about 6k words)

As written: an ME Corp manager's son. The childhood has eleven forks (the
test save: he's learning the engine). The passport in the bread bin (the
pier photograph, Ribersborg98), "how far" (the Book of Bad Days, Cocolia on
page nine), the glass bird blamed on Sigge (Mormor knew), the test (104,
an ordinary boy), the letter to the boss of the glass tower (Ryoma answers,
and in November takes his call), "tell him it's worse" (the false alarm,
the fight), the password (every Sunday for seven years; the audit finds
it), stay with the grandmother, the harbor (Low D holds; Mormor gets Honkai
sickness looking for him), the pen pal (Kuroda, his friend in 3-B, who
writes on her desk), and hiding her forgetting (the stove, the hip). After
18 he plays it as the fan, the warmest he ever is, and he never uses
game knowledge on her. He arrives in September and confesses every school
day from his second week, saying one true thing he's seen her do each time.
3-B calls him the lightning rod and Kuroda runs a book on him. Before the
arrest she doesn't move: "I'm sorry. No.", forty seconds, every day
(Noticed, Wary). In October the System tells him to wait for November; he
refuses to wait for her father's arrest and tries to stop it (the laptop,
an evidence-only call to Ryoma with no names, the folder to Hoshino Kei).
The node holds. After November 16 every other boy stops and he doesn't, and
only then do her meters move (Friendly, Polite by December; "She reads what
people say, and who's still saying it"). The route thought and "There he
is" come here. She asks why at the car. After the audit he decides to stop
digging, and the gray car kills him the next day. In the white the System
tells him she'd have been waiting for him in another week or two, which is
why Loop 8 confesses daily. "The last one standing" never completes: she
isn't waiting yet. **Unlocks:** kendo manager; Tanabe's nephew (birth).
**Lesson:** never use what he knows on her (he didn't, and it was the one thing he got right); Cocolia's people kill diggers.

### Loop 2: "Reroll" (about 1.5k words)

- **Nodes:** Tanabe's nephew from birth, wake at 18.
- She's known the nephew since they were six. For eighteen days it's fine.
  Then she looks at him at the car door and says, very quietly, "You're not
  him." Trust drops to Wary and stays there. He resets himself (a log line,
  never shown).
- **Lesson:** he can't wear a history he didn't live.
- **The System:** "Now you're playing." And, when he asks how to have a
  history with her: "You could always live it." He refuses. Sealed memories
  means giving up control.
- **Unlocks:** an orphan in Siberia. He'd asked himself in Loop 1 where
  Cocolia's people came from.

### Loop 3: "The Inside Man" (about 9k words)

- **Nodes:** an orphan in Siberia, survives the Second Eruption at five,
  Cocolia's house by Lake Baikal, wake at 18.
- **The heart of the loop is Bronya.** Seele and Eros are the two people
  she cares about most, and the loop is built on that.
- **Childhood (logs):** Bronya arrives in January 2012 with a hard face and
  healthy legs; Seele cleans her knuckles; the console ("You're bad at
  this"); she floors an older boy for him ("He's ours. Mine and Seele's").
  The landing, the sea promise. Sin Mal on the cellar stairs (he says
  nothing); in hospital Bronya won't let the doctors near until he and
  Seele are both there. Seele's dark side and Sin Mal.
- **April to September 2013 (immersive):** breakfast (she eats his bread;
  Seele gives him hers). Spring on the landing; Bronya watches the two of
  them as a sniper watches two doors; her list ("Work it out"). August 12,
  Cocolia chooses Bronya; Seele shows her mark, takes her place, and asks
  Eros only to keep it from Bronya and look after her ("Keep my place").
  The bunker; Seele comes apart. Bronya at breakfast looks at him: "You
  knew." Knife at her own throat; he pushes her to the egg and she holds
  his hand anyway, because nobody else is left on the list ("Look after the
  book"). Nineteen days. She comes back paralyzed and empty and recites
  Seele's words: "Seele said, he promised me he would look after you. So go
  back and let him." She follows him everywhere; "Subject Eros will be here
  tomorrow" every night; the almost-smile. Cocolia, frightened, sends him to
  Japan. At the gate: "Then Bronya will come later."
- **Nagazora (immersive):** orders in an envelope, the garage on Thursdays,
  Bronya's call every Sunday at nine ("Bronya has beaten your score"), the
  Mei route sanctioned and climbing. The hotel in December: Bronya asks
  where he is every night, "not even Seele." He lies that he'll pull back.
  In January the Sunday calls stop.
- **The death (full scene):** January 12, Bronya at his door. "Bronya is
  the only one he would let in." The bio-chip lifts her hand in stages; her
  face comes back all at once ("Bronya is not doing this"). He tells her
  it's all right. Shot in the chest; she kneels and presses on the wound,
  crying, in the first person for the first time since August ("I tried,"
  "I just got it back and it's this"). "You're bad at this." He dies holding
  her hand.
- **The white:** his hand still closed. The System: it was the first thing
  she'd felt since August. "You never told her about the stairs."
- **Lesson:** Cocolia's plan needs Mei alone and removes anything she holds
  on to. Stay invisible to the operation. Bronya is coming for Mei at the end.
- **Unlocks:** Mr. HOMU's other student (Cocolia's file on the Raidens
  mentioned a second child in the dojo, years ago).

### Loop 8: "The Pin" (about 13k words)

**The childhood (log lines, real, with no adult in it):**

  one reads the boy's feelings like a file and decides what to do with them.

**The third year:**

- **The daily confessions** become a childhood friend's joke, then a

- **November 16 and after,** as in the current Loop 4: he's with her when

**The Eruption and the roof (rewritten):**

- The outbreak at Chiba Academy, on its date. Because Corruption has been
  feeding it all winter, the first spike is far above canon. Theresa's
  standing order (anything over ten, call me) and the Hyperion's two-hour
  standby mean it launches at once. That's why it arrives early.
- He gets through the school on the Resistance gift, knife work and
  Sprinter.
- **The roof:** the persona has her. It's possessive of Mei and hates what
  Mei needs. "She's mine. You only rented her." Kiana fights it.
- **The hand:** it turns away from Kiana and puts its hand, lightning and
  all, through the right side of his chest.
  - The lightning cauterizes and burns the nerves, so the pain arrives as

  - It misses the heart, which gives him minutes.
- **The act:** with her hand still in his chest, he smiles. Liar's Face holds
  his face while his voice shakes and his breath bubbles. He takes the pin out
  of his pocket and holds it up where she can see it, and says the words: the
  children's ward, the counting, "So you'll come back," "I came back." Every
  word is true, and every word is an act.
- **Mei comes back through him,** not Kiana. She pulls the lightning out of
  him the moment she surfaces. That's why it stops being a soul attack and
  becomes a wound. She breaks down, kneeling, with his head in her lap.

  up. Mei won't let go of him. Corruption: Fallen.
- **Her own words:** she tells him she loves him while he's still alive, and
  she kisses him. Both objectives are complete now.
- **The Hyperion's lights** come over the roof. He dies at that moment,
  with the pin in her hand.

## Settlement and waking

- **Grade A, 3 of 5 cards:** Honkai Resistance (Blue), Liar's Face (Green),
  **Raiden Mei's Hair Pin (Gold item)**, Raiden Kenjutsu (Master), and double
  payout.
- **The pin carries the bond:** Honkai lightning knows whoever carries it.
  Lose the pin, lose the effect.
- **He takes** Resistance, the pin and the kenjutsu, and leaves Liar's Face.
  He tells himself the pin is the bond gift in a convenient shape.
- **Hidden rule (only the System knows):** items from a living heroine's run
  are her real things. The real Mei loses that pin the same night.
- Crossroads unlocks. Soul Torn; heroine runs locked for months.
- **Waking:** the pin is in his hand. His memories return, including the
  childhood he lived in Loop 8, so the merge is heavier. He examines the
  pin like an engineer (metal, lacquer, no maker's mark) and decides it's
  something the System made. The watch room, 1,184 HW, "That's a
  Herrscher," the Hyperion leaving the north tower.

## What the run changed (hidden from Eros)

  happened.

  lands. (He took the pin out of her hand.)
