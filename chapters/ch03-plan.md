# Chapter 3 plan: The Lightning Queen (Mei's run, part 1 of 3)

Status: written. Mei's run is three chapters and five loops (plan v5):
Chapter 3 is the opening and Loops 1 to 3; [ch04-plan.md](ch04-plan.md) is
Loop 4 (Five Hundred Centimeters); [ch05-plan.md](ch05-plan.md) is Loop 5
(Liar's Face), the settlement and the waking. The spine, rules, Life Path, continuity checks,
canon facts and fork pass below apply to all three. The eight-loop version
(plan v4) is in [../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/](../archive/mei-run-eight-loops/).

## The spine

Eros plans Mei's run as the corruption route. He believes the System when it
says none of it is real, and the System pays for Corruption. He already
knows the worst months of Mei's life to the day: her father's arrest, the
friends who leave, the bullies, the vote, the empty house. From the white,
that isn't a tragedy, it's a funnel.

**The menu and the body.** The Life Path lets him plan a whole life from
birth, and before 18 it's a menu: log lines, forks, a white room. He can be
cold there, and he is; he picks a child's forks for one-line reasons. At the
takeover the boy's eighteen years arrive as his own memories, and from then
on the world is too real to play coldly. Coldness in the body has to be
earned over many runs, not decided in a few loops. The run is the fight
between the two places.

- **Loops 1 to 3:** the fan at his warmest, then a reroll, then Cocolia's
  operative. He dies in each.
- **Loop 4 (Five Hundred Centimeters):** a romance. He says it at her desk
  every school day and stands next to her through the bullying until he's
  the most important person in her life. Then Kiana walks in with a USP and
  takes Mei's lunch, and he falls for his first love on night hunts at the
  port. He confesses to Kiana at the lighthouse; Mei, five meters away,
  runs before Kiana says no. On the roof Mei's other personality breaks
  Kiana first, then kills him when he runs to her.
- **Loop 5 (Liar's Face):** the same year played as a lie. He never follows
  Kiana, never looks at her where Mei can see, learns the sword from Mei,
  and on the roof walks to Mei instead of Kiana. Liar's Face does its job,
  and it works.

Underneath: the thing he builds is the thing he wants. He engineers someone
who needs him completely. It works, and it feels good. That's the horror of
the run. It isn't a punishment and it isn't a redemption.

## The rules it runs on

- **Objectives** only finish the run. Once one is complete, his next death
  ends it. The System doesn't mention that until it happens.
- **The grade:** Love and Corruption, and S needs at least five of Mei's
  eight hidden objectives (her profile).
- **Gifts:** chosen once, before the first loop: Honkai Resistance (Blue)
  and Liar's Face (Green), over Danger Sense.
- **Death resets the loop.** Herrscher lightning is a soul attack and drops
  him a whole stage. Ordinary deaths cost a scattered detail each; enough of
  them wear on him, but they don't drop a stage by themselves.
- **Corruption feeds the Herrscher.** The System never mentions it.
- **Fixed:** the arrest on November 16, Kiana enrolling in December, the
  Eruption at Chiba Academy in February, his place in Mei's class. Canon's
  2004 kidnapping of Mei is fixed too, though the screen doesn't say so.
- **Schicksal** doesn't enter Nagazora before the Eruption.
- **The run ends** when the Hyperion arrives over Nagazora, three or four days
  after the Eruption (canon), if he's still alive by then.


## The Life Path (as written)

Every loop opens with his childhood from birth as detailed log lines, like
a normal run. Eros watches from the white, and the System stops the log at
forks (usually three options). He picks; the child carries it out; the rest
of the log changes. He takes over on April 13, 2013, and plays the third
year scene by scene. Repeat loops say "The same as the fourth file" and pick
up where they change. See simulation-system.md, Life Path.

**Immersion.** At the takeover the boy's eighteen years arrive as Eros's own
memories, with everything the boy felt in them. The forks he chose coldly
from the white come back as things he did.

Births on offer: Malmö (an ME Corp man), the port children's home,
Mr. Tanabe's sister, a Siberian village, the Raidens' staff cottage (never
taken), and Willow Street as the System's joke. Loops 4 and 5 both use the
port children's home.

Loop 2's failure is now a fork: at thirteen he has the nephew tell Ryoma
about Mei's bursting lights, breaking a promise, and she never forgives
him.

### Loop 1: "The Lightning Rod" (about 6k words)

As written: an ME Corp manager's son. The childhood has eleven forks (the
test save: he's learning the engine). The passport in the bread bin (the
pier photograph, Ribersborg98), "how far" (the Book of Bad Days, Cocolia on
page nine), the glass bird blamed on Sigge (Mormor knew), the test (104,
an ordinary boy), the letter to the boss of the glass tower (Ryoma answers,
and in November takes his call), "tell him it's worse" (the false alarm,
the fight), the password (every Sunday for seven years; the audit finds
it), stay with the grandmother, the harbor (Low D holds; Mormor gets Honkai
sickness looking for him), the pen pal (Kuroda, his friend in 3-B, who
writes on her desk), and hiding her forgetting (the stove, the hip). After
18 he plays it as the fan, the warmest he ever is, and he never uses
game knowledge on her. He arrives in September and confesses every school
day from his second week, saying one true thing he's seen her do each time.
3-B calls him the lightning rod and Kuroda runs a book on him. Before the
arrest she doesn't move: "I'm sorry. No.", forty seconds, every day
(Noticed, Wary). In October the System tells him to wait for November; he
refuses to wait for her father's arrest and tries to stop it (the laptop,
an evidence-only call to Ryoma with no names, the folder to Hoshino Kei).
The node holds. After November 16 every other boy stops and he doesn't, and
only then do her meters move (Friendly, Polite by December; "She reads what
people say, and who's still saying it"). The route thought and "There he
is" come here. She asks why at the car. After the audit he decides to stop
digging, and the gray car kills him the next day. In the white the System
tells him she'd have been waiting for him in another week or two, which is
why the boy in Loop 5 confesses daily. "The last one standing" never completes: she
isn't waiting yet. **Unlocks:** kendo manager; Tanabe's nephew (birth).
**Lesson:** never use what he knows on her (he didn't, and it was the one thing he got right); Cocolia's people kill diggers.

### Loop 2: "Reroll" (about 1.5k words)

- **Nodes:** Tanabe's nephew from birth, wake at 18.
- She's known the nephew since they were six. For eighteen days it's fine.
  Then she looks at him at the car door and says, very quietly, "You're not
  him." Trust drops to Wary and stays there. He resets himself (a log line,
  never shown).
- **Lesson:** he can't wear a history he didn't live.
- **The System:** "Now you're playing." And, when he asks how to have a
  history with her: "You could always live it." He refuses. Sealed memories
  means giving up control.
- **Unlocks:** an orphan in Siberia. He'd asked himself in Loop 1 where
  Cocolia's people came from.

### Loop 3: "The Inside Man" (about 9k words)

- **Nodes:** an orphan in Siberia, survives the Second Eruption at five,
  Cocolia's house by Lake Baikal, wake at 18.
- **The heart of the loop is Bronya.** Seele and Eros are the two people
  she cares about most, and the loop is built on that.
- **Childhood (logs):** Bronya arrives in January 2012 with a hard face and
  healthy legs; Seele cleans her knuckles; the console ("You're bad at
  this"); she floors an older boy for him ("He's ours. Mine and Seele's").
  The landing, the sea promise. Sin Mal on the cellar stairs (he says
  nothing); in hospital Bronya won't let the doctors near until he and
  Seele are both there. Seele's dark side and Sin Mal.
- **April to September 2013 (immersive):** breakfast (she eats his bread;
  Seele gives him hers). Spring on the landing; Bronya watches the two of
  them as a sniper watches two doors; her list ("Work it out"). August 12,
  Cocolia chooses Bronya; Seele shows her mark, takes her place, and asks
  Eros only to keep it from Bronya and look after her ("Keep my place").
  The bunker; Seele comes apart. Bronya at breakfast looks at him: "You
  knew." Knife at her own throat; he pushes her to the egg and she holds
  his hand anyway, because nobody else is left on the list ("Look after the
  book"). Nineteen days. She comes back paralyzed and empty and recites
  Seele's words: "Seele said, he promised me he would look after you. So go
  back and let him." She follows him everywhere; "Subject Eros will be here
  tomorrow" every night; the almost-smile. Cocolia, frightened, sends him to
  Japan. At the gate: "Then Bronya will come later."
- **Nagazora (scenes):** orders in an envelope; Bronya's call every Sunday
  at nine ("Bronya has beaten your score"). The north stairwell: Mei finds
  him with noodles, gives him her omelette, and he gives her Seele's death
  as the truest thing he has (Open). Level three: the man writes nothing
  down; Eros tells him about the photograph in her lunch-box lid ("That's
  useful"). November 16, the bench under the cherry tree. The blows, each a
  scene: her mother's cancer record taped in her shoe locker ("THE RAIDEN
  CURSE"), traced to his report; at the dark gate, "I'm glad she isn't here
  to see this"; the kendo board takes her captaincy, and he says his first
  cruel thing about someone else ("They were their mothers'") (Confides,
  Bending); the third visiting form at the detention center and her head on
  his shoulder on the bus; the forum thread with a photo of the two of them.
  The hotel. January 2, the shrine: he tells her not to visit her father on
  the fourth, and she doesn't ("I should have gone") (Compromised). The hotel in December: Bronya asks
  where he is every night, "not even Seele." He lies that he'll pull back.
  In January the Sunday calls stop.
- **Bronya comes to fetch him, not kill him.** Cocolia doesn't kill her
  children, and her people wouldn't dare touch her favorite. January 12:
  "Matushka says come home." He says February. "Then Bronya will stay." She
  sleeps upright in his desk chair with the legs off, and follows him to
  school; Mei: "She doesn't look worried."
- **Captured.** Thursday at level three, the man in the plain coat, two men,
  Bronya in the back seat: "She wants you home... She asked me to say
  please." Bronya: "Bronya did not report the refusal. Matushka knew
  anyway." He's locked in the directors' apartment on the top floor of the
  ME Corp tower. Cocolia at midnight: "I don't kill children... I don't
  lose them, either. Not one more." She'll keep him until February, then
  take them all somewhere warm, "Seele always wanted to see a warm sea."
- **The reset (full scene).** The file is a save he can't use. Bronya stays
  with him and brings Seele's book ("She will want her place"). On the
  third night, on the terrace: "Subject Eros will take Bronya. Subject Eros
  will carry the bags." He goes over the rail. She gets up on the white
  legs in their stages, too slow by about three steps, the legs he let
  break, and says "Eros," his name, the first thing with feeling in it
  since August. The fall.
- **The white:** he can still hear the legs. The System: "Her legs were too
  slow. You picked that. On the cellar stairs." "You never told her about
  the stairs."
- **Lesson:** Cocolia's plan needs Mei alone and removes anything she holds
  on to. She doesn't kill her children, she keeps them, so never be one of
  hers again. Stay invisible to the operation. Bronya is coming for Mei at
  the end.
- **Unlocks:** Mr. HOMU's other student (Cocolia's file on the Raidens
  mentioned a second child in the dojo, years ago).
