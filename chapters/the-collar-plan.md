# Plan: "The Collar" (a new loop in Mei's run)

Status: proposal, not in the chapters yet. Two sample scenes from Stage 3
(the collar, the wardrobe) are in
[the-collar-sample-scenes.md](the-collar-sample-scenes.md). Spine and run
rules: ch03-plan.md. Rules for full loops: ch04-plan.md. About 15k words.

## The loop in one paragraph

After Loop 7, Torn, Eros decides the problem was never trust. She killed
him because she was frightened of losing him, and you can't lose what you
own. So he goes back to the same room above the same wall and makes her
his: not a girlfriend, a pet. He names his price, which nobody else in her
life will do, and after the receiver's men put a number on everything she
owns, she asks him to put his name on her. All winter he owns her
completely: her food, her sleep, her name, who she may speak to. She cooks
only for him, sends away the old man who loved her, breaks Kiana's wrist
when Kiana tries to take her away, and kills a man with lightning because
he says "Now." She never once relies on him. When the Herrscher wakes under
the leash and comes for him, he puts his knife in her hand to save what's
left of his soul, and she does it without asking why.

## Where it goes

- **Between Loop 7 and the current Loop 8.** It becomes Loop 8, and "The
  Pin" becomes Loop 9.
- **Why there:**
  - Only Torn Eros is cold enough. His voice is flat, his earlier loops are
    facts, and his eye on her is an inventory.
  - Loop 7 has just shown him a Mei who kept him. This is his answer: he
    keeps her. It's the darkest idea in the run, and the System has been
    waiting for it.
  - "The Pin" works because he finally gives up control, and that needs a
    fact behind it. This loop is the fact: total control, every meter he
    can buy at the top, and it still ends with her hand on the knife.
  - The soul budget holds. The System's warning ("There isn't room in you
    for another one of the big ones") moves to the start of this loop and
    hangs over all of it. He dies by a knife, an ordinary death, so he goes
    into Loop 9 still Torn.
- **Chapters (recommended):** the run becomes nine loops. Chapter 5 is Loop
  7 and this loop (about 24k). A new Chapter 6, "The Pin", is Loop 9,
  settlement and the waking. All three in Chapter 5 would run about 35k.

## Rules for this loop

- **Nothing sexual on the page** (content-rules.md; Corruption is
  non-explicit, gifts-and-rewards.md). The pet is control, not sex: her
  food, her sleep, permission, who she may speak to, her name, the tag,
  kneeling, waiting.
- **No objective completes.**
  - The kiss: he holds it back as his last move, saved for the node, when
    every meter is at the top. The gray car comes first.
  - Relies: never. She obeys him and never believes him. Being handled
    drops Trust, and her father's words are on the other side of the scale
    (the white after Loop 4).
  - So every death in this loop resets, and he knows it.
- **He can't die to the lightning.** A Herrscher death takes him to Hollow,
  and every choice in the loop is made under that.
- **Full-loop rules** (ch04-plan.md): each meter step takes several scenes;
  she resists, tests him or acts on her own; at least one failed move per
  stage; meter windows only at thresholds; ordinary days between.
- **Who's 18:** Mei, Kiana and Sayaka. Natsumi (until January 9) and Rin
  aren't, and nothing about them is described in the world's fashion.

## Stage 1: The dark window (September to November 15, short)

- He never goes to the hedge. At five he lies in the dark and listens to
  the post, and keeps his lamp off, the reverse of Loop 7.
- He learns her practice by ear in a week and can tell from bed which cut
  she's on (Quick Learner). It tells him nothing about why she stops.
- At school he's invisible, as in Loop 5. The Lightning Queen gives him the
  lift smile.
- The pet shop by the station has an engraving machine in its window, and
  he passes it every day. [Window shopping?]
- Once, in October, she stands in the dojo doors at dawn and looks up at
  his dark window for a long time.

## Stage 2: Food over the wall (November 16 to December 8)

- The arrest through the window; he doesn't go down. At the end of
  November the receiver freezes everything, the staff go, and the car stops
  coming.
- **Her own fight:** she tries to cook. Smoke from the kitchen window two
  nights running, and a burn on the back of her hand at school.
- **His move:** a plastic box on the dojo step at five, no note: rice, an
  egg, the noodle stall's broth in a flask. He's back through the hedge
  before the doors slide open, winded by the wall (Sprinter). The first day
  the box is still there at seven. The second day it's gone, and it comes
  back over the wall that night, washed. Ordinary days: the box goes back
  and forth for a week, and they never speak.
- **The visit (December 7),** her father through the glass. That evening
  she's waiting at the hedge. "Who are you?" "The student staying with Mrs.
  Okada." "Why are you feeding me?" "You weren't eating." "What do you
  want?" "You."
- **The failed move:** too soon. "I'm sorry. No." Her rejection word for
  word, the one she gives boys with letters. The next morning the box comes
  back untouched, and the boxes stop.

## Stage 3: The labels (December 9 to 14, the sample scene)

- **What he doesn't control:** the receiver's men inventory the house for
  three days, in their socks. White tags on wire go on everything: the
  lanterns, the piano, her mother's chair, the plum tree her mother
  planted, the sword her father never used (two hundred and ten). On the
  second day they take the violet stone out of her collar with a little
  tool, because it was bought with the company's money (two hundred and
  eleven). It leaves an empty ring at her throat.
- **Her own fight:** she walks round after them with her hands behind her
  back and thanks them at the door. She takes the inventory to the lawyer;
  nothing.
- The lights at two in the morning. He goes over the wall for the first
  time in three files.
- The dojo, every object in it numbered. "Everything in this house has a
  number on it. Except me." She says her father's words without knowing he
  knows them: "Everyone who comes close to me now wants something." Tanabe
  rings every evening, and she can't see what he wants, and that frightens
  her more than the men in socks. "You're the only other person who said
  what it was." She makes him say the price again, properly.
- **The deal.** His side: everything, and she does as she's told. Hers: she
  never has to hold anything up again, and the thing that turns the lights
  on will have to ask him first. **Her terms:** "Not at school." "And my
  father is never to know." He agrees to both.
- "Then put your name on it."
- **The collar (Saturday, December 14, five a.m.):** a brass tag from the
  pet shop by the station, EROS in capitals, six hundred and fifty yen, on
  the empty ring where the stone was. She kneels in seiza on the boards
  where she made him kneel in the last file. **Her name becomes the
  reward:** "When you've done what you're told, I'll say your name." "And
  when I haven't?" "Then I won't." Then, "Mei." (Friendly, Polite, Bending.)
  [Two hundred and eleven lots. And they missed one.]
- **The wardrobe (December 14 and 15):** she has the mended harness and her
  coat; the tailored sets went on the third day (lots one hundred and fifty
  to one hundred and seventy-three). He spends the boy's savings at the pet
  shop (a big-dog harness in red webbing, a leash), the secondhand shop
  under the arches (a white vinyl bodysuit that covers everything) and the
  hundred-yen shop (a clear raincoat, purple tape), and brings the boy's
  apron from the noodle stall. In her bedroom he cuts the bodysuit to a
  design from the folder while she watches. "Not at school. You agreed."
  "You said not at school. These are clothes." She can't find the gap in her
  own words: "I should have said it properly." She hems his cuts. "Is this
  what you want to look at?" "Yes." "Then I will wear it." "They'll laugh,
  and I'll know who chose it, and they won't." The apron is for cooking,
  "Only you." The raincoat is for the gate, so he can see her from his
  window. She holds out the leash, clip first: "Outside the gate." Outfits:
  image-prompts-world.md, Mei 6 to 9.

## Stage 4: Terms (December 16 to 25)

- **Kiana (the fixed node).** She comes into 3-A on the sixteenth and sees
  the tag before anything else. Hers says KIANA. "Is that your dog's name?"
  "No." "Then whose..." Mei, in the voice for strangers: "Please don't sit
  here. It's better for you if you don't." Kiana sits anyway. By the second
  day she's watching Mei wait to eat until he's started.
- **Term one cracks.** She wears what he cut to school, and the class reads
  it as a girl who can't afford a tailor. She still won't kneel there, or
  wait for him there. Outside the gate she walks a step behind him on the
  leash.
- **Mei wants something of her own.** Kiana tells her about the bench in
  the park. At the gate Mei asks, "May I give her the summer house? It has
  a heater." "No." "She's sleeping on a bench." "I know." A long pause.
  "Yes." Kiana sleeps in the park in the snow.
- **Cooking.** Mei asks to cook for him, because feeding people is how she
  holds on to them. "Only me." From then on there's one box at lunch, and
  it's his.
- **She goes her own way.** Tanabe rings every evening at seven, and every
  evening she asks "May I answer?" and he says no. On the twentieth she
  answers. In the morning she tells him herself, kneeling: "I answered him
  last night. I'm sorry."
- **The punishment, and what it costs him.** He unhooks the tag and puts it
  in his pocket. "When he stops ringing." For three nights without it the
  house burns light till dawn, and the streetlights on the hill go out one
  by one. Taking the tag away feeds the storm, so he can't punish her by
  leaving. He's on the leash too.
- **Christmas Eve.** Tanabe comes up the hill in his own small old car with
  the cake he's brought every Christmas Eve since she was six. Eros watches
  from his window. Mei at the gate: "You drive for them now. For the people
  who have my father's company. Please don't come here again, Tanabe-san.
  I'm not alone any more." (The car-pool fact is true; Eros gave it to
  her.) He bows, leaves the cake on the gatepost, and goes. It's still
  there in the morning, frozen.
- **The tag back.** Christmas morning at five, in the dojo: "He won't come
  back." He hooks it on. "Mei." (Close; Trust drops to Wary; Compromised.)

## Stage 5: Heel (January)

- **Term one falls.** On the first day back she walks through the school
  gate a step behind him in the pet shop harness, with the end of the leash
  in his pocket, in front of everyone, and nobody has asked her to. Sayaka:
  "Did he get that at the pet shop?" "Yes."
- **Cooking for one.** One box at lunch, his. She eats after him. Kiana,
  broke, watches it. "Mei-senpai... is that egg?" "It's his."
- **An ordinary day.** Dinner in the Raiden kitchen, at a table with lot
  seven on its leg. She cooks; he eats and says nothing; she watches him
  eat. Nothing else happens, and for her it's enough.
- **Term two falls.** The January visiting form. She asks to leave the tag
  off for the visit, and he says no. He waits outside the detention center
  gate. On the bus back: "He asked whose name it was." "What did you tell
  him?" "The truth." "What did he say?" "Nothing. He put his hand on the
  glass. Then the guard said time." The bus goes the rest of the way in
  silence.
- **His failed move.** Kiana won't stop coming. He tries to frighten her
  off in the corridor, coldly: what happens to transfer students with no
  address. "No." She hears the lie in his voice (Liar's Face does nothing
  for it) and stays.
- **Love's side, refused.** One night Mei starts to say it, "I lo...", and
  he says "No," and she stops. He's holding back everything on that side
  for the node. [You're very good at the dark half.]
- **The wrist (mid-January).** Kiana follows them up the hill and finds Mei
  waiting at the gate in the snow, as she has for an hour. She takes Mei's
  hand to pull her away: "You're coming with me. You don't have to stay
  with him." Mei looks at Eros. "She's touching you," he says. The bokken
  comes out of its silk bag, the kote, Mei's strike, and Kiana's wrist
  breaks. "I told you not to touch me." Kiana doesn't hit back. She holds
  out her other hand. "Okay. This one, then." Mei: "I'm sorry. No." Then
  she turns to him for her name, and he gives it to her. (In Love, Fallen.)
  [♪] Fate Deviation: Collapse risk.

## Stage 6: The gray car (Friday, January 24)

- **The correction.** The world pushes back on a Herrscher with a collar
  on, and the operation has his name at last. It was on her throat every
  morning at the school gate, and the man in the plain coat can read.
- **The road below the shrine,** the bend from Loop 5, the gray car with
  its lights off. The man from Loop 1 gets out with the gun held down by
  his leg. "You're Eros." Quick Reflexes takes Eros half a step off the
  first shot, and no further.
- **Thunder for him.** "Mei. Now." The streetlights on the hill go white
  and burst in a line toward the bend, the car's headlights blow out, and
  the lightning comes out of her and puts the man down on the road. She
  isn't sorry. She turns to Eros for her name.
- **It wakes under the leash.** Her eyes are pink in the middle. The air
  tastes of a battery, and down in the city something starts to roar. The
  persona comes up through her, and the first thing it goes for is the tag.
  "You put your name on her." Mei's own hand closes over it and won't let
  go, and for a few seconds the two of them fight over his name at her
  throat.
- **The math.** If it wins, it kills him with lightning: the big one,
  Hollow. He has his knife. He puts it in her hand, handle first, as she
  gave him the sword in the last file, and shows her where with two
  fingers. Forty years of a knife know exactly where (Hunting and Field
  Craft). "Mei." She doesn't ask why.
- **The death, in full.** The knife, the cold coming up out of the road,
  her face over him in the snow, violet again. "Did I do it right?" "Yes."
  Down the hill, someone running, one arm in a sling.
