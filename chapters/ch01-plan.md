# Chapter 1 Plan: Overtime

- **Length:** about 13,000 to 14,000 words.
- **Narration:** first person, past tense.
- **Covers:** Eros's last night on Earth to the end of his first day at
  Schicksal (March 2009).
- **Change after planning:** the System and Run 1 moved to Chapter 2, which
  skips ahead almost five years. In this chapter the core only gives him a
  shock.

## What the chapter has to do

- Show the fan. He's warm, funny and a lore nerd, and he's lonely without
  ever saying so.
- Plant the dark undertone: the people who died in Siberia, a draft that
  isn't really a choice, and an orphan nobody will look for.
- Set the power floor: Civilian, Honkai Adaptability F, and he's a man in a
  world where men don't become Valkyries.
- Plant the System's mysteries: the note with his name, the word
  "Imaginary", and the lie.
- Show his clear head once, under real pressure (the draft), without
  explaining it.

## Scenes

### 1. The last night (Earth), about 1,200 words

- 3 a.m. in an empty office, with a release due at nine. Energy drink cans
  on the desk. The second monitor has HI3 open on the event screen, and he plays a stage every time a build runs. A third
  window has a forum thread where he's arguing about how Herrscher
  authorities work, and losing.
- Pressure in his chest and his left arm going numb. He blames the energy
  drinks.
- He opens his contacts to call someone. He scrolls past his manager, his
  dentist, and a delivery driver he once tipped too much, then puts the
  phone down.
- He dies at the desk. His last thought is petty: the event ends in two
  hours and he hasn't claimed the rewards.
- **Notes:** no year and no city. Describe the body plainly. No poetry about
  dying.

### 2. Born again (Hangzhou, 1991), about 1,500 words

- He wakes up unable to lift his head. A blurry ceiling, a fan turning, and
  women speaking a language he knows about forty words of, most of them from
  food delivery apps. He works out that he's a baby the hard way.
- His body panics. His head treats it like a bug report: what he knows,
  what he doesn't, what he can test.
- He overhears Director Zhou with the note that was found with him at the
  gate: one word in Latin letters. Nobody knows what it means. She registers
  it as his name. "It's what was written. I don't change what's written."
- At four he asks her why his name is strange and she tells him about the
  note. It's the same name he had before. That bothers him for about a day.

### 3. Willow Street (1991–2000), about 1,800 words

- Mandarin comes fast, because babies are built for it. By three he
  understands everything and pretends he doesn't.
- In a home with thirty-odd children, nobody looks at a quiet one. He works
  out that being worth something to someone is the fastest way out, so he
  stops hiding.
- At six, Director Zhou catches him doing her accounts in his head. She
  doesn't call him a genius. She tests him for a week, gets him into school
  early, and still makes him wash bowls.
- At seven he tries to get her to put the home's savings into a company that
  doesn't exist yet. She tells him to finish his porridge.
- 2000, age nine: the TV in the common room. A disaster in northern Russia,
  a closed zone, smoke, no numbers. Siberia, 2000. He thinks about HI3 for
  about a minute and laughs at himself. The cook shouts about the dishes,
  and he decides a game world would have better plumbing.
- **Notes:** the TV scene plays as a joke. Readers will know. Don't wink.

### 4. Prodigy (2000–2008), about 1,600 words

- Skipping grades, competitions, a scholarship. University in Hangzhou at
  fifteen. A kid among adults. He eats alone and mostly doesn't mind.
- He cheats with the future. He writes code that's ten years ahead of its
  time and feels nothing about it.
- Professor Han takes him on.
- 2007: Director Zhou has a stroke. In the hospital she can barely talk.
  She taps her bag, and inside is an envelope with "Eros" on the front in
  her handwriting. Inside that is the original note. She kept it for
  sixteen years. She dies a few days later. Four people come to the funeral.
  He does the paperwork because nobody else can. He puts the note in his
  wallet.
- **Notes:** keep the hospital and the funeral short and plain. Don't
  explain what the note means to him.

### 5. weird_stuff_v3 (2008–2009), about 1,500 words

- Bored at seventeen, he builds a program to find disasters before the news
  does. It pulls in seismic data, weather satellites, power outages,
  ambulance counts and rerouted flights.
- It finds events where everything spikes together while the news says gas
  leak, chemical fire, military exercise. They share one signature. The
  biggest is northern Russia, 2000.
- For the second time in his life he wonders about HI3. He searches
  "Schicksal" and gets a German dictionary entry (it means "fate") and a
  metal band. He decides HI3 had Valkyries fighting giant monsters in city
  centers and his world has traffic. His real guess is weapons testing:
  every country has a desert it doesn't talk about.
- Professor Han looks at the map for a long time and tells him to drop it.
  "Some data is missing because someone wanted it missing. Those people
  have phone numbers."
- At 2 a.m., eighteen now, he uploads a cleaned-up version to a preprint
  server anyway.
- **Notes:** this is the first flaw on the page: he's sure he knows how
  things will go.

### 6. The draft, about 2,200 words

- The next evening his door is unlocked. A man in a gray suit (Brenner) sits
  at his desk reading his laptop. A woman (Asami) leans on the wall with a
  long hard case, chewing gum. His upload is gone. His server has been
  wiped.
- His heart is hammering and his hands are cold. His head is clear. He
  counts the steps to the door, and he notes the case, the van idling
  outside, and how calm the man is.
- Brenner speaks first, politely, in careful Mandarin. He reads from the
  file: no family, guardian died 2007. He asks how Eros found them.
- Eros tells him the truth. They've already read the code, so there's
  nothing to gain by lying.
- "What you found has a name. We call it Honkai." Brenner's ID card has the
  cross on it.
- Eros goes still. Inside, everything hits at once: the cross, Siberia, the
  minute in the common room, the joke about the plumbing. He's in Honkai
  Impact. Out loud, he says he's never heard the word.
- The offer is to work for them. There's another option, and Brenner would
  rather not describe it.
- Eros says yes before Brenner finishes. Asami laughs. "Most people ask
  questions first." "I've got a lot. I'll ask them on the plane." He asks
  one real question: is there a salary.
- "Is there anyone you'd like to call?" There isn't. The university will be
  told he took a scholarship abroad.
- **Notes:** the lie about the word is just a lie, with no build-up. The
  fanboy explosion happens inside and is funny; outside he's a confused
  student. The dark part (nobody to call) gets one line, and the scene
  moves on.

### 7. Shutters, about 1,500 words

- A private plane with the window shutters down, which tells him more than
  they meant it to. He asks about Asami's case. "Lunch."
- He works out she's a Valkyrie and has to act like he doesn't know what
  that is. She tells him anyway, bored: D-rank, logistics, medical and
  transport. He almost asks her a lore question that would get him shot.
- The Far East Branch technical center. A portrait of the Bishop in a
  hallway: Otto. The fan in him is thrilled. The rest of him remembers what
  Otto does to interesting people (labs, clones in tanks) and decides to be
  very boring.
- Intake. Asami runs the Honkai Adaptability test: a sting in his arm, then
  a result on a screen. Grade F. "Normal for a guy. Don't take it
  personally." He takes it personally.
- His badge: Software Engineer, Combat Simulation and Battlesuit Systems. A
  dorm room with a bed, a desk, and a window facing a wall.

### 8. Three years of bugs

- Dr. Mori, the binder, the cot and the coffee-can pyramid. The locked
  folder labeled "Apocalypse, T." The core: "Don't lick it." His palm on the
  panel gives a jolt, the light flickers, and nothing else happens.

### 9. The plan

- That night he works out the date of the Third Eruption from the game
  (Nagazora, February 2014): a little under five years. His plan: be boring,
  be useful, learn how the place works, and be far from Nagazora when it
  comes. He writes the timeline as code on a page from the binder and puts
  it in his wallet with the note. Instant noodles, alarm for six.

## New characters

Profiles are in `bible/characters/`.

- **Director Zhou Lan:** runs the orphanage. Strict, dry, cares without
  showing it.
- **Professor Han Wei:** his advisor. Kind and careful.
- **Lukas Brenner:** Schicksal internal security, and his handler from now
  on.
- **Asami Kudo:** D-rank logistics Valkyrie. Chews gum and calls him
  "genius".
- **Dr. Kenji Mori:** his overworked boss.

## End-of-chapter state

- **No System yet.**
- **Eros holds:** the note and the coded timeline (in his wallet), his
  Schicksal badge, Dr. Mori's binder. His laptop was taken.
- **Who knows what:** Brenner knows about the anomaly program and believes
  Eros knows nothing about Honkai beyond his data.
