# Chapter 2 Plan: Five Years

- **Length:** about 10,000 words.
- **Narration:** first person, past tense.
- **Covers:** spring 2009 to the morning after Run 1 (mid-January 2014).
- **Time skip rule:** opens by showing where things stand in January 2014.

## Scenes

1. **Where things stand (January 2014).** Senior engineer, 22, same room,
   ten kilometers on the seawall, F every spring. The Third Eruption is
   weeks away.
2. **The island (2009).** A reclaimed island; cranes; in June a memo says
   Theresa Apocalypse is the new head of the Far East Branch and principal
   of St. Freya.
3. **Theresa (August 2009).** She storms onto the eleventh floor with a
   training gauntlet that broke a fourteen-year-old student's wrist. The
   bug is in "Grandma", the same actuator code behind the 2008 "Subject
   deceased" ticket: a cold sensor reads as overheating. Eros fixes it in
   eleven minutes while she reads over his shoulder. "You're the weather
   data boy." "Grandfather's people sent me your file. I don't read files
   from HQ anymore." A strawberry candy. In the game she looked twelve;
   here she's a small grown woman.
4. **2010.** The Valkyrie Assault Squad is nearly wiped out. Our firmware
   didn't fail. Murata Himeko is on the survivor list.
5. **Running.** Seawall at six, the gym, Asami putting plates on his bar.
   She's turned down C-rank twice: "I fly them back. I'm fine where I am."
6. **Brenner's March coffees.** "Why did you say yes so quickly?" and, in
   2013, "You're very boring." "Thank you."
7. **The work.** He rebuilds his old program as the Far East Branch's
   anomaly watch (2011). Senior engineer (2012). Ganesha appears in India
   (2012); he writes its simulation's movement code.
8. **Mei (autumn 2013).** From public records: Raiden Mei, third year,
   Chiba district, born 1995. Older than in the game. He adds a line to the
   coded timeline.
9. **The readings (December 2013 to January 2014).** Nagazora spikes keep
   growing. He tells no one; he can't explain how he knows, and he thinks
   of Otto and Brenner's folder. The decision takes a minute and makes him
   feel sick.
10. **The surge (January 14, 2014, 2:10 a.m.).** The core churns at the same
    minute as the biggest Nagazora spike. Mori needs a 30-second biometric
    hold. The jolt doesn't stop. [Ding!] The System binds. "Static." "Does
    that."
11. **Host.** The System night, adapted from the old Chapter 1: status at
    22, five springs of F, "Imaginary", the lie, heroine simulations, the
    note, the starter pack, alarm for six.
12. **Run 1.** Unchanged except his age (22, fitter from the seawall).
13. **Morning.** The memory comes back; "fourteen years ago"; Mori asleep on
    the cot; Eros opens the Nagazora feed. The numbers haven't come all the
    way back down.
