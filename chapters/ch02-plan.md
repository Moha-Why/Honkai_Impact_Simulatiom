# Chapter 2 rewrite plan: Five Years

Status: plan, waiting for approval. Replaces the earlier Chapter 2 plan (that
version is written and in the repo; this rewrite expands it).

- **Length:** about 55,000 to 60,000 words, one chapter.
- **Part One, the five years:** March 2009 to January 13, 2014, about
  35,000 to 38,000 words, mostly new.
- **Part Two, the System:** January 14 to February 4, 2014, about 20,000
  words, the existing binding night, Runs 1 to 4 and the Mei unlock, edited
  for continuity.
- **Narration:** first person, past tense, same voice.

## Why the rewrite

1. **Schicksal read like a prison.** Five years in a room facing a concrete
   wall, no leave, no money spent, no trips. Fix: Schicksal is a big,
   secretive employer, not a jail. It has a cover story, a nondisclosure
   contract, monitored phones and a security check on a recruit who found it
   from outside. Staff get salaries, leave and better housing as they rise.
   Eros's solitude stays, but now he chooses it.
2. **The time skip summarized.** Each year gets full scenes, like any other
   chapter.
3. **His life.** Money, places, habits, friends, trips, small pleasures, and
   how they change.

## Chapter 1 edits (small, about +1,500 words)

- **The shutters and hangar:** Asami says why. "Protocol for new hires.
  Your clearance isn't through yet. Next time you'll get the window seat."
- **An onboarding scene on day one,** after the Adaptability test and before
  the badge:
  - A brisk HR officer, Ms. Vogel, slides a contract across.
  - **Salary:** €78,000 a year, paid monthly, with a hazard and secrecy
    allowance. He reads the figure twice.
  - **Leave:** 25 days a year.
  - **The cover story:** software engineer for a German industrial
    automation firm, with a mailing address in Hamburg.
  - **Phones:** calls and internet allowed on the staff network, which is
    monitored. "Assume everything you type is read, because it is."
  - **Getting off the island:** a ferry to the mainland twice a week, and
    staff flights from the runway.
  - **Housing:** junior quarters until he's promoted.
- **The concrete-wall room** stays, now explicitly junior housing. It becomes
  something he leaves in 2012.

## Part One: the five years

Recurring places that give the island texture: the seawall at six, the
cafeteria (curry on Tuesdays, which the island treats as sacred), the harbor
bar, the ferry, the supply office where you can order almost anything with a
reason, Mori's cot, the watch room. Each year opens with a short scene set
in a specific month, never with a summary.

### 2009 (age 18): learning the island

1. **The tour.** Asami shows him the island on his second day: the runway,
   the harbor, the half-built school, the ferry timetable, and the bar.
   "Rule one, never order the squid on Mondays."
2. **Orientation, "history he doesn't know."** A three-afternoon course for
   new staff, taught by an old archivist, Professor Ōta.
   - The sanitized official history: Honkai cycles, Schicksal's founding,
     the Overseer ("who has led Schicksal for a very long time"), the
     portrait in the corridor.
   - Eros has to look surprised at things he knows by heart. That's comedy,
     until a detail differs from the game and he stops laughing.
   - **The Kaslanas:** Ōta calls them the family that served Schicksal for
     generations, and calls Kallen a saint. Eros asks, "Are there any
     Kaslanas now?" Ōta: "Siegfried Kaslana. He left." A trainee mutters
     "traitor". Eros knows about Siegfried and Cecilia, the clone, and the
     girl who'll be called Durandal, and says nothing.
3. **The first payday.** He stands at the ATM in the harbor building and
   looks at the number. It's more than Director Zhou had to run Willow
   Street on for a whole season. He sends a large part of it to the home
   anonymously, with the memo "for the bowls". He buys a good chair, and
   keeps eating instant noodles.
4. **June:** the memo, and Theresa's arrival as cafeteria gossip.
5. **August:** the Theresa scene (kept): the gauntlet, Grandma, the
   strawberry candy, "The World's Cutest Valkyrie."
6. **Autumn:** he starts running (kept, expanded): two kilometers and he
   throws up. Asami in the gym.
7. **A night with Mori:** debugging a sim crash until three in the morning.
   Mori talks for once, about the daughter he sees twice a year and why he
   sleeps on the cot. Mori becomes a person.

### 2010 (age 19): his first real year

1. **Spring Festival, February.** He makes dumplings badly in the
   residence kitchen, alone. Asami and two logistics girls smell it and
   crash it. It's his first Spring Festival that feels like a family one in
   either life.
2. **Brenner's first coffee, March** (kept): "Why did you say yes so
   quickly?" New: "You asked about the Kaslanas in orientation. Why?" Eros
   says he likes old families and old stories. Brenner writes nothing down.
3. **Qingming, April, his first leave.** A staff flight to Shanghai, then the
   train to Hangzhou.
   - He sweeps Director Zhou's grave.
   - At Willow Street he meets the new director, Auntie Qian. The new stove
     and the radiators in the dormitory came from "a donor who writes 'for
     the bowls'". She doesn't know it's him.
   - Lunch with Professor Han, who listens to the Germany story, sees
     through it, and doesn't push. "Are you happy?" "I think so." "Then I
     won't ask about the phone numbers."
4. **Summer:** the Assault Squad report (kept). Himeko is on the survivor
   list, and he runs in the rain.
5. **Autumn, Theresa's history class.** St. Freya lets staff sit in on one
   class a term, and he audits Theresa's history lesson for the first-years.
   - A student asks about Cecilia Schariac, the S-rank who died in Siberia.
   - Theresa answers in four sentences and moves on, and he sees what it
     costs her. He knows how Cecilia actually died. He keeps his face still.
6. **December:** Mori asks whether weird_stuff could be rebuilt with
   Schicksal's own sensors.

### 2011 (age 20): his code saves people

1. **Building the anomaly watch** (eight months): long scenes of real work.
   He meets Yoon Seo-yeon, the watch room's senior duty officer, and argues
   with her about thresholds, then about coffee. She becomes a friend.
2. **Summer leave, Tokyo.**
   - A fan's pilgrimage in a world with no HI3. He goes to Akihabara and
     finds no Honkai anywhere. He buys a handheld and three games.
   - He stands at the big train map at Tokyo station, finds "Nagazora", and
     takes the train the other way, to the sea at Kamakura.
   - A good meal alone, and the quiet realization that he likes his life.
3. **The first catch, October.** The watch flags a small outbreak in a
   fishing town on the Korean coast three hours early. A squad is there in
   time, and there are no civilian deaths. Yoon shows him the report, and he
   sits in the stairwell for a while.
4. **December, a corridor.** Himeko passes through the eleventh floor with a
   recruit, a fierce strawberry-blonde girl of about sixteen. The girl is
   arguing that St. Freya is too slow and she wants to go straight to HQ.
   Eros recognizes Bianka Ataegina, the future Durandal, and knows exactly
   whose daughter she is.

### 2012 (age 21): rising

1. **March, his "birthday."** The anniversary of the day he was left at the
   gate. Asami finds out from his file and throws a party at the harbor bar.
   Mori falls asleep in the corner. It's the first cake with his name on it.
2. **Qingming at Willow Street.** A quiet nine-year-old boy there does sums
   in his head, the way Eros did. Eros gives him a laptop and his email
   address, and pays for a tutor.
3. **Ganesha** (kept, expanded): the flood of data and the two dead A-ranks.
   He writes the creature's movement code. A scarred A-rank instructor
   insists on running the unfinished simulation, fails it, and thanks him.
4. **Senior engineer, autumn.** Mori's machine coffee and "Don't let it go
   to your head."
   - **Salary:** up to €140,000. **Leave:** 35 days.
   - **The flat:** a staff flat on the top floor of the residential block,
     with a sea view. He leaves the concrete-wall room. Choosing furniture
     for the first time in either life defeats him, and Asami chooses it.
   - He builds a home lab, and buys a real kitchen he still mostly uses for
     noodles. Asami: "You earn three times what I do and you eat like a
     student."
5. **The run-away fund** (his practical, colder side): cash in yen, yuan
   and euros in a locked case, an escape bag kept packed by the door, and a
   satellite phone ordered through supply "for field testing". Money, to
   him, means getting out alive.
6. **December, the offer.** The Battlesuit Division at HQ wants someone for
   a six-month secondment on next-generation firmware. Mori volunteers him.

### 2013 (age 22): HQ and home

1. **January, HQ, Germany.**
   - **Arrival:** the city stays deliberately vague. A cathedral of a
     building, the Overseer's portrait again, life-size, and security that
     really is tight here, because this is Otto's house.
   - **His colleague:** Klaus Hoffmann, a cheerful HQ engineer.
2. **Rita Rossweisse.**
   - **His liaison:** she turns 18 on March 1. Perfectly polite, perfectly
     helpful, a maid's uniform, and very obviously the Overseer's eyes.
   - **The archives:** she gives him a tour past the Kaslana portraits.
     Kallen is there. Siegfried's frame hangs empty. He looks at Kallen too
     long, Rita notices, and smiles.
   - **Between them:** mutual wariness, wit, and a seed of interest that
     stays only that.
3. **Bianka, the future Durandal.**
   - **The firmware:** he writes the firmware for her new battlesuit. Her
     age in the records is 18; her real age is 17. She hates the training
     simulations because they're too easy, and she breaks them, so he makes
     them harder until she can't.
   - **Results earn respect:** she respects results and nothing else.
   - **Her losses:** the sole survivor of her first mission, at Agra Fort,
     and she doesn't talk about it.
   - **Strictly platonic:** an annoying genius little sister. He knows she's
     the real Kiana Kaslana, Cecilia and Siegfried's daughter, and he can
     never say a word.
   - **Rita and Bianka:** Rita is newly assigned to Bianka, so he sees the
     very start of that partnership.
4. **Otto, once.** Otto visits Bianka in the simulation room, gentle with
   her, all cold smile with everyone else. His eyes pass over Eros for one
   second. Nothing happens. Eros doesn't sleep that night.
5. **March, Brenner's last coffee, at HQ** (kept): "You know, Eros, you're
   very boring." "Thank you." Brenner closes the folder for the last time.
6. **July, home.** The flat, the seawall and the noodles feel like coming
   home, and he notices that they do.
7. **Autumn:** looking up Mei (kept).
8. **November:** Raiden Ryoma's arrest in the business pages (kept).
9. **December:** the Nagazora readings start (kept). He doesn't tell anyone.
10. **January 2014:** where things stand. He's at the seawall, the flat, the
    escape bag, an F every spring, and the Third Eruption is weeks away.

## Part Two: the System (existing text, edited)

- The core night, the binding, Runs 1 to 4, Asami at the bar, the knife
  practice, the shop, the countdown, the Mei unlock.
- **Every concrete-wall window becomes the flat's sea view:** the binding
  night, Fumi's deathbed ("a flat by the sea"), and the Mei unlock.
- **The System's jabs** can use his new life: the escape bag ("You packed a
  bag for running away. Adorable."), the flat, the money. The shop's "a
  month's rent on a summer banner" line stays.
- **The knife practice** moves to the end of the seawall below his flat.
- **Asami at the bar** can mention he's been "somewhere fancy" since HQ.
