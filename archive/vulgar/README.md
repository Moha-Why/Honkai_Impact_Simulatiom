# Archive: the vulgar edition

Shelved for now, not deleted. Nothing in this folder is canon for the story
while it's here, and nothing in the live bible or chapters depends on it.
The live story uses normal clothes, and Eros looks at women as a fan, not a
lech.

## What's here

| File | What it was |
|---|---|
| [world-fashion.md](world-fashion.md) | The rule that adult women in this world dress in fetish wear as everyday clothing, its age rules, and "Eros and the fashion" (his eye, by soul stage). It was meant to tie into one of the novel's secrets. |
| [image-prompts-world.md](image-prompts-world.md) | Image prompts for the adult women in that fashion, including Mei 6 to 9 for the Collar loop |
| [the-collar-plan.md](the-collar-plan.md) | The plan for "The Collar", a possible loop between Loops 7 and 8 where Mei becomes Eros's pet |
| [the-collar-sample-scenes.md](the-collar-sample-scenes.md) | Sample scenes for that loop |
| [chapter-passages.md](chapter-passages.md) | Every chapter paragraph (Chapters 3 to 5) as it read before the clothes were made normal, by file and line |
| [rules.md](rules.md) | The archived wording of the Corruption rule and of the bible lines that referred to the fashion |

## How to bring it back

- The four whole files go back with `git mv` to where they were:
  world-fashion.md and image-prompts-world.md to bible/, the two Collar
  files to chapters/. Add their rows back to bible/README.md (the rows are
  in rules.md).
- Chapter paragraphs: each entry in chapter-passages.md names the file and
  the line. Every entry is one whole paragraph and was replaced by one
  paragraph, so the line numbers still match the current files unless a
  chapter has been rewritten since. If it has, find the paragraph by its
  first words.
- Rules and bible lines: rules.md has each archived wording next to where it
  lived.
- bible/content-rules.md was never part of any of this and doesn't change.
