# Story Bible

## Style

- Written in English. No Chinese text; genre terms are written in English
  (for example, "[Ding!]" for System notices).
- First person, from Eros.
- Comedic on the surface, with a dark undertone.
- Power progression is central: every run should move his strength forward.
- The shura field, Eros surviving the heroines finding out about each other,
  is a major source of tension and comedy.

## Content Rules

### Romance involving minors

Characters under 18 may have romantic storylines, written to the standard of
closed-door young adult fiction.

**Allowed**
- Crushes, confessions, and nervous first dates
- Holding hands, hugs, and leaning on each other
- A kiss, described briefly and without sensual detail
- Jealousy, awkwardness, arguments, and breakups
- Emotional intimacy: trust, support, vulnerability

**Not allowed**
- Any sexual or explicit content
- Sexualized descriptions of a minor's body, clothing, or reactions
- Kissing or touch written to arouse rather than to show emotion
- Scenes that escalate toward or imply sex ("fade to black" included)
- Romantic or sexual pairings between a minor and an adult

**Mature romance requires adult characters.** If a storyline needs more than
the above, the characters involved must be clearly established as 18 or older
in the text. Their age is not left ambiguous, and they are not written or
described as childlike.

When in doubt, keep the focus on feelings, not physicality.

## Power Scale

Canon HI3 power levels are inconsistent. These are this story's rulings.

### Stats
Physique, Strength, Agility and Intellect share one scale. It is not linear:
each band is a big jump over the last.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 10 | Average adult |
| 20 | Peak human (Olympic athlete, world-class genius) |
| 25–40 | D-rank Valkyrie |
| 40–70 | C-rank Valkyrie |
| 70–150 | B-rank Valkyrie |
| 150–400 | A-rank Valkyrie |
| 400–1000 | S-rank Valkyrie |
| 1000+ | Herrscher |

### Valkyrie ranks
D and C ranks are logistics: medics, comms, drone operators, transport
pilots, battlesuit technicians. They are still trained Valkyries with
stigmata and can fight when they must.

| Rank | Role | Power | Environmental damage in a full fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Logistics | Survives being hit by a car; kills Honkai zombies and lesser beasts with a weapon | Breaks doors, cracks concrete |
| C | Logistics / combat support | Flips a car, fires heavy weapons by hand, holds a corridor against a pack | Punches through brick walls, wrecks a room |
| B | Frontline | Fast enough to dodge small-arms fire; a squad brings down large beasts | Levels a house, craters a road |
| A | Elite, ace of a branch | Solos large beasts, cuts through a tank, brief bursts past the speed of sound | Destroys a building or a city block |
| S | A handful in the world | Can hold off a Herrscher for a while as a team | Devastates a district; craters hundreds of meters wide |
| Herrscher | — | Wields an authority over reality | City-level and up (the Third Eruption levels Nagazora); the strongest are continental or worse |

A few canon figures (Kevin, Otto, Fu Hua) sit outside the ranks entirely.

### Honkai Adaptability
How much Honkai energy a body can take. It sets a hard cap on every
physical stat. Women adapt far more easily than men; most men stay at F
for life.

| Grade | Stat cap | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| F | 20 | Normal human. Honkai energy only harms him. |
| E | 25 | Survives mild exposure. |
| D | 40 | Basic battlesuits and weak stigmata. |
| C | 70 | Standard battlesuits and stigmata. |
| B | 150 | Frontline gear. |
| A | 400 | Elite gear. |
| S | 1000 | Top-tier gear; can touch a Divine Key without dying. |
| Beyond S | None | The Herrscher threshold. |

Stats gained above the cap are **stored**, not lost. They unlock the moment
the cap rises.

**Ways to raise it:** Purple-or-higher gifts that touch Honkai energy,
special heroine-run rewards, and rare items (Honkai crystals, core
fragments), which are risky to use.

### Gear
- **Battlesuits:** combat suits that amplify the wearer. Each needs a
  minimum Adaptability grade.
- **Stigmata:** imprints that grant a figure's strengths. Each needs a
  minimum Adaptability grade.
- **Divine Keys:** pre-era weapons, the strongest gear that exists. They
  kill anyone below S-grade Adaptability who tries to use one.

### Honkai threats

| Threat | Handled by |
|---|---|
| Honkai zombies (infected humans) | D |
| Lesser beasts | C |
| Standard beasts | B |
| Elite beasts | A, or a B squad |
| Titan-class | S, or an A squad |
| Herrscher | S team at minimum |

## Simulation System

The Imaginary Simulation System tells Eros the simulations aren't real. They are:
each run writes a branch into the Imaginary Tree.

### Simulation types
- **Normal simulations:** classic life simulations. The run plays out, mostly
  as log lines, until Eros dies; then he settles rewards based on what the run
  achieved. Death ends the run.
- **Heroine simulations:** built around one heroine, with meters and
  objectives. Death resets the run at a soul cost. Failing the objectives
  lets him end the run or restart it.

### Starts
- Every heroine run gives Eros an identity and a starting point that is
  normal or bad, usually with a hidden route toward the heroine.
- **Immediate start:** he arrives in his identity with his memories intact.
- **Childhood start:** his memories and the System stay sealed until the
  heroine turns 18. He lives the childhood as a real child her age, the
  years are summarized in log lines, and nothing romantic happens.
- Meters and objectives only activate once the heroine is 18 or older.

### Meters
- Heroine: **Affection**, **Trust**, **Corruption**
- World: **Fate Deviation**, which measures how much he has changed
  history. The higher it climbs, the harder the world pushes back.

### Objectives
- Several run at once. Completing one is enough; each extra one raises the
  reward's size and rarity.
- Once at least one objective is complete, his next death ends the run as a
  success instead of resetting it.

### Death and the soul
Soul Integrity starts at 100%.

| Death | Soul Integrity cost |
|---|---|
| Ordinary (fall, bullet, zombie, beast) | 1–3% |
| Honkai-tainted (Honkai energy exposure, corrupted beasts) | 3–8% |
| Minor soul attack (mental attacks, soul-targeting Honkai weapons) | 8–15% |
| Herrscher-level soul attack | 15–40% |

- Repeating the same death changes nothing.
- Inside a run, soul damage costs memories in proportion to the damage:
  past-life memories, things learned during the run, and memories of
  earlier loops. It never touches his foreknowledge of the game.
- Leaving a run restores those memories. Soul Integrity recovers about 1%
  per real day.
- Below 50% he can't start heroine runs; below 30%, no runs at all.
- If he drops below 20% inside a run, the System ejects him and the run
  fails.
- He can die on purpose to force a reset, at the usual cost.

### Fate Deviation
**Per run (0–100):**

| Range | State | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 | Stable | None |
| 30–60 | Disturbed | Honkai activity rises |
| 60–90 | Unstable | Enemies grow stronger; events come early |
| 90–100 | Collapse risk | The branch pushes back with correction events |

**Global (real world):** rises with revivals, echoes and real-world changes.
As it climbs it draws attention: first Otto, then Kevin and the World
Serpent, and eventually the Will of Honkai itself.

### Aftermath
- After a heroine run, Eros's identity in it is treated as dead or missing.
  A later run with the same heroine continues from there.
- Failed runs reset completely. He can retry later from a different start.
- **Pre-era heroines:** a completed run brings her back to the present with
  memories of the final loop only. The edit is local to her own thread. She
  arrives after a delay, often somewhere dangerous, and weak at first.
- **Living heroines:** a completed run collapses into her as echoes (dreams,
  déjà vu, a sense she knows him), stronger with each objective completed.
  The echoes only surface once the real timeline reaches the run's start.

### Interface
- A floating panel only Eros can see, plus the System's voice, which opens
  notices with "[Ding!]". The voice is polite, warm, and a bit too
  reassuring.
- **Status panel:** name, age, Combat Rating, Physique, Strength, Agility,
  Intellect, Honkai Adaptability, Soul Integrity, gifts, Fate Fragments.
- **Combat Rating:** his overall strength shown as a Valkyrie rank
  equivalent. He starts as Civilian (unranked).
- **Starting panel:** Physique 9, Strength 9, Agility 10, Intellect 28,
  Honkai Adaptability F, Soul Integrity 100%.

### Starter pack
A free first run, one free gift reroll, and 100 Fate Fragments.

### Gifts
Gifts are traits. Items use the same color tiers.

| Color | Meaning | Draw rate | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| White | A normal human trait | 50% | Light Sleeper, Iron Stomach |
| Green | A talented human trait, top 1% | 28% | Quick Learner, Perfect Pitch |
| Blue | Peak human, borderline superhuman | 14% | Photographic Memory, Pain Tolerance |
| Purple | Superhuman, Valkyrie-tier | 6% | Honkai Resistance, Danger Sense, Minor Regeneration |
| Gold | Exceptional, S-rank-adjacent; defines a fighting style | 1.8% | Lightning Affinity, Sword Saint's Eye |
| Red | Herrscher-adjacent; touches reality itself, usually with a drawback | 0.2% | A fragment of an authority, Imaginary Sense |

- **Hidden gifts** show as ??? until a trigger reveals their color.
- **Draws:** 5 at the start of each run; he keeps 2 for that run.
- **Permanent slots:** gifts kept after a run go into permanent slots. He
  starts with 3.
- **Upgrades:** merging two copies of the same gift raises it one color.
  Merging can reach Gold but never Red; Red can only be drawn.
- **The male ceiling:** physical gifts still obey his Adaptability cap; the
  excess is stored.

### Settlement
Every run is graded E, D, C, B, A or S.

| Grade | Picks | Rarity bias |
|---|---|---|
| E–C | 1 of 3 | Mostly White and Green |
| B–A | 2 of 3 | Blue and Purple appear |
| S | 3 of 3 | Purple and up; Gold possible |

- **Normal runs** are graded on lifespan, achievements and hidden events.
- **Heroine runs** give one pick per objective completed, and every pick is
  at least Blue.

### Reward types
- **Keep a gift:** move one gift from the run into a permanent slot.
- **Experience package:** skills and knowledge from the run (swordsmanship,
  languages, combat instinct) arrive as muscle memory. Technique can exceed
  what his body can actually execute.
- **Physical template:** 10–30% (by grade) of his simulated body's stat
  advantage over his real body. Subject to the Adaptability cap; the excess
  is stored.
- **Item:** an object pulled out of the run. One per pick, nothing alive,
  nothing larger than he can carry. Divine Keys and Herrscher cores can't be
  taken.
- **Fate Fragments:** currency. A normal run pays 20–200; a heroine run
  pays 300 or more per objective.
- **Heroine-run exclusives:** a **bond gift** unique to that heroine (its
  color rises with objectives completed), her personal item, or an
  Adaptability raise.

### Shop (Fate Fragments)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Gift reroll | 50 |
| Extra normal run | 100 |
| Choose a normal run's scenario | 300 |
| Soul repair (+5%, once a week) | 500 |
| Extra permanent gift slot | 1000, rising each time |

### Entering a run
- He enters through sleep. His body stays put, and one run takes about one
  night however many years pass inside.
- One normal run per day is free.
- Heroine runs unlock when the System decides and announces it.

## Eros (draft)

### Basics
- Named Eros in both lives. An orphan in both lives.
- **Earth:** software engineer and die-hard HI3 fan, of both the characters
  and the power system and lore; died of overwork at
  32 during a crunch, with an HI3 event still running on his second monitor.
- **HI3:** born in 1991, left as a baby at an orphanage in Hangzhou with
  nothing but a note reading "Eros". Who left it is an open question.
- A child prodigy with an adult mind. University at 15; at 17 his
  anomaly-detection software exposes Honkai events Schicksal covered up.
- Drafted by Schicksal in 2009, at 18. With no family, nobody asks where he
  went.
- **Looks:** handsome, tall and slim, black hair, dark eyes, the tired look
  of someone who codes until 3 a.m. Seems harmless, and uses that.

### Personality arc
Eros starts as a warm fan. Each death and failure in the simulations strips
that away and reveals who he really is underneath. Two forces pull against
each other for the whole story:
- **Deaths strip him down:** every death and failure wears away the fan and
  shows more of his true personality.
- **Merges bleed feelings in:** feelings from each run carry over into him,
  the one thing his judgement cannot clear away.

### Starting personality (the fan)
- **Fan at heart:** loves these characters and knows every tragedy waiting
  for them. His inner voice fanboys ("That's Mei. That is actually Mei.").
- **Power-system nerd:** knows how stigmata, battlesuits, Herrscher cores,
  and Honkai Adaptability work, and wants to experience them himself.
- **Shameless dreamer:** completely open with himself about wanting a harem.
  It's a fan's fantasy.
- **Already a min-maxer:** treats problems as systems to optimize, but still
  plays like it's a game.

### True personality (what emerges)
- **Power first:** he knows that as a man in this world, dying is easy.
  Strength is his number one priority, and everything else serves it.
- **Cold manipulator:** he manipulates people without guilt. Charm,
  kindness, and honesty are tools he picks up when they work.
- **Clear head under pressure:** in the moments that matter he does feel
  things. His body is at war with itself, but his judgement stays clear,
  and he acts on the judgement.
- **Harem as ambition:** strong women who belong to him are proof of power
  and a power base. He still needs something he is afraid to lose, even if
  he would never call it love.
- **Harmless on the surface:** easygoing, polite, a little lazy-looking at
  work. People underestimate him, which suits his plan to stay invisible.
- **Lonely:** two lives without a family. He would never admit he wants
  anyone to need him.

### Flaws
- **Arrogant about foreknowledge:** he assumes he knows how things go, and
  is caught off guard when they don't.
- **Treats people as routes:** the heroines are flags, meters, and rewards
  to him, more so while he believes the runs are fake.
- **Physically ordinary:** knows he dies easily and plays it safe until the
  System changes that.

### Voice and habits
- Narrates in first person: dry and sardonic, full of programmer and gamer
  jargon (bug, patch, RNG, speedrun). Professional and polite at work.
- Talks to the System the way a programmer talks to a rubber duck.
- Keeps the whole canon timeline in an encrypted file, written in code
  nobody else would think to read.
- Lives on instant noodles.
- **Moral line:** dark. Whatever gets him stronger and keeps him alive is
  on the table; he only holds back when it would cost him more than it gains.
